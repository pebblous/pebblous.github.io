Tangible Data is Pebblous's effort to make data something you can handle: to pick up, turn around, and press on the AI data you used to read in tables and charts. It has two strands. Tangible Data Story is data journalism in which readers explore research data themselves; Tangible Data Art is work by the Pebblous Data Art Lab (DAL) that turns data into sensory experience. Stories handle data through accuracy and sources, artworks through experience and questions. The hub keeps the two under one roof without mixing them.

Tangible Data Story

The explorers we build during research, opened inside the article itself. Photos, coordinates, and numbers on screen are real measurements; synthetic examples built to explain a principle are labeled as such. A new story is added whenever an experiment leaves data worth handling.
