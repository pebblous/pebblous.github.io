Synthetic data is made when real data is scarce or hard to use. This series follows two tracks: new research on generating and validating synthetic data, and the launches, funding rounds and acquisitions in the industry.

Research posts ask whether the work was compared against adding the same amount of real data. Industry posts keep verified results apart from company claims. Precisely targeted synthetic data was the first problem Pebblous took on.

Tracks and days

What was generated, how it was validated, and which conditions moved performance and which did not.

Industry · Sun · Wed

What happened, what is being sold, and how far the verified results go.

Weekly and monthly

On Fridays the week's posts and the major news we missed are wrapped up as Pebblous Synthetic Data Weekly. On the last day of the month the month is retold as Synthetic Data Monthly.
