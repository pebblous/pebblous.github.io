Robots and autonomous machines learn from records that cameras, joints and force sensors leave at the same moment. This series follows those datasets, the models trained on them, and experiments run on real hardware.

Dataset posts put sensors, time sync and license in one table. Model posts report which benchmark, what score, and whether there was a real-robot test. Experiment posts record success rates by condition and where failures cluster.
