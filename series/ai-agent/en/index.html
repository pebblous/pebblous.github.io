How good an AI agent looks depends on how it is measured. This series follows agent frameworks, methods for measuring performance, AI safety evaluations, and AI regulation.

Performance posts state the task, time, tools and scoring first. Safety posts say who evaluated — the company itself or an outside party. Regulation posts separate what is final, delayed, or in court. We look at agents with the eyes we use to diagnose data.
