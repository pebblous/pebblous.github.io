Pebblous Data Communication Team · 한국어

Model performance is decided by data. This series follows three things that decide data quality: the rules and standards that apply to training data, new papers that measure and fix data defects, and what newly released datasets are good for — and where they fall short.

Every post separates what we verified, what the authors claim, and how Pebblous reads it. Each one ends on the same question: what should we record and measure in our own data?

Papers · Sat · Tue

Which data defect a paper targets, how it is measured, and how much fixing it changes the model.

New datasets · Mon · Thu

Size, collection, labels, license and known defects in one table — what a dataset is good for and where it is risky.

Weekly and monthly

On Fridays the week's posts and the major news we missed are wrapped up as Pebblous Data Quality Weekly. On the last day of the month the month is retold as Data Quality Monthly.
