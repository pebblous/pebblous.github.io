## LinkedIn (EN)

Before it had a product, Pebblous had a manifesto.

In April 2021, two ETRI researchers named their company and, ahead of any product design or funding round, sat down to write a founding manifesto. It opened with a question: "How far are you from your data?" The image of turning scattered grains of sand into a pebble you can hold gave the company its name (Pebble + Fabulous) and its first solution, Pebblo.

Five years on, parts of that metaphor have become real products: DataClinic, which diagnoses data quality, and PebbloSim, which generates synthetic data. Other lines quietly fell away, like the early plan to trade pebbles in a metaverse.

The founding question still stands. How do you make data something people can actually hold? Only the answer has grown sharper.

## Facebook (KO)

만우절에 온 문자 한 통으로 시작된 회사가 있습니다.

2021년 4월 1일, 이주행 님이 이정원 님에게 함께 회사를 만들자고 제안했고, 답은 3분 만에 왔다고 합니다. 농담 같은 날짜에 온 진담이었습니다.

두 사람이 회사 이름을 정하고 가장 먼저 한 일은 제품 설계도, 투자 유치도 아니었습니다. 한 편의 선언문을 쓰는 일이었습니다.

그 글은 이 문장으로 시작합니다.

"당신과 데이터 사이의 거리는 얼마인가요?"

데이터는 차갑고 손에 잘 잡히지 않는데, 그 거리를 좁혀 보겠다는 다짐을 회사보다 먼저 적어 둔 셈입니다. 모래알 같은 데이터를 조약돌처럼 만들겠다는 비유에서 페블러스라는 이름도 나왔습니다.

5년이 지나 그 문장들 중 일부는 DataClinic과 PebbloSim이라는 제품이 됐고, 메타버스에서 조약돌을 거래하겠다던 대목처럼 조용히 사라진 것도 있습니다. 무엇이 남고 무엇이 달라졌는지, 2021년 원문을 한 글자도 고치지 않고 그대로 실었습니다.

회사가 커지기 전에 적어 둔 첫 마음은, 지금 읽어도 여전히 유효할까요.

## Facebook (EN)

There is a company that began with a text message sent on April Fools' Day.

On April 1, 2021, one researcher asked another to start a company together, and the answer came back in three minutes. A real proposal, on a day made for jokes.

Once they had chosen a name, the first thing the two did was not design a product or raise money. It was to write a manifesto.

The piece opens with a single line.

"How far are you from your data?"

Data tends to stay cold and hard to hold, and here were two people writing down, before the company itself existed, a promise to close that distance. The name Pebblous grew out of the same image: turning grains of sand into a pebble you can pick up.

Five years later, some of those sentences have become products, DataClinic and PebbloSim, while others quietly disappeared, like the plan to trade pebbles in a metaverse. To show what stayed and what changed, we reprinted the 2021 original without altering a single word.

The first intention, set down before the company grew, does it still hold when you read it today?
