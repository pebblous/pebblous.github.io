## LinkedIn (KO)

트럼프 대통령이 11월 20일 백악관 회견에서 미국 기업의 해외 데이터센터 건설을 허용하지 않겠다고 했다. 굳이 짓겠다면 "엄청난 세금"을 물리겠다고 덧붙였다.

세율은 나오지 않았다. 시행 시점도, 어떤 법을 근거로 삼겠다는 것인지도 밝히지 않았다. 참모들에게 미리 알리지 않았다는 사실은 본인이 그 자리에서 말했다.

미국 헌법 제1조 8절은 조세를 부과하고 징수할 권한을 의회에 준다. 대통령이 새 세금을 혼자 만들어 물릴 수는 없고, 의회가 법으로 넘긴 권한이 있을 때만 예외가 생긴다.

바로 전날에는 같은 종류의 권한이 실제로 쓰이는 모습이 나왔다. 상무부 산업안보국이 11월 19일 첨단 반도체 원격 접근 규정을 공포하고 그날로 시행했다. 이쪽에는 규정 이름이 있고, 시행일이 있고, 기준선이 되는 숫자가 있다. 수출 통제 권한은 의회가 법으로 행정부에 넘겨 둔 것이어서 근거를 적을 칸이 애초에 마련돼 있었다.

발언 당일 알파벳은 2.7%, 마이크로소프트는 2.8%, 아마존은 4.1% 내렸다.

주가 쪽은 그대로 읽으면 안 된다. 그 주 증시 전체가 내리막이었고 S&P 500은 11월 4일 이후 9%가량을 잃은 뒤였다. 회견 직후 한두 시간의 움직임은 0.3%를 밑돌았다고 전한 매체도 있다.

그런데도 세율 없는 한 문장이 수천억 달러 규모 투자 계획을 깎는 요인으로 계산됐다.

데이터센터 입지를 정해 온 값은 지금까지 둘이었다. 사용자와의 거리에서 나오는 지연 시간, 그리고 그 나라 조문이 요구하는 조건. 세율이 정해지지 않은 세금은 둘 중 어느 쪽으로도 적히지 않는다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

A tax was threatened on November 20 with no rate, no date and no statute behind it. Speaking to reporters at the White House, the president said American firms would not be permitted to put data centers in other countries, and that any that went ahead would be taxed "tremendously."

He named no figure. He set no timetable. Under Article I, Section 8 the power to lay and collect taxes belongs to Congress, and a president reaches it only where a statute has handed him a piece of it. He did not say whether he would ask Congress to legislate or reach for an authority the executive already holds.

He also volunteered that the idea had not been put to his own staff before the briefing.

Hold that against the previous day. On November 19 the export control arm of the Commerce Department issued a rule on remote access to advanced chips, effective immediately, with a named compute threshold and a compliance deadline in February 2027. Delegated authority leaves a place to write the reasoning down, and the rule used it.

Markets did not wait for that distinction. Alphabet fell 2.7 percent, Microsoft 2.8 and Amazon 4.1 on the day.

Those figures need handling with care. The week was broadly down, with the S&P 500 off roughly 9 percent since November 4, and one report put the move in the first hours after the briefing below 0.3 percent.

What remains is a sentence being priced into capital plans worth hundreds of billions.

Two things have decided data center siting until now: how far the user sits from the building, and what the local statute demands. Both can be costed in advance. A rate that does not exist cannot.

## Facebook (EN)

Think of the moment someone sits down to decide whether to open one more region.

Two values go into that decision. How long a request takes to travel between the user and the facility, and what the traffic costs when it crosses a continent.

Both are written in milliseconds and dollars. Whoever runs the arithmetic arrives at the same answer.

A law saying certain data has to sit physically inside a country does not stop the arithmetic either. You read the text and you cost the compliance.

The third force, the one that surfaced at the White House on November 20, has no such value attached.

Companies building data centers abroad will face "a tremendous tax," the president said, and no rate, no start date and no statute came with it. He added that he had not told his own staff yet.

A force with an empty value.

Unlike the other two, this one has nowhere to sit in a design. Faced with a tax that has no rate, the realistic move is to defer the decision, and while the decision waits, the latency and the transfer bills keep accruing.

"If the data we run on had to move to another country, is there a document anywhere that says what that would cost?"
