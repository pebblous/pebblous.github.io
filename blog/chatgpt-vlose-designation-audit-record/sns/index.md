## LinkedIn (KO)

유럽 집행위원회가 8월 31일 챗GPT를 초대형 온라인 검색엔진으로 지정했다. AI 챗봇이 이 범주에 들어간 것은 처음이다.

분류를 가른 것은 기능이었다. 챗GPT는 사용자의 질문에 대화로 답하면서 동시에 웹을 검색한다. 집행위는 그래서 디지털서비스법상 검색엔진에 해당한다고 봤다. 이 등급을 받은 서비스는 그동안 구글 검색과 빙 둘뿐이었다. 챗GPT는 세 번째로 이름을 올리면서 이용자 수로는 곧바로 빙을 앞질렀다.

등급이 붙으면 의무가 따라온다. 서비스와 알고리즘 시스템에서 나오는 체계적 위험을 매년 스스로 평가하고, 자기 비용으로 독립 감사를 받고, 심사를 통과한 연구자에게 데이터를 열어야 한다. 기한은 2027년 1월이고, 어기면 전 세계 연매출의 6%까지 과징금을 물 수 있다.

정해지지 않은 것은 그 평가와 감사가 무엇을 근거로 성립하느냐다. 같은 규정이 광고에 대해서는 저장소에 남길 항목 일곱 가지를 조문에 열거해 두었다. 어떤 질의에 어떤 출처를 검색했고 그중 무엇을 인용했는지에 대해서는 그런 조문이 없다. 근거 문서를 3년간 보존하라는 의무는 있지만, 그 문서가 무엇이어야 하는지는 사업자가 정한다.

보존은 이미 있는 기록에만 적용된다. 답이 만들어지는 순간에 남기지 않은 것은 나중에 만들어 낼 수 없다. 규제가 요구하기 전에 손봐야 하는 쪽은 문서 양식이 아니라 로그 스키마다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

The European Commission designated ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine on August 31. No AI chatbot had been placed in that category before.

The reasoning rested on what the service does rather than on any new rule written for AI. ChatGPT engages with a user's prompts while also searching the web, which under the Digital Services Act makes it a search engine. Only two services had ever carried that label, Google Search and Bing. ChatGPT arrives third and passes Bing on the way in.

The label brings duties. OpenAI has to assess the systemic risks stemming from its service and its algorithmic systems every year, submit to an independent audit at its own expense, and open data to vetted researchers. The deadline is January 2027, and non-compliance can cost up to 6% of global annual turnover.

What is not settled is the evidence those assessments will rest on. The same regulation writes seven items straight into the law for the advertising repository. For which sources a generative answer searched and which of them it cited, there is no equivalent article. Supporting documents have to be kept for three years, but what those documents are is left to the provider.

Retention only reaches records that already exist. Whatever a system fails to capture as an answer is composed cannot be manufactured later. Before the regulation asks for anything, the thing to fix is the log schema. The document template comes after that.

## Facebook (EN)

"Where did this sentence come from?"

You have probably clicked a citation under a ChatGPT answer and failed to find the sentence anywhere on the page it pointed to. There is no way to tell which end went wrong.

On August 31 the European Commission designated ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine. It is the first AI chatbot to be placed in that category. The reason was that it answers a user in conversation while searching the web at the same time.

The label brings duties. An annual assessment of systemic risks, an independent audit paid for by the provider, and data opened to researchers who pass a vetting process. The deadline is January 2027.

What held me longest while writing the piece was somewhere else. The same regulation spells out, for advertising, seven things that have to sit in the repository. For generative answers there is no such article. I have started thinking of that difference as "the format of the record." Advertising has one. Answers do not have one yet.

"A year from now an auditor points at a single answer and asks why those sources, in that order. What would you reach for?"

A duty to preserve only reaches what is already there.

Whatever goes unrecorded at the moment an answer is composed cannot be assembled afterwards.

So this sits ahead of a compliance department managing a deadline. Where the recording points go along the path an answer travels is the earlier question. The order has been the same in every data quality engagement at Pebblous. A system that never decided what to keep cannot answer anything later.

Europe has given providers until January 2027 to work that format out. What shape it should take is something nobody has written down yet.
