Pebblous has opened an AI Test & Evaluation Center and entered the business of testing the quality of AI training data and issuing test reports on the result. In September 2026, the Korea Laboratory Accreditation Scheme (KOLAS) accredited Pebblous as a testing laboratory for data quality under ISO/IEC 5259-2:2024 and ISO/IEC 25024:2015. The accreditation number is KT1362, and it is valid through September 2030.

The center issues two kinds of report. The KOLAS accredited test report records the result of testing data quality against the two international standards above. The general test report records the result of testing AI models and software against the items and methods the applying organization defines; it is a non-accredited report and sits outside the KOLAS accreditation scope. Consultations and applications are handled at the Pebblous AI Test & Evaluation Center.

1

Where Data Quality Needs Third-Party Evidence

The performance of an AI model depends heavily on its training data. Yet a claim of good quality from the party that built the data rarely convinces a client or a review body. In settings such as acceptance inspection for public data construction projects, final evaluation of government research and development projects, and procurement proposals, evidence issued by a third party carries weight.

At the announcement of the accreditation, Joo-Haeng Lee, CEO of Pebblous, said the accreditation lets the company measure data quality against international standards and show the result in a report the state recognizes. The AI Test & Evaluation Center is the organization that carries out that testing work.

2

Accreditation, Testing and Certification Are Different Words

Before choosing a report, three words need to be told apart.

Term Who does it What it means
Accreditation KOLAS Assesses whether a testing laboratory meets the ISO/IEC 17025 requirements and is competent to perform tests within a defined scope, and grants formal recognition
Testing Pebblous AI Test & Evaluation Center Measures a subject against agreed standards and procedures and issues the result as a test report
Certification A separate certification body Judges whether a product, a service or a system meets a set of criteria and grants a certificate

KOLAS accreditation is a scheme that formally recognizes the competence of a testing laboratory. It does not mean KOLAS guarantees the quality of a particular product or dataset. The document Pebblous issues is likewise not a certificate but a test report, and it records the result of measuring the subject it was asked to test.

Pass and fail are not settled on their own either. ISO/IEC 5259-2 defines how to measure quality; it does not set a passing line. To state conformity in a report, the decision criteria have to be agreed at the application stage.

3

Which Standards the Testing Uses

Accredited testing rests on two international standards.

Standard What it covers What it measures
ISO/IEC 5259-2:2024 Data used for analytics and machine learning Basic characteristics such as accuracy, completeness and consistency, and characteristics that matter for AI training such as balance, diversity, representativeness and similarity
ISO/IEC 25024:2015 Structured data Measures defined by the data quality model

The 24 quality characteristics and measures defined in 5259-2 are laid out in a summary table on the Pebblous blog. The accreditation scope covers the quality characteristic groups of 5259-2 and the measure groups of 25024. Whether accredited testing applies to a given request is confirmed by reviewing the test method and the formula for each measure.

The Two Reports Serve Different Purposes

Dimension KOLAS accredited test report General test report
Nature Accredited testing within the KOLAS accreditation scope Non-accredited testing, outside the KOLAS accreditation scope
Test criteria ISO/IEC 5259-2, ISO/IEC 25024 Criteria and methods the applying organization defines
Test subject Structured and unstructured datasets for AI training AI models and software, data processing systems
Main measures Data quality such as completeness, accuracy and consistency Function and performance such as accuracy, processing speed and reproducibility
Test location Pebblous Sejong test site The applying organization's site or remote, as agreed
KOLAS accreditation shown on report Yes No
Typical use Delivery for public data construction projects, official external submission Evidence for quantitative project targets, internal verification

What decides the choice is what the receiving party asks for. If the receiving party requires a report from an accredited testing laboratory, the KOLAS accredited test report is the one needed. Accredited testing, however, applies only to items within the accreditation scope. Items outside it, such as model performance or response time, are handled as general testing and issued as a separate report. If a project agreement requires an accredited report for every metric, check with the managing agency first whether a general test report may be submitted.

5

Four Cases That Call for a Test Report

  • Data construction projects: Evidence the quality of the delivered dataset with a KOLAS accredited test report.
  • Government research and development projects: Submit test results showing whether the performance targets were met at final evaluation.
  • AI voucher programs: Evidence whether the quantitative targets agreed with the client company were met.
  • Procurement and delivery: Show the performance of the product proposed or delivered with objective material.

Five Steps From Application to Issuance

  1. Request a consultation: Enter contact details and information about the data on the consultation form.
  2. Consultation and quotation: Confirm the test items and scope, and receive a quotation.
  3. Test agreement: Fix the criteria, the methods and the schedule in a test plan and a test agreement.
  4. Test execution: Run the test in the agreed environment and review the results.
  5. Report issuance: Issue the test report once the review is complete.

An application form on its own is enough to open a case. A test item specification, a quantitative target document, a product manual or a test environment diagram will speed the consultation up. Cost and duration vary with the number of test items, the size of the data and the test location, so they are advised after the application has been reviewed.

The AI Test & Evaluation Center is in Daejeon, and KOLAS accredited testing is performed at the Sejong test site. Testing at the applying organization's own site applies only to general test reports.

7

Testing Runs Separately From the Solution

Pebblous also supplies Data Clinic, a solution that diagnoses and improves data quality. Because one company handles both the solution and the testing, impartiality matters. Test report issuance runs as independent testing, separated from that solution.

ISO/IEC 17025 requires impartiality and validity of results from a testing laboratory. Test results have to rest on objective evidence, and commissioning a test does not guarantee that a target value will be met.

8

What International Acceptance Means, and Where It Stops

KOLAS is an accreditation body that has signed the Mutual Recognition Arrangement (MRA) of the International Laboratory Accreditation Cooperation (ILAC). The signatories to the ILAC MRA have agreed to accept results from the laboratories each of them has accredited. Accredited test reports from Pebblous can therefore be accepted among the countries party to the arrangement.

This effect does not stand in for every regulatory procedure. Which reports a given country's regulation or procurement procedure accepts is decided by the rules of that procedure. In European Union regulation, for instance, accreditation of a testing laboratory and designation as a notified body are separate schemes, and that difference was covered separately in Korean Data-Quality Test Reports Count Only as Evidence in Europe. An accredited test report can serve as supporting material in technical documentation, but it does not stand in for conformity assessment.

Five Things to Check Before Applying

Which report should I apply for?

If the receiving party requires an accredited report, or if data quality has to be evidenced officially, apply for the KOLAS accredited test report. If the goal is to check the performance of a model or software against criteria you set yourself, the general test report is the right one. If the call is hard to make, describe the situation on the application form and it will be covered in the consultation.

Can a general test report be used as an accredited report?

It cannot. The general test report is a non-accredited report outside the KOLAS accreditation scope, and it carries no KOLAS accreditation shown on report.

If I receive a Pebblous report, have I been certified for data quality?

No. A test report is a document recording measurement results, and Pebblous is an accredited testing laboratory, not a certification body.

Is on-site testing possible?

For the general test report, yes. If the system under test is hard to move, the test is run at the applying organization's site. KOLAS accredited testing is performed at the Sejong test site, so it is not run on site.

What about cost and duration?

They vary with the number of test items, the size of the data and the test location. If there is a submission deadline, note it in the inquiry field of the application form and it will be reflected in the schedule.
