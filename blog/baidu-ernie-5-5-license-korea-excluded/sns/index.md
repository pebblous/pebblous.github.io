## LinkedIn (KO)

바이두가 11월 5일 허깅페이스에 푼 2,800억 매개변수 모델은 한국에서 내려받는 일부터 라이선스와 어긋난다.

ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base의 가중치 옆에는 LICENSE라는 파일이 하나 같이 올라왔다. Apache 2.0 같은 기성 라이선스가 아니라 바이두가 이 모델을 위해 따로 쓴 문서이고, 1.11조가 허용 지역을 "유럽연합·영국·대한민국을 뺀 전 세계"로 정의한다. 2조는 라이선스 허여 자체를 그 안쪽으로 한정하고, 5.3조는 바깥에서 모델과 파생 모델, 그 결과물을 사용·복제·수정·배포·전시하지 못하게 한다. 내려받는 일이 곧 복제다.

관문은 하나 더 있다. 4조가 돈을 기준으로 다시 나눈다. 관련 제품의 연매출이 5,000만 달러를 넘는 기업은 쓰기 전에 바이두의 서면 허가를 받아야 하고, 그 아래라도 상업적으로 쓰면 한 번 통지하고 "Powered by ERNIE"를 달아야 한다. 5.5조는 이 모델의 결과물로 다른 인공지능 모델을 개선하는 일을 막는다.

제한은 한 세대에서 끝나지도 않는다. 5.2조가 파생 모델을 넘길 때 같은 조건을 다음 계약서에 그대로 담으라고 요구하니, 모델 카드에 ERNIE 5.5가 적혀 있지 않은 모델이 세 지역 제외를 물려받고 있을 수 있다. 바이두는 왜 세 곳을 뺐는지 적지 않았다. 석 달 전 같은 세 곳에 미국까지 더해 네 곳을 뺀 미니맥스는 규제 불확실성과 저작권 분쟁을 이유로 들었다.

페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 등급으로 매겨 진단한다. 모자란 자리를 합성 데이터로 메우기로 했다면, 그 합성 데이터를 만들 모델은 벤치마크 표에서 고르게 되지만, 써도 되는지는 5.5조 같은 문장이 가른다.

지난해까지 모델 도입 검토는 Apache 2.0 배지를 확인하는 것으로 끝났다. 이제는 조항 번호가 붙은 문서를 여는 일이 그 앞에 하나 더 붙는다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Baidu's return to open weights after sixteen months comes with a license that South Korea is not inside.

The weights for ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base, a 280-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model that switches on 14 billion per token, went up on Hugging Face on November 5. Beside them sits a file titled ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base Model License Agreement v1.0, written for this model rather than borrowed from Apache 2.0. Section 1.11 defines the Territory as all geographical areas worldwide other than the European Union, the United Kingdom and South Korea. Section 2 grants the license only within it, and Section 5.3 bars use, reproduction, modification, distribution and display of the model, its derivatives and their outputs anywhere else. Downloading is reproduction.

A second gate stands behind the first, and Section 4 draws it with money. A commercial user whose related products earn more than $50 million a year needs Baidu's written authorization before starting; below that figure, one notice and a "Powered by ERNIE" credit are enough. Section 5.5 forbids using the outputs to improve any other artificial intelligence model, which rules out generating synthetic data to teach a different system.

The terms travel downstream as well. Section 5.2 requires the same restrictions to be written into any agreement covering a derivative, so a model several steps removed can carry the three exclusions without naming ERNIE 5.5 anywhere on its card. Baidu published no reason for leaving the three places out. MiniMax, which in August withheld free use of its H3 video model's weights from the same three plus the United States, cited regulatory uncertainty and copyright litigation.

Pebblous pegs the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. A plan that calls for synthetic data to fill a gap picks the generating model off a benchmark table, and whether it may be used there is decided by a sentence like Section 5.5.

Through last year, model intake ended at the sight of an Apache 2.0 badge. It now starts with a document that has clause numbers.

## Facebook (EN)

A model usually enters a company quietly.

Someone downloads the weights from a repository and puts them on internal storage. No purchase order, no sign-off.

Beside the ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base weights Baidu posted on Hugging Face on the fifth there was one more file, named LICENSE. Section 1.11 of it fixes the places where the model may be used as the whole world other than the European Union, the United Kingdom and South Korea.

Korea was outside that line before anyone downloaded anything.

The clause I read twice, though, was not the territorial one. It was Section 4, the one that divides by money. Up to $50 million in annual revenue from the related products, a single notice carries the use. Past that line, written authorization has to be in hand before the line is crossed, and use without it has to stop.

The condition is attached not to the company we are this year but to the company we might be next year.

Pebblous details the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. Those numbers describe a dataset as it stands today. The form filled in when a model comes through the door tends to describe today as well. Which threshold changes the terms, and who is watching for it, is not a field I have seen on one yet.

"Of the open models running in your company, how many change terms if the business grows?"

A scoreboard helps choose a model. Whether the model may be used is settled in the file lying next to it, under a clause number.
