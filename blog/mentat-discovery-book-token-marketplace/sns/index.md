도서 라이선싱 기업 멘탯이 10월 13일 연 장터에서 책은 '권'이 아니라 '토큰'으로 검색됩니다.

멘탯 디스커버리에는 국내 출판사 90여 곳과 계약한 책 약 3만4,000종이 올라 있고, 전체 분량은 약 80억 토큰입니다. 분야와 카테고리로 묶음을 고르면 그 묶음의 토큰 수와 예상 비용이 한 화면에 뜹니다. 화면에서 바로 도장을 찍는 구조는 아니고, 규모와 비용을 확인한 쪽이 상세 카탈로그를 요청하면 거기서부터 계약 협의로 이어집니다.

그전까지 AI 기업은 쓸 만한 책을 가진 출판사를 수소문하고, 권리 관계를 확인하고, 분량과 값을 협의하는 일을 출판사마다 되풀이했습니다. 어느 출판사에 어떤 분야의 책이 몇 종 있는지 모아 둔 목록 자체가 없었기 때문입니다. 출판업계는 권으로 세고 AI 업계는 토큰으로 세는데, 멘탯은 그 환산을 플랫폼이 먼저 해 놓고 문을 열었습니다.

다만 토큰 수가 재는 것은 분량뿐입니다. 30년 묵은 전문 지식이 들어간 책과 하루 만에 쓴 같은 두께의 책은 토큰 수에서 구별되지 않습니다. 멘탯도 값을 토큰 수에 그대로 비례시키지 않고, 학습용 데이터셋은 기간과 쓸 수 있는 모델군을 한정한 1회성 정액으로, 참조·인용 API는 쓰일 때마다 쌓이는 종량제로 갈랐습니다.

책값을 매긴 다른 신호들은 또 다른 단위를 씁니다. 앤트로픽이 저자 집단소송을 합의하며 내놓기로 한 금액은 약 15억 달러로 보도됐고, 저작물 약 50만 건에 나뉘어 권당 약 3,000달러로 환산된다고 알려졌습니다. 법원을 거친 첫 대규모 가격 신호는 토큰이 아니라 작품 단위로 매겨진 셈입니다.

마이크로소프트가 2026년 초 연 퍼블리셔 콘텐츠 마켓플레이스는 사용량에 따른 토큰 단위 로열티를 도입했다고 전해집니다. 멘탯의 참조·인용 모델과 결이 같습니다. 다만 도서 출판사의 참여는 더딘 편으로 전해지고, 하퍼콜린스는 종당 약 5,000달러 수준으로 개별 협상한 것으로 알려졌습니다.

페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 정량 진단합니다. 품질 지표만으로는 거래가 성립하지 않습니다. 누구의 권리이고 어디까지 쓸 수 있는지가 데이터 옆에 함께 적혀 있어야 AI 기업이 계약으로 넘어갑니다.

토큰은 거래를 시작하게 해 주는 공통 단위이고, 최종 금액은 토큰 밖에서 정해집니다. 쓰임새와 대체 불가능성과 독점 조건이 그 값을 가릅니다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

On the marketplace Mentat opened on October 13, books are searched by the token rather than by the copy.

Mentat Discovery lists about 34,000 books licensed from more than 90 Korean publishers, coming to roughly 8 billion tokens in all. Pick a bundle by field or category and its token count and estimated cost come up on the same screen. Nothing is signed there: a buyer who has checked size and cost requests the detailed catalog, and the contract discussion starts from that point.

Before this, an AI company asked around for publishers holding usable books, checked who owned which rights, and negotiated volume and price publisher by publisher. No list existed anywhere showing which publisher held how many books in which field. Korean publishing counts in copies and the AI industry counts in tokens, and Mentat did the conversion at the platform level before opening the door.

A token count, though, measures length. Three hundred pages holding thirty years of specialist knowledge and three hundred pages written in a day are not told apart by it. Mentat did not let price track token count in a straight line either: a training dataset goes out as a one-time flat fee bounded by the contract term and the model families it covers, while the citation API is metered and accumulates with every use.

The other price signals on books use other units again. The amount Anthropic agreed to pay to settle a class action by authors was reported at about $1.5 billion, spread across some 500,000 works, which has been put at roughly $3,000 a work. The first large price signal to pass through a court was set per work, not per token.

The Publisher Content Marketplace that Microsoft opened in early 2026 is described as carrying usage-based royalties counted in tokens, which is the shape of Mentat's citation model. Book publishers have been slower to join, by those same accounts, and HarperCollins is understood to have negotiated separately at around $5,000 a title.

Pebblous quantifies the quality of AI training data through DataClinic. Quality metrics alone do not make a transaction. Whose rights these are and how far they can be used have to be written beside the data before an AI company moves to a contract.

A token is the common unit that lets a deal begin, and the final amount is settled outside the token. Mode of use, difficulty of replacement and exclusivity terms are what pull it apart.

## Facebook (EN)

An editor can tell you, without looking anything up, how many pages a book they made runs to, what size it was trimmed at, how many copies went into the first printing.

Token count is not one of them.

Publishing counts its goods in copies, printings and print runs. The number written into a contract is a royalty rate, and a book's standing is how many printings it went through.

The AI industry counts the same prose in tokens. How much a model can read at once, how much data went into training it, what the bill comes to for using it: all tokens.

One side says a book printed 3,000 copies, and the other asks how many tokens that comes to.

So the first job at the negotiating table was conversion. Counting the characters in a book, working out how many tokens that makes, each side guessing from its own instincts what the volume should be worth. The whole sequence ran again every time the publisher changed.

Mentat Discovery, which opened on October 13, did that conversion before it opened the door.

About 34,000 books licensed from more than 90 Korean publishers are listed, and the same list is also written as about 8 billion tokens. Pick a bundle by field and the token count and the estimated cost come up together.

Which also means one of the two units has become the common language. It is the token.

And a token count measures length. Nothing past that.

Three hundred pages holding thirty years of specialist knowledge and three hundred pages written in a day are not told apart by it. Whether the content can be found anywhere else does not show up in it either.

"Of two bundles the same size in tokens, which one should cost more?"

Mentat did not let price track token count in a straight line either. A dataset taken for training is a one-time flat fee bounded by the contract term and the model families it covers. The citation API, which consults a book and shows the source each time an answer is built, is metered and accumulates with use.

Pebblous quantifies the quality of AI training data through DataClinic. What Mentat did was not to make the books better; it turned them into a shape a price can attach to. Rights cleared and volume stated in a unit both sides know are where a negotiation starts from.

The training file carrying title, author, publisher and ISBN alongside the body text is what that looks like in practice. Whether an ISBN field is present does not change the quality of the data. It changes whether the data can be traded.

How to treat a book that sold little and is hard to replace is a question nobody has answered. It arrives after a common unit has lowered the threshold of negotiation.
