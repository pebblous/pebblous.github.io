## LinkedIn (KO)

과학기술정보통신부가 10월 13일 코엑스에서 공개한 2027년 피지컬 AI 신규 사업 예산은 1,210억 원이고, 그 가운데 400억 원이 모델이 아니라 로봇이 배울 행동 데이터를 모으는 설비로 갔습니다.

나머지 둘은 정보통신기획평가원이 맡는 핵심기술 개발 550억 원과 정보통신산업진흥원이 맡는 우편 물류 실증 260억 원입니다. 400억 원짜리 사업의 이름은 '피지컬 AI 트레이닝 센터' 구축입니다. 로봇과 센서와 원격조작 설비를 갖춘 학습 환경을 2027년에 한 곳 지어, 여러 기업이 거기서 실제 작업에 필요한 행동 데이터를 만들고 검증하게 합니다.

왜 설비를 공용으로 짓는가. 그만한 장비를 갖춰 놓고 오래 돌릴 수 있는 회사가 많지 않기 때문입니다. 과기정통부는 올해 7월 브리핑에서 로봇 학습에 필요한 데이터 양을 두고 "사람이 하루 8시간씩 데이터를 만든다고 해도 충분한 데이터를 확보하는 데 5년이 걸린다"고 설명한 바 있습니다.

공개된 자료에 없는 것은 그 설비가 데이터를 어떤 형식으로 적을지, 그 형식을 누가 정할지입니다. 같은 날 같은 장소에서 열린 얼라이언스 성과 점검 간담회에서 참석자들이 올린 현장 의견 다섯 가지 가운데 하나가 '데이터 표준화와 공동 활용'이었습니다. 요구는 그날 그 자리에 있었고, 같은 날 공개된 예산표에는 그 항목이 없었습니다. 센터는 세 사업 가운데 공고 시점조차 나오지 않은 사업입니다.

2027년에 돌아가는 공용 설비는 한 곳이고, 먼저 지어진 곳이 적어 두는 형식은 나중에 네 곳이 더 생겼을 때 참조점이 됩니다. 페블러스는 모은 데이터가 학습에 쓸 수 있는 상태인지 정량으로 진단하는 일을 해 왔습니다. 그 자리에서 보면 400억 원은 반가운 숫자이고, 형식 칸은 아직 아무도 값을 넣지 않은 칸입니다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Korea's science ministry set out its 2027 physical AI budget at COEX in Seoul on 13 October. Three newly funded programs come to ₩121 billion, about $78 million, and ₩40 billion of that buys not a model but a place to collect the behavior data robots learn from.

The other two shares are ₩55 billion for core technology development, run by IITP, and ₩26 billion for a postal logistics pilot, run by NIPA. The ₩40 billion program is named Physical AI Training Center construction. One site goes up in 2027, fitted with robots, sensors and remote-operation equipment, and companies share it to produce and verify the behavior data their own tasks require.

Why pool the equipment at all. Few companies can set up rigs like that and keep them running long enough to matter. At a briefing in July the ministry put the scale of the problem in units of time, saying that "even if a person made data eight hours a day, securing enough data would take five years."

What the material does not say is in what format the facility will record its data, or who decides that format. At an alliance review meeting held the same day in the same venue, one of the five points participants raised from the floor was "data standardization and shared use." The industry asked that day, in that room. The budget table released the same day carries no line for it. Of the three programs, the center is the only one with no call date.

One shared facility runs in 2027, and whatever format it writes in becomes the reference point when four more arrive. Pebblous works on judging whether collected data is in a state fit for learning. From that seat the ₩40 billion is a welcome number, and the format cell is one nobody has put a value in yet.

## Facebook (EN)

Two things were disclosed at the same venue on the same day.

On one side, the 2027 physical AI budget. Korea's science ministry had newly funded three programs, ₩121 billion between them, and ₩40 billion of that went to building a Physical AI Training Center.

Not money for making a model, and not money for installing one in a workplace. It pays for a learning environment fitted with robots, sensors and remote-operation equipment, built at a single site, where companies produce the behavior data their own tasks require.

On the other, the alliance review meeting. Of the five points participants raised from the floor, one was "data standardization and shared use."

The ask was in the room that day. The budget table released the same day has no line for it.

The ₩121 billion splits into core technology development, center construction and the field pilot, and in none of the three is there an owner or a date for deciding the data format.

One condition rides along with the phrase shared use. For several companies to use each other's data from the same facility, it has to be written the same way.

The coordinate frame a robot arm's joint angles are recorded in. How camera video and force-sensor readings are aligned in time. Whether a failed attempt is kept or thrown away.

When those differ between institutions, the data piles up and does not combine.

"Between the equipment drawings and the document that says how the data will be written, which one has to come out first?"

Pebblous quantifies the quality of AI training data at DataClinic. When what arrives for diagnosis was gathered in more than one place, there is a step that comes before measuring anything. Checking whether the same field means the same thing in each of them.

One shared facility runs in 2027. The June strategy drew five regional centers, and the budget holds one of them.

Whatever that first site writes down becomes the reference point when four more arrive.

Nobody has put a value in that cell yet.
