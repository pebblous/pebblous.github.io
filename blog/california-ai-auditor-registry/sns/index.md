## LinkedIn (KO)

AI를 감사할 자격을 주정부가 심사하는 제도가 미국에서 처음 생겼다. 그런데 무엇을 자로 삼을지는 감사인이 신청서에 직접 적어 낸다.

개빈 뉴섬 캘리포니아 주지사가 9월 9일 SB 813과 AB 1405에 서명했다. SB 813은 주정부 기관이 "독립 검증 기관(IVO)"을 지정하는 절차를 만든다. AB 1405는 주법 준수를 확인하는 감사를 수행하는 개인과 법인을 주 등록부에 올리고, 2029년 1월 1일부터는 등록 없이 그 감사를 할 수 없게 한다. 주지사실은 이런 제도가 전국에서 처음이라고 밝혔다. 7월 일리노이 SB 315가 감사를 받는 쪽을 규제했다면, 캘리포니아는 감사하는 쪽을 규정했다.

비어 있는 자리가 이 제도의 특징이다. SB 813은 지정 신청자에게 자기가 쓸 벤치마크와 지표, 방법론을 직접 제안하게 하고, 주정부가 지정 기준을 만들 기한은 2028년 1월 1일이다. 감사를 받을지 말지도 기업이 정한다. 감사를 받은 사실은 소송에서 "관련은 있으나 결정적이지는 않다"로만 쓰인다. 면책이 아니라 증거 능력이다.

재정적 독립 조항이 어디까지 미치는지는 8월 26일 METR·레드우드 리서치 보고서가 앞서 보여 줬다. METR는 오픈AI로부터 보수를 받지 않았지만, 조사에 쓴 분석 크레딧은 오픈AI가 무상으로 댄 것이었고 분석에 쓴 모델도 그 사건에 연루된 오픈AI 자체 모델이었다. 독립이 흔들린 통로는 대가가 아니라 조사 수단이었다.

법이 정한 것은 사람이고, 남은 변수는 자료다. 학습 데이터의 출처와 라벨 이력, 평가 기록이 남아 있지 않으면 등록번호를 단 감사인 앞에서도 열어 볼 것이 없다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

The first US regime in which a state decides who is qualified to audit AI has been signed. The auditors themselves propose the yardstick those audits will use.

California Governor Gavin Newsom signed SB 813 and AB 1405 on September 9. SB 813 creates a process for a state agency to designate "independent verification organizations." AB 1405 puts the people and firms that perform state-compliance audits on a state register and bars anyone unregistered from performing those audits from January 1, 2029. The governor's office called the framework first in the nation. Illinois SB 315, signed in July, regulated the party being audited; California regulates the party doing the auditing.

The blank spaces define this regime. SB 813 has applicants propose the benchmarks, techniques, metrics and methodologies they intend to use, and the state has until January 1, 2028 to write the designation criteria. Whether to be audited at all remains the company's choice. An audit performed under those standards is "relevant but not dispositive" in litigation. That is evidentiary weight, not a safe harbor.

How far the financial independence clauses reach was already on display in the METR and Redwood Research report of August 26. METR took no payment from OpenAI, but the analysis credits it spent were provided free by OpenAI, and the model it ran the analysis on was OpenAI's own, the one involved in the incident. Independence bent through the instrument, not the fee.

The laws settled who the auditor is. The record is the part still in play. Without provenance for training data, a history of who labeled what and when, and logs of which evaluations ran under which conditions, a registered auditor has nothing to open.

## Facebook (EN)

A state agency will issue each AI auditor a unique registration number, and the auditor has to display that number prominently in its advertising.

That sentence is in AB 1405, which California signed on September 9. It was lifted straight from accountancy licensing.

SB 813, signed the same day, creates the process by which a state agency designates an "independent verification organization." Neither law requires anyone to audit AI. They decide who is qualified to. The governor's office called the framework first in the nation.

The shape of the regime held me less than the working day of the person carrying that number.

"An auditor walks into a company with a registration number. What do they actually get to open?"

The statutes do not answer. SB 813 has each applicant write down the benchmarks, techniques, metrics and methodologies it proposes to use. The party doing the measuring brings its own ruler. The state has until January 1, 2028 to write the designation criteria, and the register opens January 1, 2029. From now until then runs a little over two years of what I would call "the blank yardstick."

That blank space already let something through in late August. METR took no payment for investigating OpenAI's Hugging Face breach. But the API credits it spent on the analysis were provided free by OpenAI, and the model it analyzed with was OpenAI's own, the one involved in the incident. Independence bent through the instrument, not the money.

So one thing holds even while the yardstick is blank. Whoever shows up, what they open is the record. Where training data came from, whose hands the labels passed through and when, which evaluations ran under which conditions. That is not far from where Pebblous has been working, quantitatively diagnosing the quality of AI training data through DataClinic.

The criteria take shape only in 2028. The record accumulating now is the one thing nobody can assemble retroactively then.

"Would our data provenance, labeling history and evaluation logs still explain themselves if a third party opened them as they are?"
