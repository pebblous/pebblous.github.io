## LinkedIn (KO)

한국 기업의 AI 신뢰 격차가 1년 만에 28.9점에서 1.5점으로 줄었다. 그런데 좁혀진 거리의 절반이 넘는 몫은 기대가 내려온 쪽에서 나왔다.

SAS가 9월 30일 공개한 '데이터 및 AI 영향력 보고서: 새로운 신뢰의 경제학'에 적힌 수치다. 시장조사기관 IDC의 리서치 인사이트를 바탕으로 28개국 2,699명이 답한 2년 차 조사다. 여기서 신뢰 격차는 뺄셈이다. 기업이 자기 회사의 AI를 얼마나 믿는가에서, 그 AI를 실제로 얼마나 관리하고 있는가를 뺀다.

한국 기업의 두 점수는 모두 가운데로 움직여 만났다. 관리 역량은 45점에서 57.1점으로 올랐고, 체감 신뢰는 73.9점에서 58.6점으로 내렸다. 격차는 두 점수의 거리일 뿐 높이가 아니다. SAS가 '신뢰할 수 있는 AI 리더'로 치는 기준선에 한국 기업은 올해도 들어오지 못했다.

내린 쪽을 나쁜 소식으로만 읽을 이유는 없다. 믿음이 73.9점이던 해에 관리 역량은 45점이었으니 그 숫자는 근거보다 앞서 나간 것이었고, 1년 돌려 본 뒤 다시 매긴 점수 쪽이 정직하다.

같은 조사의 전 세계 수치도 방향이 같다. 생성형 AI에 대한 신뢰는 76%인데, 스스로 판단하고 행동하는 에이전틱 AI에 대한 신뢰는 66%로 내려간다. 맡기는 몫이 커질수록 믿음은 줄어든다.

문제는 올라온 12.1점 안쪽이 고르지 않다는 데 있다. 국내 보도가 점수를 밝힌 네 항목 가운데 모델 거버넌스 및 감독이 9.3점 올라 가장 크게 개선됐고, 데이터 품질 및 거버넌스는 53.9점으로 가장 낮은 자리에 소폭 상승으로 머물렀다. 책임 있는 AI 정책은 1년 내내 움직이지 않았다.

에이전틱 AI를 이미 도입한 한국 기업은 42.6%, 모든 AI 프로젝트에 데이터 품질 관리를 의무로 거는 곳은 1.9%다. 두 수치는 재는 대상이 달라 배수로 말할 수는 없지만, 나란히 놓으면 순서는 보인다.

기대를 낮춰 좁힌 격차와 재료를 고쳐 좁힌 격차는 다음 해에 남는 것이 다르다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Korea's AI trust gap has all but closed, from 28.9 points to 1.5 in a single year. More than half of that closing came from belief falling rather than management improving.

The figures are from the Data and AI Impact Report: The New Economics of Trust, which SAS published on September 30. It is the second annual edition, drawing on research insights from IDC, with 2,699 respondents across 28 countries. The gap is a subtraction: how far a company believes in its own AI, minus how much of that AI it actually manages.

Both Korean scores travelled toward the middle to meet there. Management capability climbed from 45 to 57.1. Perceived trust dropped from 73.9 to 58.6. A gap is a distance, never a height, and Korean companies again failed to reach the line SAS draws for trustworthy AI leaders.

The fall need not read as bad news. Capability stood at 45 in the year trust was scored 73.9, so that higher number had run ahead of its evidence, and the score given after a year of actually running the systems is the honest one.

The global results point the same way. Trust in generative AI runs at 76%, and trust in agentic AI, which decides and acts on its own, falls to 66%. The more that gets handed over, the less belief there is.

What complicates the picture is that the 12.1-point rise is uneven. Of the four items Korean coverage scored, model governance and oversight gained 9.3 points, the largest move. Data quality and governance sits lowest at 53.9 after only a slight gain, and responsible AI policy did not move at all.

Meanwhile 42.6% of Korean companies already run agentic AI, and 1.9% make data quality control a required step on every AI project. The two measure different things and should not be quoted as a ratio, but set side by side they show an ordering.

A gap closed by lowering expectations and a gap closed by repairing the materials leave very different things behind the following year.

## Facebook (EN)

"How much do you trust the AI your company runs?"

The people who answered that question last year were handed it again this year.

SAS and IDC put it to 2,699 people across 28 countries, and the Korean answer came down from 73.9 to 58.6.

Over the same year, the score for how much of that AI those companies actually manage climbed from 45 to 57.1.

The two numbers met in the middle. A distance of 28.9 points became 1.5.

Korean coverage ran it as a narrowing trust gap. That is not wrong.

Split the 27.4 points of narrowing in two, though, and capability supplied 12.1 while expectation supplied 15.3. The falling side is the larger one.

I would not file the fall under bad news. In the year companies scored their trust at 73.9, their capability stood at 45, so the higher number had run ahead of its evidence. The 58.6 they gave after a year of actually running the systems is the more honest figure.

I have been writing this down as the room left behind when overconfidence clears.

Which leaves the question of what moved into that room.

The item that climbed hardest inside management capability was model governance and oversight, by 9.3 points. Data quality and governance stayed at 53.9, the lowest of the four items with a published score.

Approval routes and sign-off chains go up relatively fast on paper. Recording which data a model consumed, where it came from and what condition it was in means changing systems. So it keeps getting pushed back.

42.6% of Korean companies already run AI that decides and acts on its own. 1.9% apply data quality control to every AI project.

"Are the materials for retracing why our AI answered that way anywhere inside the company right now?"

What Pebblous keeps returning to in its work on AI-Ready Data sits in the same place: where the data underneath has not been verified, even oversight is not verification.

Room created by lowering expectations gets spent once.

Room created by repairing the materials accumulates.
