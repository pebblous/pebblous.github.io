## LinkedIn (KO)

함부르크대 연구진이 독일 스타트업 100곳의 AI 마케팅 문구를 감사했다. 성능을 숫자로 내세운 곳이 62곳이었는데, 그중 49곳은 그 숫자를 어떤 데이터에서 어떤 조건으로 얻었는지 한 줄도 적지 않았다.

채점은 사람이 했다. 저자 두 명이 한 곳에 평균 한 시간을 들여 홈페이지 밖 기술 설명 페이지까지 따라 들어갔고, 무료 체험이나 데모가 있으면 직접 돌려 봤다. 채점 항목도 기준을 먼저 세워 사례를 맞춰 넣은 것이 아니다. 표본 일부를 아무 틀 없이 먼저 읽어 반복되는 패턴 다섯 가지를 찾아낸 다음 항목으로 굳혔다. 논문은 AIES 2026에 채택됐고 사전 공개본이 10월 8일 arXiv에 올라왔다.

그 62곳 가운데 출처와 측정 방법을 모두 밝혀 최고 등급을 받은 곳은 한 곳도 없었다. 더 눈에 띄는 것은 수치를 내세운 곳들 안에서의 순위다. 뒷받침이 약할수록 종합 점수가 나빴다. 숫자가 아예 없는 것보다 조건이 빠진 숫자가 더 나쁜 신호였다는 뜻이다. 숫자는 검증 가능해 보이는 겉모습을 주기 때문에, 조건이 떨어져 나간 숫자는 모호한 형용사보다 독자를 더 멀리 데려간다.

응답자 쪽 수치는 범위를 좁혀 읽어야 한다. 인터뷰와 설문에 참여한 63명은 AI 리터러시가 높은 전문직과 대학원생이고 독일 소비자의 확률 표본이 아니다. 연구진도 한계로 적어 두었다. 그 집단 안에서 과장을 직접 겪은 쪽의 'AI 탑재' 라벨 신뢰도 평균은 1.71, 겪은 적 없는 쪽은 2.48이었고, 두 집단은 AI 친숙도에서는 갈리지 않았다. 차이를 만든 것은 한 번 겪어 본 경험이다.

같은 결함은 국내에서도 적발됐다. 공정거래위원회는 2025년 11월 7일 한국소비자원과 함께 오픈마켓 가전·전자제품에서 AI 워싱 의심 사례 20건을 찾아 자진 수정과 삭제로 정리했다고 발표했다. 그중 한 건은 세탁물이 적을 때만 AI 모드가 적용되는데 그 조건이 상품 페이지에 없던 경우였다.

페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 정량 진단한다. 성능 수치 하나를 받으면 그 값이 어느 데이터셋의 어느 분할에서, 어떤 전처리와 라벨 기준으로 나왔는지부터 되묻는다. 측정 방법이 빠진 '정확도 몇 %'는 틀린 주장이라기보다 검증할 수 없는 주장이고, 검증할 수 없는 주장은 광고 문구와 같은 자리에 선다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Researchers at the University of Hamburg audited the AI marketing of 100 German startups. Sixty-two of them put performance forward as a number, and 49 of those wrote nothing about which data or which conditions produced it.

The scoring was done by hand. Two authors spent about an hour on each company, following the trail past the homepage into technical pages, and running the free trial or demo where one existed. The criteria were not fixed in advance and then matched to cases: the researchers read part of the sample with no framework at all, found five recurring patterns, and hardened those into the scoring criteria. The paper was accepted at AIES 2026 and the preprint went up on arXiv on October 8.

Not one of those 62 disclosed both the source and the method behind its figure. The more telling result is the ranking inside the group that advertised numbers at all: weaker substantiation went with a worse composite score. A figure with no conditions attached is a poorer signal than no figure. A figure looks checkable, which is exactly why one stripped of its conditions carries a reader further than a vague adjective does.

The perception figures need their scope kept on. The 63 people interviewed and surveyed are AI-literate professionals and graduate students, not a probability sample of German consumers, and the researchers say so in their limitations. Within that group, those who had run into exaggeration rated their trust in an "AI-powered" label at 1.71 on average against 2.48 for those who had not, and the two groups did not differ significantly in AI familiarity. What separated them was having watched it happen once.

The same defect has been caught in Korea. On November 7, 2025, the Korea Fair Trade Commission and the Korea Consumer Agency announced 20 suspected AI-washing listings found in home-appliance and electronics categories on major open markets, resolved through voluntary correction or removal. One of them advertised an AI mode that only engages on small laundry loads, a condition the product page never mentioned.

Pebblous diagnoses the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. When a performance figure arrives, the first thing asked is which dataset and which split it came from, under what preprocessing, against what labeling standard. An accuracy figure with no method attached is less a false claim than an uncheckable one, and an uncheckable claim sits exactly where advertising copy sits.

## Facebook (EN)

There is a voice AI that cannot dial any phone number containing a 2.

A participant in the study wrote that about a system they had used themselves. The system did not understand the way they say 2.

The paper sets that account beside the homepage copy of an automotive voice-AI company, which says it builds "Digital Employees that could think, talk, and act alongside their human counterparts."

The study, published on October 8 by researchers at the University of Hamburg, scored the public marketing of 100 German startups on seven criteria.

Sixty-two advertised performance as a number. Forty-nine of those said nothing about the data or the conditions behind it.

That much is roughly what you would expect.

The less expected part sits under it.

Among the 62 that put a number forward, weaker substantiation went with a worse overall score. A figure with its conditions stripped off was a poorer signal than no figure at all.

A number looks checkable. Strip away everything that would let you check it and the look survives intact.

And once someone has trusted that look, they read the next number through it.

Respondents who had run into AI-washing rated their trust in an "AI-powered" label at 1.71. Those who had not rated it at 2.48. The two groups did not differ in how much they knew about AI. What separated them was having been through it once.

These respondents are AI-literate professionals and graduate students, so this is not a consumer sample. It is a number produced by the most demanding audience available.

Which means the cost of exaggeration is not paid by the company that exaggerated.

It is paid by whoever measures honestly next.

"What would I need in order to measure this figure again?"

If an answer comes back, the number becomes a claim you can write into a contract as a condition. If none comes back, it cannot be used for comparison.

Pebblous diagnoses the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. When a performance figure arrives, the first question is which dataset and which split produced it, under what labeling standard.

What the researchers counted across 100 companies was not the volume of lying. It was how often no answer to that question was ready.

Writing down the limits is not purely a cost to the seller, either. Once the conditions are on the page, a failure outside them is out of scope rather than a product defect.

Seventy-six left that space blank anyway.
