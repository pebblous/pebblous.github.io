미국 FDA가 영상 판독 AI의 시판 전 심사 면제 청원을 기각한 것은 접수 161일째였습니다. 법은 180일 안에 답이 없으면 그 청원을 인용된 것으로 본다고 정해 두고 있습니다.

9월 17일 연방관보에 실린 최종 명령이 그 결정을 법이 정한 형식으로 확정했습니다. 청원은 제조사가 자기 기기의 위험을 스스로 평가하고 거기 맞는 시판 후 관리 계획을 스스로 정하게 해 달라고 요청했습니다. FDA가 문제 삼은 것은 계획이 부실하다는 점이 아니었습니다. 회사가 그렇게 정한 계획이 충분한지를 FDA가 읽고 판단할 자리가 청원 구조 안에 없다는 점이었습니다.

청원의 전제는 사람이 최종 판독자로 앉아 있으니 기기가 달라져도 현장에서 걸러진다는 것이었습니다. FDA는 유방암 선별검사를 들어 반박했습니다. 선별 촬영만으로는 진단이 내려지지 않아, 생검을 하기 전까지 의사는 기기가 짚은 것이 진짜 양성인지 가를 수 없습니다. 감시하는 사람은 앉아 있는데 확인해 줄 자료가 그 자리에 없습니다.

기각 서한은 FDA 자신의 기록도 꺼냈습니다. 특정 CAD 제품코드로 수년간 허가된 510(k)를 전부 보니 모두 한 번씩 보류를 거쳤고, 보류 사유에는 예외 없이 성능 시험 방법이나 결과의 미비가 들어 있었습니다. 청원이 면제의 문턱으로 삼자고 한 과거 허가 이력이, FDA의 기록 안에서는 성능 문제를 걸러 내지 못한 조건이었습니다.

사후 관리라는 발상 전체가 닫힌 것은 아닙니다. FDA는 같은 자리에서 사전결정 변경관리 계획을 대안으로 가리켰습니다. 계획서를 허가 신청 때 함께 내고, 승인된 범위 안의 갱신은 새 신고 없이 적용하는 구조입니다. 식약처도 6월 26일 디지털의료기기 변경관리 계획서 가이드라인을 제정했습니다.

페블러스가 데이터 계보와 AI 자재명세서를 문서로 남기는 이유도 같은 자리에 있습니다. 어떤 데이터로 학습했고 무엇을 정답으로 삼았고 어떤 조건에서 성능을 쟀는지가 남아 있어야, 그것이 바깥에서 읽을 수 있는 계획이 됩니다.

사후 관리가 사전 심사를 대신하려면, 그 관리가 규제기관이든 고객이든 읽을 수 있는 형식으로 먼저 존재해야 합니다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

The FDA turned down a petition to exempt radiology AI from premarket review on day 161. Under the statute, silence past day 180 would have granted that petition by default.

A final order carried in the Federal Register on September 17 put the April decision into the form the law requires. The petition asked that manufacturers be allowed to assess the risk of their own devices and set a post-market monitoring plan scaled to it. The agency's objection was not that the plan was thin. Nothing in the petition gave the FDA a way to read that plan and judge whether it was enough.

The petition rested on the premise that a licensed radiologist sitting as final reader would catch a device that had changed. The FDA answered with breast cancer screening. A screening image does not settle a diagnosis, so until a biopsy is done the reader cannot separate a true positive from a false one. The supervision is in place. The confirmation is not.

The decision letter also produced the agency's own files. Every 510(k) cleared under one CAD product code over several years had gone on hold at least once, and every hold cited gaps in performance test methods or results. The clearance history the petition proposed as a threshold had not screened out performance problems in the FDA's own records.

None of this closes the door on post-market oversight. The FDA pointed to predetermined change control plans instead, where the plan is filed with the original submission and updates inside the cleared envelope ship without a new notification. Korea's MFDS issued a guideline built on the same order for digital medical devices on June 26.

This is where data lineage and an AI bill of materials earn their keep at Pebblous. What a model trained on, what counted as ground truth, and the conditions under which performance was measured have to be written down before any of it can be read from outside.

Post-market monitoring stands in for premarket review only once it exists in a form a regulator, a customer or an auditor can actually read.

## Facebook (EN)

A screening report sometimes carries an extra line under the radiologist's reading: an AI assisted with it.

I have never once looked up what that software cleared, or when.

A document out of the United States took up the same question, so I read it.

A petition had asked the FDA to let certain radiology software reach the market without premarket review. Software that flags a suspicious lesion on a scan, or moves an urgent patient up the queue.

Its premise was simple. A licensed radiologist sits as the final reader, so if the device drifts, the clinic will notice.

The FDA answered with breast cancer screening.

A screening image does not settle whether there is cancer. Often a biopsy is what settles it.

Which means that until that biopsy happens, the reader cannot confirm whether what the device flagged was real.

Someone is watching. What would tell them they were right or wrong is not in the room.

"Ground truth that arrives late."

That shape is not confined to medicine.

A delivered model can quietly lose accuracy in the field while the signal that would reveal it comes days later, or never comes at all.

In those jobs the heavier fact is not that performance fell. It is that nobody knew it had.

What the FDA found missing in the petition was, in the end, something to read. A place where a plan a company wrote for itself could be read from outside and judged.

This is why Pebblous keeps data lineage on paper. What a model learned from, what counted as ground truth, and the conditions under which performance was measured have to survive somewhere before the next judgment can be made.

"Who in your organization could prove your model's field performance on paper today?"

If no name comes to mind, then what the FDA could not find in the petition may be missing on our side as well.
