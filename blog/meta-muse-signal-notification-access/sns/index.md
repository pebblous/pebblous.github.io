## LinkedIn (KO)

메타의 AI 비서 Muse가 시그널 메시지의 본문을 읽었다. 암호는 깨지지 않았다.

로이터가 시그널 직원, AI Now Institute 연구진과 함께 접근 경로를 다시 만들어 본 결과를 11월 18일 내놓았다. 안드로이드에서는 '알림 접근' 권한 하나로 도착한 메시지의 미리보기 글자를 가져갔고, 맥에서는 손쉬운 사용 도구와 화면 갈무리를 엮어 같은 글자에 닿았다. 읽힌 것은 암호문이 아니라 이미 풀려서 화면에 떠 있던 글자다.

이 사건의 무게는 두 경로가 지나지 않은 자리에 있다. 메타는 외부 서비스를 하나씩 붙이라고 Connector라는 연동 기능을 따로 두었는데, 시그널을 거기 올린 적 없는 사용자에게서도 메시지가 읽혔다. 설치할 때 한 번 내준 범용 권한이 연동 목록과 상관없이 작동한 것이다.

같은 시도는 아이폰에서 통하지 않았다. 두 운영체제가 알림을 다루는 방식이 애초에 다르다. 애플은 이번 주 맥 업데이트로 손쉬운 사용 접근을 좁혔다. 플랫폼이 설정을 바꾸면 닫히는 구멍이었다는 뜻이고, 규제 당국이 움직였다는 보도는 아직 없다.

데이터 경계를 묻는 질문이 한 칸 옮겨 간다. 어떤 데이터를 주기로 동의했는가에 더해, 그 비서가 무엇을 볼 수 있는 자리에 앉았는가를 같이 물어야 한다. 앞쪽 답은 연동 목록에, 뒤쪽 답은 운영체제 권한 목록에 있다. 페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 정량 진단한다. 창고에 들어온 데이터를 재는 자와, 화면에서 읽혀 나가는 글자를 세는 자는 아직 같은 표 위에 없다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Meta's AI assistant Muse read the body of a Signal message. The encryption was never broken.

Reuters published the finding on November 18, after rebuilding the access routes alongside Signal staff and researchers at the AI Now Institute. On Android, a single notification access permission handed Muse the preview text of an arriving message. On macOS, accessibility tooling plus screen capture reached the same characters. Nothing was decrypted; the characters had already been decrypted and drawn on a display.

The revealing detail is which route was not used. Meta ships a separate feature, Connector, for attaching outside services one by one, and Signal had never been attached by the users whose messages were read. A permission granted once during setup turned out to operate independently of that list.

The same attempt failed on iPhone, because the two operating systems treat notifications differently to begin with. Apple narrowed accessibility access in a macOS update this week. That makes it a gap a platform can close by changing a setting, and so far no regulator has been reported to have acted.

The question behind any data boundary shifts by one step. Alongside which data someone agreed to hand over sits a second question: where is the assistant seated, and what falls within its view from there. The first answer lives in a list of linked services; the second lives in a list of operating system permissions. Pebblous audits the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. Material arriving at the warehouse gets measured; text leaving through a screen has no column of its own yet.

## Facebook (EN)

Turn the phone face down and a message still arrives. A few lines surface on the lock screen.

Those few lines are already decrypted. The screen exists so that someone with full hands can glance down and catch what came in.

Reuters walked back through the access routes with Signal staff and researchers at the AI Now Institute. What Meta's assistant Muse read, in that test, was those few lines. The account came out on November 18.

It read them for people who had never attached Signal to Muse.

The notification shade and the accessibility layer are passages cut for people. One so that a person who cannot see the screen can hear what is on it, one so that a person in the middle of something can catch a message in passing.

Which is why no inventory anywhere counts them as routes that data travels. The reader at the far end was a person.

"From where the assistant sits on our work machines, how much is in view right now?"

Pebblous audits the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. What a report like that covers is material that arrived at the warehouse. What leaked here never arrived anywhere; it was read off a display.

Apple narrowed one of the two passages this week. That it could be narrowed says something about how long it had been open.
