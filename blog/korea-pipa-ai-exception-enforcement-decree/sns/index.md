## LinkedIn (KO)

심의를 통과해도 특례를 받는 것은 회사가 아니라 의결 하나다.

개인정보보호위원회가 11월 23일 「개인정보 보호법 시행령」 일부개정령안을 입법예고했다. 이미 공포돼 2027년 3월 9일 시행되는 AI 특례의 세부 기준이다. 가명처리나 익명처리를 한 데이터로는 개발이 어려운 경우, 개인정보위의 심의·의결을 거치면 원본 개인정보를 학습에 쓸 수 있다.

의결을 받으면 열 개 조문의 적용이 빠진다. 목적 외 이용 제한부터 민감정보와 고유식별정보, 주민등록번호, 영상정보처리기기 조항까지다. 다만 빠지는 범위는 의결에 적힌 목적과 데이터의 종류, 처리 방법 안쪽이다. 그 경계를 넘는 처리에는 원래 조문이 그대로 걸린다. 특례 안에 있는 데이터셋과 밖에 있는 데이터셋이 한 회사 안에 함께 있게 된다는 뜻이다.

개정안이 요구하는 안전조치 넷도 같은 단위로 붙는다. 책임자 지정과 내부관리계획 수립, 심의 범위 밖 이용 방지, 접근권한 제한과 통제, 이용할 필요가 없어졌을 때의 파기. 넷 중 어느 것도 회사 전체가 아니라 그 데이터셋을 겨냥한다. 민감정보나 고유식별정보를 쓰거나, 아동의 특성을 반영하거나, 사람을 지속적으로 관찰·추적하는 모델을 만드는 경우에는 심의 전에 위험요인평가를 먼저 받는다.

확정된 것은 법률이고 시행령은 아직 초안이다. 의견은 2027년 1월 4일까지 받고, 그 뒤로 법제처 심사가 남아 있다. 법무법인 해설들은 적용 배제 목록에 제공 제한 조문인 제18조와 제19조가 들어 있어 제3자 제공까지 열리는지가 조문만으로는 갈린다고 짚는다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

What clears a review in Korea's new AI exception is not a company. It is one resolution.

The Personal Information Protection Commission put a draft amendment to the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act out for public comment on November 23. It fills in the AI exception written into the Act already promulgated and taking effect on March 9, 2027. Where anonymized or pseudonymized data will not carry the development work, a company may train on raw personal data once the commission reviews and approves the request.

Approval lifts ten provisions, from the limit on use beyond the original purpose through the rules on sensitive data, unique identifiers, resident registration numbers and image recording devices. The lift runs only as far as the resolution does, covering the stated purpose, the categories of data and the processing method. Step outside that line and the original provisions apply again. A company ends up holding datasets inside the exception and datasets outside it at the same time.

The four safeguards in the draft attach at the same level. Name an officer and run an internal management plan, block use beyond what was approved, restrict and control access, and delete the data once the need for it ends. None of the four is written against the company as a whole. Where the work involves sensitive data or unique identifiers, reflects the traits of children, or builds a model that observes and tracks people over time, a risk factor assessment comes before the review.

The statute is settled; the decree is not. Comments close on January 4, 2027, and legislative review follows. Law firm commentaries note that the exclusion list includes Articles 18 and 19, which govern provision to third parties, leaving it unclear from the text alone whether the exception reaches that far.

## Facebook (EN)

Approvals are the kind of thing you want early.

A permit in hand before the work begins. Most of them are built to reward exactly that.

Korea's privacy regulator published a draft decree on November 23 that turns the habit around.

From March 9, 2027, a company that cannot develop an AI system on pseudonymized or anonymized data may train on raw personal data, once the Personal Information Protection Commission has reviewed the request and resolved to allow it.

But if processing has not begun within six months of that resolution, and there is no good reason why, the processing is shut off entirely.

A permit that ages in the drawer.

Nor does approval close the matter. The commission monitors whether the resolution is being carried out, and can ask for records whenever it needs them.

A resolution, then, is less a gate you pass than a clock that starts.

So the order most teams would reach for, secure the approval and schedule the work behind it, turns out to be the expensive one here. The filing date has to be counted backwards from the day someone first touches the data.

One question follows from that.

"Can we name the day we will actually start touching that data?"
