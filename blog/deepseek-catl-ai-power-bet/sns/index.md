10월 22일 로이터와 블룸버그는 딥시크가 1,000억 위안 규모의 조달을 며칠 안에 마무리한다고 전했습니다. 참여한 곳 가운데 최대 투자자로 꼽힌 둘이 텐센트와 CATL입니다. 목표액은 500억 위안에서 시작해 적어도 두 번 올라 지금 자리에 왔습니다.

CATL이 AI 모델 회사에 돈을 넣은 이유는 아직 설명된 적이 없습니다. 대신 이 회사가 올해 사들인 목록이 방향을 말합니다. 5월에는 데이터센터 운영사 VNET의 지분 38.1%를 9억 4,200만 달러에 가져갔습니다. 8월에는 데이터센터에 직류 전기를 넣는 장치를 만드는 중헝전기의 모회사 지분을 받았고, 6월에는 딥시크의 첫 외부 라운드에도 들어갔습니다. 세 건 모두 지분은 크게 받되 운전대는 받지 않는 모양으로 짜였습니다.

딥시크가 파는 것은 토큰입니다. 량원펑은 9월 투자자 모임에서 연환산 매출이 10억 달러에 이르렀고 그 대부분이 API 판매에서 나온다고 밝혔습니다. 그리고 8월 16일부터 이 회사의 요금표는 하루를 둘로 갈랐습니다. 중국 업무시간대 토큰 값이 나머지 시간의 정확히 두 배입니다. 전기 요금의 계시별 요금제와 같은 모양입니다.

딥시크가 요금을 그렇게 가른 이유를 전기 값이라고 밝힌 적은 없습니다. 회사가 내놓은 설명은 자원 배분과 서비스 안정성이었고, 설비가 붐비는 시간을 피하게 하려는 수요 조절로 읽는 편이 사실에 가깝습니다. 다만 추론 수요와 전력 수요는 같은 시계를 씁니다. 둘 다 사람들이 일하는 낮에 몰립니다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

The biggest backer of an AI company raising about $15 billion turned out to be a battery maker.

Reuters and Bloomberg reported on October 22 that DeepSeek is days away from closing a raise of 100 billion yuan. Of the firms taking part, the two named as the biggest investors are Tencent and CATL. The target began at 50 billion yuan and was lifted at least twice on the way to where it stands now.

Why CATL put money into an AI model company has never been explained. What it bought this year says more. In May it took 38.1 percent of the data center operator VNET for $942 million, and in August it took a stake in the parent of Zhongheng Electric, which makes the equipment that feeds direct current into data centers. In June it joined DeepSeek's first outside round. All three were arranged the same way: the money goes in big and the steering wheel does not come along.

What DeepSeek sells is tokens. At a September investor meeting Liang Wenfeng said annualized revenue had reached $1 billion, most of it from API sales. Since August 16 the company's price list has split the day in two. A token bought during Chinese business hours costs exactly twice what the same token costs in the remaining hours. Time-of-use electricity pricing has that shape.

DeepSeek has never said electricity is why it split the price. The reason the company gave was resource allocation and service stability, and reading it as demand shaping stays closer to the facts. Even so, inference demand and power demand run on one clock. Both crowd into the daytime, when people are at work.

Pebblous puts a number on the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. An answer that has to be asked again, a generation rerun because of a hallucination: both are extra tokens, and where power enters the unit cost of inference, extra tokens are an extra electricity bill. Data that reaches a conclusion in one pass and data that takes three can score the same on accuracy and still produce different invoices.

More than fifteen firms ended up on the list.

Midea in appliances, Meituan in delivery, Geely in cars, GigaDevice in semiconductors. Internet companies like NetEase were there too.

The two named as the biggest investors were Tencent and CATL.

CATL makes batteries for electric cars.

Had I read the same list two years ago, I would have expected a chipmaker or a cloud company in that seat.

The raise DeepSeek is closing comes to about 100 billion yuan, roughly $15 billion. The target started at 50 billion yuan and was lifted at least twice.

Why CATL put money into an AI model company is still unexplained.

What remains is the list of what it bought this year.

In May it took 38.1 percent of VNET, a company that builds and runs data centers, for $942 million.

In August it took 49 percent of the parent of Zhongheng Electric, which makes the equipment that feeds direct current into data centers. Part of what it paid was not cash but one of its own power-conversion companies, handed over whole.

In June it joined DeepSeek's first outside round.

Add CATL's core business, storage batteries, and the stack comes to four layers. From the place where the model runs down to the place where the electricity is kept, the whole column is filled.

Lay the three deals on top of one another and one thing is common to all of them. The money goes in big and the steering wheel does not come along.

At Zhongheng Electric the actual controller did not change. At VNET it holds 38.1 percent and still agreed to vote as the founder wishes. At DeepSeek it never reached the shareholder register.

It reads as buying not control of a company but the position that company stands in.

So what is DeepSeek selling?

At a September investor meeting Liang Wenfeng said annualized revenue had reached $1 billion. Most of that is the token fees enterprises and developers pay to reach the model through the API.

And since August 16 DeepSeek's price list has split the day in two.

A token bought during Chinese business hours costs exactly twice what it costs in the remaining hours. The whole weekend falls on the cheap side.

Time-of-use electricity pricing has that shape.

DeepSeek has never said electricity is the reason. What the company offered was resource allocation and service stability.

Even so, inference demand and power demand run on one clock. Both crowd into the daytime, when people are at work.
