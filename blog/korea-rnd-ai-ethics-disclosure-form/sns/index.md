## LinkedIn (KO)

국가연구개발에 AI를 쓴 연구자에게 어떤 모델을 어디에 썼는지 적어 내라는 서식이 나왔다.

과학기술정보통신부가 9월 20일 '국가연구개발 AI 연구윤리 가이드'를 확정했다. 보고서와 논문에 붙이는 공개 서식에는 활용 도구명과 모델, 활용 내용, 활용 기간, 최종 책임 확인 네 칸이 있다. 가이드가 채워 둔 예시는 도구 이름 하나로 끝내지 않고, 선행 연구 탐색과 방법론 도식에 각각 어느 모델을 썼는지까지 나눠 적었다.

7대 권고사항은 모두 "해야 함"으로 끝나고, 어겼을 때 무슨 일이 생기는지는 적혀 있지 않다. 제재처분이 적힌 자리는 심사에서 숨긴 지시문으로 평가를 왜곡하는 행위 한 조항뿐이다.

그 조항이 어디서 왔는지는 가이드가 배경을 적은 첫 장에서 직접 밝혔다. 은닉 프롬프트를 활용한 학술 리뷰 조작이다. 2025년 7월 니케이 아시아는 arXiv 프리프린트 17편에서 그 지시문을 찾아냈다.

가이드 자체는 권고라 법적 구속력이 없고, 이포커스는 그 한 줄도 새 벌칙이 아니라 기존 연구부정행위 제재가 은닉 프롬프트에도 걸린다는 안내에 가깝다고 짚었다. 서울신문이 전한 현장 반응에서는 도구로 쓰는 것과 주체적으로 판단하는 것의 경계가 실무에서 또렷하지 않다는 지적이 나왔다.

가이드에 적힌 말은 아니지만, 연구윤리 문서라는 이름표를 떼고 보면 저 네 칸은 계보 기록의 항목과 같다. 페블러스가 AI-Ready Data를 말할 때 붙드는 구별이 여기 걸린다. 자료가 있다는 것과 그 자료가 어디서 왔는지 말할 수 있다는 것은 다른 상태다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Korea has put out a form that asks researchers on national R&D projects to write down which AI model they used, and where.

Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT finalized its national R&D AI research ethics guide on September 20. The disclosure form released with it carries four boxes: the tool and model name, what it was used for, the period of use, and a signed statement of responsibility. The worked example does not stop at a tool name. It records which model searched the prior literature and which model built the methodology diagram, item by item.

All seven recommendations end in "should," and none of them says what follows if one is broken. A sanction appears in exactly one clause: hiding instructions inside a submitted manuscript to steer a review counts as research misconduct. That clause traces back to July 2025, when Nikkei Asia found hidden prompts in 17 arXiv preprints from 14 institutions across eight countries.

The guide is non-binding. E-Focus noted that the sanction line creates no new penalty and mostly serves notice that existing misconduct rules already reach hidden prompts. Researchers quoted by Seoul Shinmun said the line between using AI as a tool and exercising one's own judgment stays blurred in daily practice.

Peel the research-ethics label off those four boxes and a familiar record shows through: what was used, which version, for how long, and who answers for the result. On the data side that goes by the name of lineage. The guide does not put it that way, but it is the distinction we hold on to whenever we talk about AI-Ready Data. Having the material and being able to say where it came from are two different states.

## Facebook (EN)

"Can you say, right now, which model and which version went into the work you handed over last month?"

That was the question I got stuck on while reading Korea's new guide. The tool name comes back easily enough. The version does not.

On September 20, Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT finalized an AI research ethics guide for national R&D. A disclosure form came with it, and it carries four boxes. The tool and model, what it was used for, the period of use, and a final statement of responsibility.

It is not a checkbox for whether AI was used. It is a place to write down, in sentences, what was handed over and for how long.

The last box is a single sentence and a signature line. The researcher confirms that the process of using generative AI was managed transparently and in good faith.

Confirming that a process was managed only holds if a record of managing it exists. Somebody can sign over a process that left nothing behind. But then the signature is a statement leaning on memory.

Move the same boxes onto company work and they sit empty far more often. AI came into cleaning datasets and refining labeling rules a long time ago, and the trace of it usually survives in one person's memory and in chat windows that have scrolled away. Schema and files travel with the handover document. Which model took over which part does not.

This is the distinction we hold on to whenever we talk about AI-Ready Data. Having the material and being able to say where it came from are two different states. The first holds as long as the file exists. The second holds only if somebody wrote it down at the time.

"On the day that form lands on our own desk, what could we actually write in it?"

A form that arrived on national R&D first is unlikely to stay there for long.
