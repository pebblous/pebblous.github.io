180억 달러가 물린 AI 데이터센터의 대출채권이 액면을 밑도는 1달러당 89센트에 거래됐다.

오라클이 9월 24일 뉴멕시코 산타테레사의 데이터센터 캠퍼스 프로젝트 주피터 개발사에 불가항력 통지를 보낸 직후다. 통지가 든 사유는 전기다.

늦은 것은 반도체가 아니라 가스관과 허가다. 이 캠퍼스는 외부 전력망에서 전기를 받지 않고 부지 안에 깐 블룸에너지 연료전지로 2.45기가와트를 직접 만들기로 했다. 그 연료전지에 넣을 가스를 끌어올 관이 주 신탁지 통과 신청에서 두 차례 거부돼 공급 개시가 반년 밀렸고, 발전소를 돌릴 대기질 허가는 11월 23일 결정 시한을 앞두고 아직 나오지 않았다.

통지가 바꾸는 것은 액수가 아니라 시점이다. 2028년 가동이 무산되면 임대료 납부 개시가 최대 3년 뒤로 밀린다. 지급이 시작되면 오라클이 낼 총액은 계약 그대로고, 개발사 블루아울이 그만큼 늦게 받는다. 이연이 통지만으로 확정되는 것도 아니다. 불가항력 사유가 실제로 있었다는 데 양측이 합의해야 효력이 생긴다. 오라클은 프로젝트가 계획한 일정대로 가고 있다고 밝혔다.

값을 먼저 고친 쪽은 주식시장이 아니라 신용시장이었다. 대출채권에는 지연 위험의 값이 이미 붙었고, 우리가 매달 결제하는 AI 서비스 요금표에는 아직 그 줄이 없다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

An $18 billion loan behind an AI data center changed hands last week at 89 cents on the dollar.

The trades followed Oracle's force majeure notice, sent on September 24 to the developers of Project Jupiter, the campus it has agreed to occupy in Santa Teresa, New Mexico. The cause the notice names is electricity.

What is running late is not silicon. It is a pipeline and a permit. The campus does not draw from the grid outside its fence: it is to make 2.45 gigawatts on site with Bloom Energy fuel cells, fed by a newly laid gas line. New Mexico's State Land Office has twice refused that line a crossing over state trust land, pushing first gas back about six months, and the air permit the plant needs is still pending against a November 23 deadline.

The notice moves timing, not money. If the 2028 start falls through, the date rent begins slides back by as much as three years. Once payments do begin, the total rent is what the lease always said, and Blue Owl, the developer's owner, simply waits longer for it. The deferral is not settled either. Oracle and Blue Owl have to agree that a qualifying event actually occurred. Oracle says Project Jupiter remains on its planned schedule.

Credit repriced before equity did. Delay risk now carries a number in the loan market and none at all on the AI service invoices the rest of us pay every month.

## Facebook (EN)

Four buildings are going up on 1,400 acres of New Mexico desert.

The electricity that will run them is not coming from the grid outside the fence. It is to be made on site, in a fuel cell plant, fed by natural gas arriving through 18 miles of newly laid pipe.

That pipe has to cross six-tenths of a mile of state trust land. New Mexico's State Land Office has refused the crossing twice.

Six-tenths of a mile. A 2.45-gigawatt plan is held up there.

On September 24, Oracle sent the campus developers a force majeure notice. If the power arrives late and the 2028 start falls through, it says, the date rent begins can be pushed back by as much as three years. Nothing is being forgiven. The total stays what the lease said, and only the waiting changes hands.

I have started thinking of the letter as a delay invoice. A pipeline refusal, a permit review, a water lawsuit: things that happened on the ground, moving through one clause of a contract and settling onto somebody's books. The first book rewritten was not equity but credit. The $18 billion lent against this campus traded below par.

And the invoice still has one blank line on it.

"How much of this delay is priced into the AI services we pay for every month?"

When we talk about AI-Ready Data at Pebblous, we usually start with the training data. Is the format consistent, are the labels right, is the origin traceable. This week says another kind sits outside that list. A position in the interconnection queue. A decision deadline on a permit. The stage a lawsuit has reached. None of it appears on a model scorecard, and all of it decides when a plan can actually stand. A single date, November 23, 2026, holds the next quarter of a 2.45-gigawatt plan.

A delay does not disappear. It moves from book to book, and eventually it is written on a line somewhere. Nobody has said yet which line that is.
