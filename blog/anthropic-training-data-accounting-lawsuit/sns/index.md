손해배상 액수보다 눈에 걸리는 건 구제 목록이다.

소니뮤직퍼블리싱과 워너채플뮤직을 비롯한 35개 음악 출판 법인이 8월 28일 앤트로픽을 제소했다. 아홉 개 구제 항목 중 e항은 클로드의 학습 데이터와 학습 방법, 알려진 모델 능력에 대한 회계 보고를 명해 달라고 법원에 요구한다. 어떤 가사와 저작물로 학습했는지 특정하고, 그 데이터를 수집·복제·처리·인코딩한 방법을 공개하고, 수집이나 라이선스에 관여시킨 제3자까지 밝히라는 내용이다. 뒤이은 f항은 침해 사본을 법원 감독하에 폐기한 다음 그 이행 방식을 선서 보고서로 제출하라고 적었다.

코퍼스 안을 볼 수 없는 쪽이 코퍼스 안을 아는 쪽에게 목록을 내놓으라고 법원을 통해 요구하는 구조다. 네 번째 소인은 여기서 한 걸음 더 간다. 저작권관리정보 제거를 침해와 별개인 독립 청구로 세웠고, 원고가 근거로 든 것은 2021년 앤트로픽이 본문 추출 도구를 바꾼 기록이다. 푸터의 저작권 고지를 남기는 도구 대신 그것을 확실히 지우는 도구를 채택했다는 주장이다.

여기까지는 모두 원고의 주장이다. 앤트로픽은 아직 답변서를 내지 않았고, 같은 변호인단이 낸 세 번째 소송이며 계류 중인 사건의 주장을 재활용한 것이라고 반박했다. 구제 목록은 법원의 판단이 아니라 원고가 바라는 바를 적어 낸 문서에 지나지 않는다.

그럼에도 산출물의 규격은 이미 공개됐다. 데이터 계보 대장은 요구받은 시점에 만들 수 있는 문서가 아니다. 페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 정량 진단해 왔고, 이번 소장은 그 데이터가 어디서 왔고 무엇이 빠졌는지를 적어 둔 기록을 묻는다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

The damages number is the familiar part of this lawsuit. The list of remedies is not.

Thirty-five music publishing entities, Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell among them, sued Anthropic on August 28. Item (e) of the prayer for relief asks the court to order an accounting of Claude's training data, training methods, and known model capabilities: identify the lyrics and other works the models were trained on, disclose how that data was collected, copied, processed, and encoded, and name any third parties engaged to collect or license it. Item (f) follows with destruction of infringing copies under court supervision, then a sworn report detailing how the order was carried out.

The structure is straightforward. The side that cannot see inside the corpus is asking the court to make the side that can produce a list. The fourth count goes further, treating removal of copyright management information as a claim that stands apart from infringement. The plaintiffs ground that count in a 2021 record of Anthropic comparing text-extraction tools and, they allege, dropping the one that left footer copyright notices intact.

All of this is what the plaintiffs allege. Anthropic has not answered the complaint, and told Fortune this is the third suit from the same lawyers, recycling allegations already before the courts. A prayer for relief is a request, not a ruling.

Even so, the shape of the artifact is now on the public record. A lineage ledger is not a document you can assemble on the day it is demanded. Pebblous has been measuring the quality of AI training data through DataClinic, and what this complaint asks about is the adjacent record: where that data came from, and what was stripped out of it along the way.

## Facebook (EN)

I read the 48-page complaint backwards, starting from the last pages.

The prayer for relief is usually the dull part. Pay this much. Stop doing that.

I stopped at the fifth of nine items.

"An order requiring Defendants to provide an accounting of the training data, training methods, and known capabilities of Anthropic's AI models."

An accounting. Not a claim about money, but a claim about a list.

Identify the lyrics you trained on. Disclose how the data was collected and processed. Name the third parties you engaged to collect or license it.

The suit was filed on August 28 by thirty-five music publishing entities, Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell among them. It is what the plaintiffs allege, and Anthropic has not yet answered.

Still, that one sentence stayed with me.

Keeping data lineage is old advice. For quality control, for reproducibility. It has mostly lived on the list of things that are good to do well, internally.

This complaint opens a second column beside that list. A column for 'artifacts a court can order into existence.'

And that is what catches. A ledger like this is not a document you can assemble on the day someone asks for it. It accumulates one line at a time: the day you collected something, the day you chose a version, the day you changed a preprocessing rule.

"Could we produce tomorrow a ledger of the corpus we put into training today?"

The work Pebblous has been doing on data sits near that question. Seeing what is inside a dataset, and writing down where it came from, seem to be the front and back halves of the same ledger.
