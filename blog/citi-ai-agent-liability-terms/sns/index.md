## LinkedIn (KO)

시티은행이 11월 중순 온라인 이용약관에 H절을 새로 넣었다. 외부 AI 비서에게 계좌 접근을 맡긴 고객은 그 비서가 한 일을 자기가 승인한 것으로 본다는 조항이다. 시티가 직접 만들어 제공하는 AI 도구는 그 조항에서 빠진다.

간주는 첫 위임에서 끝나지 않는다. 조문을 옮긴 보도에 따르면, 고객이 건넨 자격 증명이 다시 공유되거나 퍼져 다른 사람이나 다른 비서가 쓰더라도 시티는 그 접근을 여전히 고객이 승인한 것으로 본다. 해킹을 당해도 고객 책임이라는 요약이 여기서 나왔다. 비서의 활동을 지켜볼 의무 역시 고객 쪽에 적혀 있다.

같은 문장을 적은 곳이 더 있다. PNC는 11월 18일 약관을 고쳤고 시티즌스는 같은 주에 고객에게 경고 메일을 보냈다. JP모건체이스와 뱅크오브아메리카는 10월에 먼저 알렸다.

다섯 곳의 자산을 합치면 9조 6천억 달러다. 다만 미국에서 가장 큰 다섯 은행은 아니다. PNC는 자산 순위 여덟 번째이고 시티즌스는 그보다 작다.

약관이 그렇게 적었다는 것과 법이 그 해석을 받아 준다는 것은 다른 문제다. 미국의 전자자금이체 규정은 무단 이체를 소비자가 승인하지 않았고 이익도 돌아가지 않은 이체로 정의하고, 제3자가 훔친 자격 증명으로 한 이체도 여기에 들어간다는 것이 소비자금융보호국 입장이라고 법률 분석은 전한다. 로그인에 성공한 사실이 거래 하나하나를 승인했다는 뜻은 아니다. 더 불리한 쪽은 반대 경우다. 고객이 제 손으로 로그인 정보를 건넸다면, 권한을 거둬들였다고 은행에 알리기 전까지는 그 비서의 행위가 무단으로 잡히지 않는다.

앤트로픽은 이 다툼을 설계로 비껴갔다. 클로드 금융 기능은 플레이드를 거쳐 계좌를 읽기만 하고 돈은 옮기지 못한다. 쓰기 권한을 들이지 않으면 누가 승인했느냐를 따질 자리도 생기지 않는다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Citi added a clause to its online terms of use in mid-November. A customer who lets an outside AI assistant into a Citi account is treated as having authorized whatever that assistant then does. The same clause carves out the AI tools Citi builds itself.

The deeming does not stop at the first handover. Reporting that carried the contract language says that where the credentials are shared on, so another person or another AI agent uses them, Citi still counts that access as authorized by the customer. Watching the agent is the customer's duty too. That is where the line about being on your own even after a hack comes from.

Other banks have written the same sentence. PNC amended its terms on November 18 and Citizens emailed a warning to customers that week, following notices from JPMorgan Chase and Bank of America in October. The five hold $9.6 trillion between them, which does not make them the five largest banks in the country. PNC ranks eighth by assets and Citizens is smaller again.

What the terms call authorized and what the law calls authorized are not one thing. Regulation E defines an unauthorized transfer as one the consumer never authorized and gained no benefit from, and law firm analysis reports the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau treating transfers made with stolen credentials as falling inside it. A successful login is not approval of every transaction that follows. The harder case points the other way: where the customer handed the login over personally, the rule stops calling the result unauthorized until the bank has been told the authority is withdrawn.

Anthropic designed its way around the argument. The Claude finance feature reads an account through Plaid and cannot move the money sitting in it. Keep write access out and nobody has to establish who approved what.

Pebblous puts the quality of AI training data into figures, and DataClinic is where the work happens. A contract can decide what counts as authorized by writing the sentence. The condition of a dataset is not decided by a sentence. It has to be read off the material.

## Facebook (EN)

Has anyone read one of those notices about amended terms of use all the way to the end?

I mostly have not.

The online terms Citi revised in mid-November carry a new provision called Section H. A customer who lets an outside AI assistant reach the account is treated as having authorized what that assistant does.

That much is roughly where you would expect the line to fall.

The sentence after it goes somewhere else. If the credentials the customer handed over are shared further, or spread until another person or another assistant is holding them, Citi still treats the resulting access as authorized by the customer.

'What the first handover covers.'

Terms of use do not settle what happened. They settle what it will be taken for. The US rule on electronic fund transfers keeps its own definition standing nearby, an unauthorized transfer being one the consumer never authorized and gained no benefit from, and a single sentence in a contract does not drag that definition along behind it. A customer who passed the login details over personally, though, has trouble getting inside that protection at all. Not until the bank has been told the authority is withdrawn.

Through DataClinic, Pebblous answers how good a body of AI training data is. An answer of that kind holds together while someone can still say which fence the material sits inside. The stretch Section H opens is the one past the fence, where what happens next appears in nobody's notice.

"Of the access we have handed out so far, how much have we told anyone we are taking back?"

The AI tools Citi built itself stay outside the provision. What divided the two was not how capable the assistant is but whether the bank can control the access. How long liability can rest on the side nobody controls looks like a question the regulation will answer before the terms do.
