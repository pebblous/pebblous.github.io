## LinkedIn (KO)

오픈AI가 10월 5일 챗GPT에 새 광고 자리를 열었다. 이용자가 그림을 만들어 달라고 하면 결과가 나오는 화면 옆에 광고가 붙는다.

시범 운영은 이달 안에 미국에서 일부 광고주를 상대로 시작한다. 챗GPT가 주마다 닿는 사람은 12억 명이다.

오픈AI가 그은 선은 또렷하다. 광고는 만들어지는 그림과 떨어져 있고, 답변 내용은 광고 때문에 달라지지 않으며, 적합성을 검증하는 외부 파트너는 비공개 대화를 열람하지 않는다.

그러면 광고 효과는 무엇으로 재는가. 발표의 나머지 절반이 그 답이다. 이름이 오른 측정 회사가 열여덟 곳인데, 이들이 하는 일은 광고를 누르는 순간 함께 넘어간 표식을 광고주 쪽 구매까지 따라가 두 사건을 잇는 것이다. 무엇을 그려 달라고 적었는지는 이 과정 어디에도 들어가지 않는다. 대화 없이도 측정은 된다.

대신 다른 것이 오간다. 개인정보 처리방침 개정을 다룬 보도에 따르면 오픈AI는 광고주로부터 구매 기록을 받고, 쿠키 번호와 기기 번호 같은 식별자를 마케팅 업체와 공유한다. 대화는 머물고 번호가 움직인다. "대화를 광고주에게 넘기지 않는다"와 "아무 데이터도 오가지 않는다"는 다른 말이다.

빈칸은 다른 쪽에 있다. 더블베리파이와 인터그럴 애드 사이언스가 광고 붙일 자리를 검증하기로 했는데, 대화를 보지 않는 검증사가 그러면 무엇을 보는지는 발표문에 적혀 있지 않다. 더블베리파이 최고경영자 본인이 챗GPT에서는 맥락이 페이지나 영상이 아니라 대화 자체로 정의된다고 말했는데도 그렇다. 귀인 업체 명단에 오른 DV록커복스가 더블베리파이 계열이니, 성과를 집계하는 쪽과 자리를 검증하는 쪽에 같은 이름이 들어가 있기도 하다.

광고가 붙을 맥락은 그동안 페이지였고 검색어였고 영상이었다. 이번에 하나가 늘었다. 사람이 무엇을 그려 달라고 적었는가다. 검색어는 이미 있는 것을 찾는 말이고 그림 주문은 아직 없는 것을 만들어 달라는 말이라, 구매 의도에 더 가깝게 읽힌다.

그래서 데이터를 다루는 쪽에 남는 질문은 대화가 넘어가느냐가 아니다. 그 신호를 누가 보관하고 누가 열람하느냐다. 보관 기한도 열람 권한도 발표문과 도움말 어디에도 없다. 페블러스가 AI-Ready Data를 말할 때 데이터의 출처와 경로를 먼저 묻는 이유가 여기에 있다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

OpenAI opened a new advertising slot in ChatGPT on October 5. A user asks for a picture, and an ad appears beside the screen where the result arrives.

The trial starts later this month in the United States with an initial group of advertisers. ChatGPT reaches 1.2 billion people each week.

The line OpenAI drew is explicit. Ads stay separate from the image being created, answers do not change because of an ad, and the outside partners verifying suitability do not read private conversations.

So what measures the ads? The other half of the announcement is the answer. Eighteen measurement companies were named, and what they do is follow an identifier that travels with the click into the advertiser's app or website, then join that click to whatever purchase happens there. Nothing a user typed as a picture request enters the work. The measurement runs without the conversation.

Something else moves instead. Reporting on the privacy policy update says OpenAI receives purchase records from advertisers and shares limited identifiers such as cookie IDs and device IDs with marketing partners. The conversation stays and the numbers travel. "We do not hand conversations to advertisers" and "no data moves at all" are different statements.

The empty box is on the other side. DoubleVerify and Integral Ad Science will verify where the ads can sit, and the post never says what a verifier that does not read the conversation reads instead. DoubleVerify's own chief executive said that in ChatGPT, context is defined by the conversation rather than a page or a video. Among the ten attribution vendors, DV Rockerbox belongs to DoubleVerify, so the same corporate name sits on the side that tallies performance and the side that checks placement.

The context an ad attaches to has been a page, a search term, a video. One more joins the list now. It is what a person typed when they asked for a picture. A search term looks for something that already exists, while a picture request asks for something that does not exist yet, which is why it reads as closer to purchase intent.

So the question this leaves for anyone handling data is not whether conversations are handed over. It is who keeps this new signal and who gets to read it. Neither the announcement nor the help page gives a retention period or an access rule. That is why Pebblous asks about the origin and the route of data first when it talks about AI-Ready Data.

## Facebook (EN)

Have you ever typed something like "draw me a two-seat sofa for the living room, Nordic style" into ChatGPT?

That line is not a search term. A search term looks for something that already exists. A picture request asks for something that does not exist yet.

On October 5, OpenAI said an ad will now appear beside the screen where the result of that request arrives. The trial starts in the United States later this month.

The promises in the post are stated plainly. The ad stays separate from the image being created, and the outside partners verifying suitability do not read private conversations.

But reading the promises all the way through left one question standing.

"If the conversation never leaves, what measures the ad?"

An identifier does. It travels with the click, follows the user into the advertiser's app, and joins the click to the purchase that happens there. Nothing anyone typed as a picture request enters that work. What does move is the other way around: purchase records come in to OpenAI from advertisers, and cookie IDs and device IDs go out to marketing partners. The conversation stays and the numbers travel.

I have started thinking of what appeared in that slot as the picture-request signal. Not a page, not a search term, not a video, but a sentence someone wrote about a thing that does not exist yet. And it has been placed about as close to purchase intent as a sentence can get.

"Who keeps this signal, and who gets to read it?"

What has been published is the deadline that applies once a user asks for deletion: gone from the servers within 30 days. How long data that nobody deletes is kept, and who inside the company opens it, is not in the same document.

Origin and route are what Pebblous asks about before quality when it diagnoses a dataset. Without a written record of where something came from, whose hands it passed through and where it went, one sentence is enough to settle the matter: we do not hand conversations over.

That sentence may well be true. The questions it cannot answer stay open anyway.

Asking for one picture of a sofa had quietly become the doorway to those questions.
