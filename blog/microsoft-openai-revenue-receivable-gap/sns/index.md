## LinkedIn (KO)

10월 28일 마이크로소프트 공시에서 머리기사가 된 숫자는 오픈AI와의 거래로 벌어들인 100억 달러였다. 같은 서류의 다른 줄에는 그 상대에게서 아직 받지 못한 72억 달러가 적혀 있었다.

두 수를 빼서 실제로 손에 쥔 돈이 28억 달러쯤이라고 읽은 글이 빠르게 돌았다. 그렇게 빼는 짝이 아니다. 100억 달러는 석 달 동안 쌓인 양이고, 72억 달러는 분기 마지막 날 하루에 남아 있던 잔액이다. 뒤쪽에는 이번 분기 이전에 생긴 몫도 들어 있다.

남는 사실은 간격이다. 6월 말 60억 달러였던 잔액이 석 달 만에 72억 달러가 됐다. 넘기는 속도가 받는 속도보다 빨라졌다는 뜻으로 읽을 수 있지만, 결제 조건이 길어진 것만으로도 겉모습은 똑같다. 연체된 금액도 결제 조건도 공개되지 않아 지금은 둘을 가릴 수 없다.

가장 널리 퍼진 11%라는 비율도 회사가 적은 값이 아니다. 실적 발표에서 골드만삭스 애널리스트가 분기 매출 923억 달러와 100억 달러를 견주어 계산해 되물은 값이고, 사티아 나델라 최고경영자는 그 비율을 받아 적지 않았다.

이 거래에서 마이크로소프트의 매출은 오픈AI 쪽에서 보면 비용이다. 칩 회사와 클라우드 회사와 모델 회사의 매출을 차례로 더해 AI 시장 규모를 만들면, 각 회사의 숫자가 하나도 틀리지 않아도 합계만 부풀어 오른다. 페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 정량으로 진단한다. 한 값이 경로를 달리해 두 번 들어와 있는지는 열의 자료형만 봐서는 드러나지 않는다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

The number that led Microsoft's October 28 filing was the $10 billion it earned from its commercial dealings with OpenAI in a single quarter. A different line on the same filing records $7.2 billion that OpenAI has not yet paid.

A reading spread quickly that subtracts one from the other and leaves about $2.8 billion in hand. They were never a pair that subtracts. The $10 billion accumulated across three months, while the $7.2 billion is the balance standing on the final day of the quarter, and it includes amounts that arose before this quarter began.

What does remain is the gap. The balance was $6 billion at the end of June. Services going out faster than payments come back would produce that, and so would a billing cycle that simply grew longer. Neither the overdue amount nor the payment terms has been disclosed, so the two cannot be told apart.

The 11% that traveled furthest was not written by the company either. A Goldman Sachs analyst worked the ratio out on the earnings call by setting $10 billion against quarterly revenue of $92.3 billion, and Satya Nadella, the chief executive, declined to adopt it.

Microsoft's revenue in this arrangement is, from OpenAI's side, a cost. Add up the revenue of chip companies and cloud companies and model companies in turn to size the AI market, and the total swells while not one company's number is wrong. ## Facebook (EN)

"They earned $10 billion and never collected $7.2 billion."

That sentence went past me several times over two days.

Subtract, and $2.8 billion is left. It is tidy, and tidy numbers are easy to believe.

The two do not subtract.

The $10 billion accumulated from July through September. The $7.2 billion is what was still outstanding on the single day of September 30. It is adding up three months of invoices and then taking away the height of the unpaid pile on today's desk.

The same unit. A different ruler.

Both are written in dollars. That is what puts the subtraction so close to hand.

What does remain is this. The balance stood at $6 billion at the end of June, and three months later it was $7.2 billion. The gap widened, and the public record does not settle whether OpenAI is slow to pay or the billing cycle simply grew longer.

The place I have met this structure most often is the weekly metrics email.

"New sign-ups this week: 1,200" and "Subscribers: 40,000" arrive on one line. Both are numbers and both are counts of people. The first piled up over seven days, the second is the balance on the morning the email went out, and nothing in the table marks that difference. A query that subtracts or divides the two runs without a warning.

"Is this column measuring a period, or a single day?"
