## LinkedIn (KO)

라벨링 도구를 팔던 스노클AI가 학습 데이터를 직접 만들어 팔기 시작하자, 회사 값이 13억 달러에서 35억 달러로 올랐다.

스노클AI가 시리즈 E로 3억 5,000만 달러를 받았다. 이 회사는 2019년 창업 뒤 고객의 데이터에 규칙과 모델로 라벨을 자동으로 붙여 주는 소프트웨어를 팔았다. 데이터를 만드는 일은 고객 몫으로 남고, 그 일을 빠르게 해 주는 연장을 파는 구조였다.

2025년 9월부터 파는 물건이 바뀌었다. 전문가가 손을 댄 완성된 데이터셋, 모델을 훈련시킬 강화학습 환경, 모델의 실력을 확인할 평가 과제를 회사가 직접 만들어 납품한다. 라이선스를 팔 때는 몇 명이 쓰느냐가 값을 정하지만, 데이터셋을 팔 때는 그 데이터가 얼마나 어렵고 얼마나 많으냐가 정한다. 매출이 가파르게 오른 시점도 파는 단위를 바꾼 때와 겹친다.

창업자 알렉스 랫너는 이 변화에 이름을 붙였다. 물량과 인력 배치의 문제였던 앞 단계가 데이터 1.0이고, 어려운 문제를 정확히 겨냥한 데이터를 만드는 연구와 기술의 문제가 데이터 2.0이다. 사람의 자리는 작성에서 검수와 판정으로 옮겨 간다.

회사가 내건 연환산 매출 3억 7,500만 달러는 12개월 동안 들어온 돈이 아니라 최근 실적을 1년 치로 늘린 값이다. 1년이 안 돼 18배가 됐다는데, 로이터가 전한 1년 전 수치는 약 2,000만 달러다. 머코어나 핸드셰이크의 매출은 전문가에게 나가는 60~70%를 빼기 전의 총액이라 바로 견줄 수 없다.

페블러스가 이 소식을 눈여겨본 이유는 랫너의 논리에 있다. 모자란 데이터를 정확히 겨냥해 합성하는 일은 페블러스가 2021년 창업 때 붙든 첫 과제였다. 데이터클리닉으로 데이터의 빈자리를 찾고, 데이터 그린하우스에서 진단과 합성, 재진단의 고리를 돌린다. 페블러스는 아직 데이터를 만들어 팔지 않지만 꾸는 꿈은 닮았다. 데이터를 수렵하지 않는다. 데이터를 경작한다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Snorkel AI used to sell software for labeling data. Once it began building training data itself and selling that, its valuation went from $1.3 billion to $3.5 billion.

Snorkel AI has raised a $350 million Series E. Since its founding in 2019 the product had been software: customers used rules and models to label their own data automatically. Building the data stayed the customer's job, and Snorkel sold the tool that made the job faster.

Since September 2025 the thing being sold has changed. Snorkel now builds and delivers finished datasets that experts have worked over, reinforcement learning environments for training models, and evaluation tasks for checking what a model can do. Sell a license and the invoice is set by how many people use it; sell datasets and it is set by how hard the data is and how much of it there is. The steep climb in revenue coincides with that change in the unit of sale.

Chief executive Alex Ratner has a name for the shift. Data 1.0 was a problem of volume, staffing and logistics. Data 2.0 is a research and technology problem: building data aimed precisely at the hard problems. People move from authoring toward review and adjudication.

The $375 million is an annualized run rate, a recent stretch projected out to a year rather than twelve months of receipts. The company says it grew more than eighteenfold in under a year, and Reuters put the run rate a year earlier at about $20 million. Peers such as Mercor and Handshake report gross figures before the 60 to 70 percent paid out to experts, so they do not compare directly.

Pebblous paid attention because of Ratner's logic. Synthesizing precisely the data a model is missing was Pebblous's first project when it was founded in 2021. DataClinic finds the gaps in data, and the Data Greenhouse runs a loop of diagnosis, synthesis and re-diagnosis. Pebblous does not yet sell data it makes, but the dream is similar: we do not hunt for data, we cultivate it.

## Facebook (EN)

There is a line I went looking for in this year's budget and could not find.

Inference costs had a line. Training runs had a line.

Gathering the data those models feed on, cleaning it, deciding what the right answer even is: that had no line anywhere.

It was not missing. It was scattered, sitting quietly inside a few people's working weeks.

Last week I watched a price tag get attached to that work.

Snorkel AI announced a $350 million Series E on September 22, at a valuation of $3.5 billion, close to three times what the company carried in May 2025.

For years what this company sold was software that put labels on your data automatically. That is selling the tool. What it sells now is finished datasets and reinforcement learning environments, built and delivered by the company itself.

The tool gave way to the judgment.

Alex Ratner, who founded it, has named the boundary. The earlier phase, Data 1.0, was about gathering cheap data in volume, a staffing and logistics problem. The later one, Data 2.0, is about making difficult data properly, which he calls a research and technology problem.

The numbers still need care. The revenue figure is an annualized run rate, a recent stretch projected out to a year rather than money that arrived over twelve months, and the eighteenfold growth starts from a correspondingly small base. What stayed with me was not the multiple. It was that the boundary had moved.

At Pebblous we ask what standard a value was judged against before we ask whether it is internally consistent, and the reason sits in the same place. As long as the criteria survive, and the people who can set them, preparing data stays something you can put a price on rather than something you absorb.

So the question the announcement left me with was not really about Snorkel.

"Where does our own data work stall: at not enough hands, or at not knowing what the right answer is?"

If it is hands, adding people fixes it. If it is the answer, ten times the people will not.

Telling the two apart is not hard. Watch how often two reviewers looking at the same record disagree.

We are going to start by rebuilding that missing line in our own budget.
