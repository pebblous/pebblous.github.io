## LinkedIn (KO)

고용노동부가 'AI 시대 새로운 사회계약 녹서'에 담을 주제를 국민에게서 직접 받는다. 11월 12일 발표였고, 이르면 다음 주부터 약 3주간이다.

녹서는 정부가 결론을 내기 전에 쟁점을 정리해 사회에 내놓는 문서다. 결론과 정책 방향을 담은 백서의 앞 단계에 선다. 노사 단체와 청년·비정규직 조직, 전문가까지 23명이 9월 9일부터 격주로 모여 왔고, 연말에 나올 결과물은 정책이 아니라 주제별 질문을 묶은 목록이다.

답을 뒤로 미루는 데에는 사정이 하나 겹쳐 있다. 기업이 경영상 이유로 한꺼번에 사람을 내보낼 때 노동부에 내는 신고서가 묻는 항목은 넷이다. 해고 사유, 예정 인원, 근로자대표와 협의한 내용, 해고 일정. 사유 칸에 들어가는 것은 경영 악화 같은 결과이고, 그 결과를 부른 기술을 따로 가릴 항목은 서식에 없다.

칸을 먼저 만들어 본 곳의 기록이 남아 있다. 뉴욕주는 2025년 3월 대량 해고 신고 서식에 기술·자동화 선택지를 더했는데, 1년 동안 162개 기업이 750건 넘게 신고하면서 그 선택지를 고른 곳은 한 곳도 없었다. 현지 법률가들이 드는 이유는 기술이 아니라 장치의 설계다. 법은 그대로 두고 서식만 고쳐 의무인지 선택인지가 분명하지 않았고, 제재는 신고를 통째로 빠뜨렸을 때만 걸렸다. 캘리포니아 SB 951은 적는 칸과 분기별 공표를 한 법에 함께 적었다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Korea's labor ministry wants questions before it writes any answers. On November 12 it said the public would get a window of roughly three weeks, opening as soon as the week after, to say what a green paper on a new social contract for the AI era ought to cover.

A green paper lays out the issues a government has not resolved and puts them back to the people living with them, one step ahead of the white paper that carries a decision. The panel behind this one holds 23 seats: the two union federations, employer groups, bodies speaking for young and non-regular workers, and 12 academics. It has met every second week since September 9, and what lands at year-end is a themed set of questions rather than a position.

There is a practical reason the answers can wait. A Korean employer filing ahead of a mass layoff fills in four items. Why. How many people. What the consultation with the workers' representative covered. By when. The first item takes an outcome, a downturn or a shrinking line of business, and which technology produced that outcome has no item of its own.

One state has a full year of returns from building that item. New York attached a question about technology and automation to its mass layoff system in March 2025; twelve months on, 162 employers had lodged over 750 notices and none had ticked it. Lawyers there blame the instrument rather than the technology. The form moved and the statute did not, so whether the item is compulsory remains unclear, and the penalty bites only on a filing that never arrives. California wrote it the other way. SB 951, in force from January 2027, places the item and the quarterly publication of whatever lands in it inside a single statute.

A fact with no field to sit in leaves nothing behind at all, and an absent value never registers as an error. Which questions the green paper selects is what decides the counting that follows.

## Facebook (EN)

What does the paperwork say on the day someone leaves a job?

A downturn. A contraction of operations. Usually something along those lines.

When a company in Korea lets a large group go for business reasons, the filing it sends to the labor ministry asks four things. Why. How many. What was discussed with the workers' representative. By when.

The reason field holds an outcome. Nowhere on the page is there a line for whatever produced it.

New York tried drawing that line. In March 2025 the state put a question about technology and automation onto its mass layoff system. A year on, 162 employers had sent in more than 750 notices, and not one had ticked it.

So there are two conditions now, and they are not the same. New York has a box that stays empty. Korea has no box.

An empty box is still a count. Lawyers in the state could point at the design, at a form that changed while the statute stayed put, at a penalty that reaches only an employer who skips the filing, because there was something to point at. A box that was never drawn leaves nothing behind.

On November 12 the Ministry of Employment and Labor in Seoul said the public would have roughly three weeks, opening as soon as the week after, to say what belongs in its green paper on a new social contract for the AI era. What arrives at year-end is a list of questions, not a policy.

Pebblous puts a number on the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. Measure quality for long enough and you learn which defects surface last. A wrong value announces itself eventually. A fact with nowhere to be written never does, because an absent value is not an error.

"Which statistic is picking up what happened to my job?"

That is a question worth sending during the three weeks. Whether the year-end list makes room for it changes what exists as a number after that.
