## LinkedIn (KO)

10월 18일 앤트로픽이 회사 지분 5%를 공익 기금에 넣겠다고 발표했습니다. 쓰임새로는 AI가 노동시장에 미칠 충격에 대한 대응이 거론됐습니다. 그 돈을 받을 사람을 무엇으로 가리는지는 적히지 않았습니다.

기금은 별도의 비영리 재단이 아니라 앤트로픽이 100% 소유하는 LLC입니다. 지분 이전은 주주 승인을 받아야 효력이 생깁니다. 여기에 기업가치가 기준선 대비 50% 오를 때마다 이사회 재량으로 2%를 더 넣는 조건이 붙어 있습니다. 그 기준선이 얼마인지, 기금을 누가 운영하는지, 받을 사람을 어떻게 가리는지는 앞으로 몇 달 안에 밝히겠다고만 적혔습니다.

일론 머스크와 데이비드 색스가 곧바로 내놓은 반응도 액수가 아니라 정하는 절차를 겨눴습니다. 다만 비판은 누가 결정하느냐에서 멈췄습니다. 그 결정에 쓸 자료가 어디서 나오는지는 묻지 않았습니다.

앤트로픽은 이미 경제 지수로 AI와 직업의 겹침을 집계해 왔습니다. 그런데 그 지수는 어떤 직군의 과업이 모델 사용과 겹치는지까지만 보여 줍니다. 자사 측정 논문도 노출도가 높은 직군에서 실업률이 올라간 흔적은 찾지 못했다고 적었습니다. 지금까지 또렷하게 잡힌 변화는 해고가 아니라 줄어든 채용입니다. 채용되지 않은 사람은 어느 실직자 명부에도 오르지 않습니다.

기금이 사람을 골라 돈을 주는 쪽을 택하면, 돈을 내는 쪽이 누가 피해자인지 가리는 자리에도 같이 서게 됩니다. 페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 정량 진단합니다. 측정에 늘 따라붙는 물음은 그것을 만든 쪽 바깥에서 결과를 다시 볼 수 있느냐입니다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Anthropic said on October 18 that it will commit 5% of its shares to a public benefit fund. Responding to the labor market impact of AI was named among the uses. How the fund will pick the people who receive the money was not.

The fund is not a separate nonprofit but an LLC wholly owned by Anthropic. The transfer takes effect only with shareholder approval, and a further 2% is added at the board's discretion each time the valuation rises 50% over a baseline. What that baseline is, who runs the fund, and what criteria sort recipients are all described only as things the company will publish in the next few months.

Elon Musk and David Sacks both responded as soon as the announcement went up, and neither objected to the amount. Both objected to who decides. Neither asked where the data behind that decision would come from.

Anthropic already compiles an economic index that maps conversations with its own models onto occupational tasks. It shows which jobs overlap with AI use. It cannot say that a particular person lost work because of AI. The company's own measurement paper found no unemployment effect in the most exposed occupations, and the clearest change so far has run through hiring rather than layoffs. Someone who was never hired appears on no unemployment roll.

If the fund pays individuals, the party writing the checks also becomes the party deciding who counts as harmed. Pebblous verifies the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. The question that follows any measurement is whether anyone outside the party that built it can check the result.

## Facebook (EN)

Have you ever sent in an application and waited?

A rejection at least arrives. Most of the time nothing arrives at all.

In the revision the Stanford Digital Economy Lab published this summer, there was one finding I went back to several times.

Employment for people aged twenty-two to twenty-five had fallen 19% below that of their less exposed peers, and the gap had opened through hiring rather than layoffs.

A person who is laid off leaves a record. A name goes into the company's reason field and onto an unemployment claim.

A person who never hears back leaves nothing anywhere.

On October 18 Anthropic said it would put 5% of its shares into a public benefit fund. Among the uses it listed was responding to the labor market impact of AI. The size of the money was written as a number. How the fund will pick the people who receive it was described only as something the company will publish in the next few months.

There is no settled word for these people. For now I write it down as "the unlisted." Someone clearly pushed out, whose being pushed out was entered in no ledger anywhere.

"A sentence saying this person lost work because of AI: who writes it, and from what?"

Anthropic has been building an economic index from the conversations that arrive at its own models. It shows which occupations overlap with AI use. It cannot determine whether one particular person lost work for that reason. Public employment statistics cannot either, and neither can private layoff trackers.

Pebblous also works on putting the quality of AI training data into numbers. What that work keeps running into is the moment when the party that builds a measure is also the party whose gains and losses ride on it. From then on, a correct result is still hard to believe.

The precondition for this fund may be a ledger rather than money.

Who builds that ledger, and who checks it, is what I would look for ahead of the amount when the document arrives.
