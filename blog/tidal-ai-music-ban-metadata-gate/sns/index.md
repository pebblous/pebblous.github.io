타이달이 12월 9일부터 AI가 만든 곡을 지운다. 연말까지 2,900만 곡이 넘는데, 어떤 곡이 AI 생성인지는 곡을 올린 유통사가 신고란에 적어 낸 값으로 가린다.

블록이 소유한 이 음악 스트리밍 서비스는 11월 24일 완전 AI 생성곡의 유통 자체를 금지한다고 밝혔다. 카탈로그에서 완전 AI 생성으로 분류된 곡은 2,700만 곡, 전체의 6.3%다. 그중 96%는 재생 기록이 한 번도 없다. 삭제가 끝난 뒤에도 하루 8만 곡 안팎이 접수 창구에서 되돌아갈 것으로 회사는 내다본다.

테크크런치가 전한 거부 기준은 한 줄이다. 곡의 메타데이터에 완전히 AI로 만들었다고 적혀 있으면 받지 않는다. 소리를 분석한다는 설명은 1차 보도 어디에도 없다. 거부 여부를 정하는 값을 거부당할 쪽이 스스로 적는 셈이다.

가장 걸러 내고 싶은 쪽일수록 신고란에 정직할 이유가 없다. 프랑스의 데저가 신고 대신 소리에서 합성 신호를 찾는 쪽으로 간 까닭이 여기에 있다. 데저가 공개한 수치를 보면 완전 AI 생성곡이 만든 재생은 전체의 1~3%에 그치는데, 그 재생 가운데 2025년 월 최대 85%가 사기로 판정됐다. 카탈로그 전체로 넓히면 같은 해 8%다.

타이달도 6월에 완전 AI 생성곡에 표시를 붙이고 수익 지급을 끊은 적이 있다. 그런데도 최근 90일 업로드의 43% 이상이 AI 생성으로 탐지됐다. 돈을 막아도 유입이 줄지 않았다는 사실이 이번 삭제의 전제다.

라벨이 없는 데이터 더미에서 AI가 만든 것을 가려내야 하는 조직이라면, 이 소식이 음악 업계 뉴스로만 읽히지는 않을 것이다. 지금 쓰는 라벨이 누군가 적어 넣은 신고인지 시스템이 남긴 측정인지, 그 값이 비어 있을 때 기본값이 통과인지 보류인지가 관문의 실제 성능을 정한다. 페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 정량 진단한다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Tidal starts deleting AI-generated music on 9 December, and more than 29 million tracks will be gone by the end of the year. What marks a track as AI-made is whatever its distributor wrote in the filing field.

The streaming service, owned by Block, said on 24 November that it will stop accepting fully AI-generated tracks and will bar distributors from delivering them at all. Of the catalogue, 27 million tracks, or 6.3 percent, carry that classification, and 96 percent of those have never been played once. After the purge the company expects to turn away some 80,000 tracks a day at intake.

The rejection rule, as TechCrunch reported it, runs to a single line: a track is refused if its metadata says it was made fully using AI. Nothing in the primary coverage describes audio analysis. The value that decides admission is entered by the party seeking admission.

The uploaders with the most to lose have the least reason to fill that field in honestly. Deezer went the other way and looks for synthetic signatures in the audio. Its figures suggest why: fully AI-generated tracks account for only 1 to 3 percent of streams there, yet in the peak month of 2025 up to 85 percent of those streams were judged fraudulent, against 8 percent catalogue-wide.

Tidal had already tagged such tracks and cut their payouts back in June. Even so, more than 43 percent of uploads over the past 90 days were detected as AI-generated. Closing the money did not close the tap, and that is the premise of this deletion.

For anyone holding an unlabelled pile of data, this is not only a music story. ## Facebook (EN)

There is a field on the upload form that asks whether a track was made by AI.

From 9 December, Tidal deletes every track whose field says it was made fully by AI. More than 29 million of them before the year is out.

Days later, what I still had in mind was not the count of deleted tracks. It was the field.

The value that decides whether a track is accepted is written by the person asking for it to be accepted.

Nothing in the reporting says Tidal listens to the audio. With roughly 80,000 tracks arriving a day, nobody is listening to them one at a time either.

A songwriter with nothing to hide ticks the box honestly, and that track is the one caught.

Someone flooding the service to farm royalties leaves the box empty. Leaving it empty costs nothing.

That may be close to why Deezer went looking in the audio instead. By Deezer's own numbers, fully AI-generated tracks make up only 1 to 3 percent of streams, and yet in the worst month of 2025 up to 85 percent of those streams were judged fraudulent.

Move the scene into the documents and images piling up inside a company, and the question gets short.

"Who filled in the field that says a person wrote this?"

A value someone typed and a value a system recorded sit in the same column, and after a while nothing separates them. A blank one usually drifts through as a pass. Diagnosing data quality, Pebblous keeps stopping in front of that column.

Tidal could classify 27 million tracks at all only because it attached labels back in June.

Deleting can wait. Writing down what came in does not seem to have a later.
