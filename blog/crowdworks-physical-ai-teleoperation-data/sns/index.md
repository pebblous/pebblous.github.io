## LinkedIn (KO)

크라우드웍스가 10월 7일 코엑스 'AI 페스타 2026' 부스에 올린 것은 완성된 로봇이 아니라 데이터가 만들어지는 공정이었다. 작업자가 VR 장비를 착용하고 로봇을 원격으로 조종하면, 그 움직임이 기록되어 학습 데이터로 가공된다.

제품의 이름은 '피지컬 AI 데이터 플랫폼'이다. 들어온 기록을 그대로 쓰지는 않는다. 회사는 모은 행동을 태스크와 시나리오, 서브태스크, 액션의 네 단계로 쪼갠다고 밝혔다. 무작위로 쌓인 조작 기록을 파운데이션 모델이 바로 학습할 수 있는 형태로 바꾸기 위한 위계다. 데이터가 나오는 경로도 하나가 아니다. 로봇이 직접 남기는 기록과 시뮬레이션에서 만들어지는 데이터가 있고, 부스에서 보여 준 원격 조종이 사람 쪽에 해당한다.

데이터가 실제로 쌓이는 곳은 LG전자가 8월 서울 양재에 연 로봇 데이터팩토리다. 과학기술정보통신부와 정보통신기획평가원이 추진하고 LG전자가 주관하는 월드 파운데이션 모델 과제에서 크라우드웍스가 데이터 파이프라인을 맡았다. LG전자는 연내 로봇 학습용 데이터 10만 시간을 확보하겠다고 밝혔다. 쉬지 않고 기록해도 약 12년치에 해당하는 분량이다.

같은 발표에 숫자가 하나 더 있었다. 데이터 작업자 67만 명, 누적 미션 데이터셋 3억 건. 로봇 데이터 플랫폼을 소개하는 문단 옆에 놓였지만 이 지표가 세고 있는 것은 창업 이래 회사가 해 온 작업 전체다. 이미지 라벨링도 텍스트 주석도 여기 들어간다. 회사가 거짓을 말한 것은 아니다. 숫자가 틀린 것이 아니라 분모가 적히지 않았다.

이 데이터를 만드는 일의 삯은 따로 조사된 적이 있다. 한국데이터경제신문 심층리포트는 국내 데이터 라벨러의 평균 실질 시급을 9,860원으로 적었고, 이는 올해 법정 최저임금에 못 미친다. 크라우드웍스가 밝힌 수치가 아니라 라벨링 업계 전반을 조사한 값이고, 로봇을 조종하는 일과 화면 앞에서 주석을 다는 일은 작업 성격도 다르다.

발표 수치를 받아 적을 때 던질 질문은 크기가 아니라 범위다. 이 값은 무엇을 합산했고 어느 시점부터 세었는가. 그리고 누가 어떤 기준으로 만들었는가. 로봇 행동 데이터는 사람이 조종한 움직임이 그대로 모델의 기준선이 되기 때문에 마지막 질문이 특히 무겁다. 페블러스가 AI 학습 데이터를 진단할 때 건수보다 범위를 먼저 묻는 이유도 여기에 있다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

What CrowdWorks put in its booth at AI Festa 2026 in Seoul on October 7 was not a finished robot but a collection process. A worker wears VR equipment and operates a robot by remote control, and the resulting movement is recorded and processed into training data.

The product is called the Physical AI Data Platform. The raw recordings are not used as they arrive. The company says it splits each captured behavior into four levels, from the task down through the scenario and subtask to the individual action, so that a foundation model can learn from recordings that would otherwise be unstructured. Nor does the data come from one place. Some of it is what the robots themselves leave behind, some is generated in simulation, and the teleoperation on display is the part that comes from people.

The data itself accumulates at the robot data factory LG Electronics opened in Yangjae, Seoul, in August. Under a world foundation model program run by Korea's science ministry and IITP and led by LG Electronics, CrowdWorks builds the data pipeline. LG has said it aims to secure 100,000 hours of robot training data by the end of the year, which is roughly twelve years of around-the-clock recording.

One more figure appeared in the same announcement: 670,000 data workers and 300 million cumulative mission datasets. It sat beside the paragraph introducing the robot platform, but what the metric counts is every job the company has done since it was founded, image labeling and text annotation included. Nothing about it is false. The number is not wrong; the denominator simply was not written down.

What this kind of work pays has been surveyed separately. An in-depth report by Korea Data Economy News put the average effective hourly wage for data labelers in Korea at 9,860 won, below this year's statutory minimum. That is an industry-wide figure rather than one CrowdWorks disclosed, and steering a robot is not the same job as drawing boxes on a screen.

The question to ask of an announced figure is its scope rather than its size. What was added together, and when did the counting start. Then: who produced it, and against what standard. Robot behavior data makes that last question heavy, because a human operator's movement becomes the model's baseline directly. It is why Pebblous asks about scope before volume when it diagnoses AI training data.

## Facebook (EN)

A robot on the stand, and beside it a person wearing VR equipment.

The person moves an arm and the robot makes the same movement. That movement is recorded, and it becomes training data for the robot.

This was CrowdWorks at AI Festa 2026 in Seoul on October 7. What the company put on display was not a finished robot but the process by which the data gets made.

We hear often that robots are learning on their own. The first half of that sentence has a person in it, holding the controls and repeating the same motion again and again.

The data accumulates at the robot data factory LG Electronics opened in Yangjae, Seoul, in August. In a space built to look like a home, the robot cleans. In one built to match a washing machine plant, it carries parts, stacks them and assembles them. LG's target for the year is 100,000 hours of robot training data, which is about twelve years of around-the-clock recording.

One more figure came with the announcement: 670,000 data workers, 300 million cumulative mission datasets.

It sat inside a paragraph about the robot platform, but what it counts is every job the company has done since 2017. Image labeling, text annotation, all of it. Nothing about this is dishonest. The sentence began with "to date," and the company never hid that the total was company-wide. It simply never said what had been added together.

I would like to give figures like that a name: numbers with the scope left blank.

The count is exact; what the count covers is missing. A wrong value gets caught in review. A value with no scope passes review and goes on to be misread.

"Is this count in my table what we collected this time, or everything we hold?"

That is why we ask teams at Pebblous to record the scope and the time of measurement alongside the count itself.

Robot behavior data carries one more question. The operator's movement becomes the model's baseline directly, so the skill and the working conditions of whoever held the controls are carried into the quality of the data. Whether the shape of that person's day ever makes it into the metrics is another matter.
