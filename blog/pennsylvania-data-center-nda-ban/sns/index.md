하루 뒤 스포트라이트 PA가 정보공개청구로 확인한 서명 문서 최소 여덟 건을 공개했습니다. 읍 감독관과 카운티 위원장, 주 정부 내각 장관이 아마존웹서비스·밴티지·QTS와 맺은 계약이었고, 그중 한 건에서 실제로 비밀로 지켜진 정보는 최종 이용자가 아마존이라는 사실 하나였습니다.

더 오래 남을 조항은 금지 조항 옆에 붙어 있습니다. 같은 행정명령이 운영사에 전년도 에너지와 용수 사용량, 피크 시간대 사용량, 최대 일수요를 해마다 제출하도록 했습니다. 금지는 한 번의 사건으로 끝나지만, 같은 항목을 같은 서식으로 여러 해 여러 사업장이 제출하면 그것은 문서가 아니라 시계열이 됩니다.

다만 행정명령이 정한 것은 제출 의무이고, 제출된 자료를 어떤 형식과 주기로 공개할지는 본문에 없습니다. 법률이 아니어서 다음 행정부가 되돌릴 수도 있습니다. 페블러스가 데이터 품질을 진단할 때 반복해서 보는 장면도 비슷합니다. 값이 틀린 경우보다 같은 항목을 부서마다 다른 서식으로 적어 둔 탓에 비교가 안 되는 경우가 훨씬 흔합니다.

8.4기가와트 1단계와 550조원을 앞에 두고 시행령을 쓰고 있는 한국에도 같은 질문이 남습니다. 무엇을 금지할지가 아니라, 무엇을 해마다 기록으로 남기게 할지입니다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

The regulation went after neither the power nor the water. It went after the fact that the negotiations were private.

Pennsylvania Governor Josh Shapiro signed Executive Order 2026-05 on August 18, stating that the use of nondisclosure agreements with data center projects is not permissible. A day later, Spotlight PA published at least eight signed NDAs obtained through public records requests. Township supervisors, a county commission chair, and a state cabinet secretary had signed with Amazon Web Services, Vantage, and QTS. In one of those cases, the only information actually kept confidential was that the end user would be Amazon.

The clause that will outlast the ban sits right beside it. The same order requires operators to file total energy and water consumption for the prior year, average hourly use at peak, and maximum day demand. A ban is a single event. A filing comes back every year, and when the same fields arrive in the same format from many sites over many years, the result stops being a document and becomes a time series.

The order stops short of the next step. It sets the duty to file, not the format or cadence for publishing what gets filed. And because it is an executive order rather than a statute, a future administration can undo it.

Pebblous sees a version of this in data quality work. Values that are simply wrong are less common than values recorded by each department in a different format, which is what makes them impossible to compare.

The same question is open in Korea, where a first phase of 8.4 gigawatts and 550 trillion won is underway and the enabling decree is being drafted right now. Not what to prohibit, but what to require on the record every year.

## Facebook (EN)

Sign the nondisclosure agreement first, and then we can meet.

That was the condition put to Craig High, a township supervisor in Anthony Township, Montour County. What the company wanted to build would be explained after the signature, not before.

High declined to sign, and the meeting never happened. Months passed before he learned what the project was. A data center.

The story ran in Spotlight PA on August 19. Public records requests had turned up at least eight signed agreements. Township supervisors, a county commission chair, and a state cabinet secretary had put their names down, and in one of those cases the only information actually kept confidential was that the end user would be Amazon.

One day earlier, on August 18, Governor Shapiro signed an executive order stating that nondisclosure agreements are not permissible in data center projects. The regulation reached past the power and the water and landed on the privacy of the negotiation itself.

The clause that will be read longer, though, sits next to that one.

Operators must file the prior year's energy and water consumption, down to average hourly use at peak.

A ban is a single event. A filing returns every year. When the same fields arrive in the same format across several years, they stop being paperwork and become something you can hold side by side.

Until now, each locality's terms were what I would call an unformatted promise. Written down somewhere, certainly, but never in a shape that lets you lay two of them next to each other.

This is close to what Pebblous keeps running into in data quality work. Values that are outright wrong are the smaller problem. The larger one is the same field recorded by each department in its own format, which quietly removes the possibility of comparison. Retrofitting a standard has always cost more than agreeing on the format at the start.

Korea is drafting the decree for a first phase of 8.4 gigawatts and 550 trillion won right now.

"What should be prohibited, and what should be written down every year?"

Without the first year on record, there is no baseline to compare against. The window for choosing a format is usually short.
