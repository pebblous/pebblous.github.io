유럽 자동차 업계의 데이터 체계에 국내 부품사를 올리겠다며 네 기관이 적어 낸 과제 넷 가운데, 설비를 들이거나 공장을 짓는 항목은 하나도 없다.

LG유플러스는 11월 11일 서울 용산 사옥에서 카테나-X, 코피니티-X, IBCT와 '한국 제조업 데이터 신뢰 생태계 구축 협약'을 맺었다고 13일 밝혔다. 카테나-X는 완성차 업체와 부품사가 탄소배출량과 부품 생산 이력을 공통 기준으로 주고받는 유럽발 체계다. 설계의 핵심은 데이터를 한곳에 모으지 않는다는 데 있다. 원본은 각 회사 서버에 남고, 상대와 항목과 기간을 걸어 필요한 부분만 그때그때 내준다.

시점을 정한 쪽은 규제다. 유럽연합 배터리 규정은 2027년 2월 18일부터 전기차 배터리에 디지털 여권을 붙이도록 한다. 세계에서 처음으로 의무가 되는 디지털 제품여권이다.

칸을 채우는 일이 왜 어려운지는 먼저 걸어 본 회사가 적어 뒀다. LG전자는 지금 유통되는 탄소발자국 수치 상당수가 생산 현장에서 나온 값이 아니라 업계 평균에 기댄 값이라고 공식 블로그에 썼다. 평균을 옮겨 적은 칸은 공정을 고쳐도 좀처럼 움직이지 않는다. 포드와 협력사 두 곳이 실제 측정값을 세 단계에 걸쳐 이어 붙인 시범 사업에서는, 그렇게 산정한 탄소발자국이 평균에 기댄 값보다 46% 낮게 나왔다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Four organizations signed a pact this week to put Korean parts makers inside Europe's automotive data ecosystem. The four tasks they wrote into it build no factory and buy no equipment.

LG Uplus said on November 13 that two days earlier, at its Yongsan headquarters in Seoul, it had signed the Korea Trusted Manufacturing Data Ecosystem Partnership with Catena-X, Cofinity-X and IBCT. Catena-X is the German-led standard under which carmakers and their suppliers pass each other figures such as carbon emissions and part production histories. It pools nothing. Every company keeps its originals on its own servers and releases named items to named counterparties for a fixed window.

The clock belongs to regulation. From February 18, 2027, the EU battery regulation requires a digital passport on electric vehicle batteries, the first digital product passport anywhere to carry the force of law.

Why those cells are hard to fill has already been set out by a company that got there earlier. LG Electronics said on its corporate blog that much of the carbon footprint data now in circulation is not measured on the production floor but borrowed from industry averages. A cell holding an average barely shifts when the process behind it improves. In a pilot where Ford and two of its suppliers carried measured values across three tiers, the footprint came out 46 percent below the average-based figure.

So the demanding part is not a good number. It is a number that still shifts when the factory behind it shifts. Pebblous inspects AI training data with DataClinic and attaches a figure to each item.

## Facebook (EN)

Go down to the second and third tiers of a supply chain and one person commonly carries quality, safety and carbon at the same desk.

In November another piece of news came down to that desk.

LG Uplus said on November 13 that it had signed a pact with Catena-X, the data exchange system of the European car industry, with Cofinity-X, which runs that system as a commercial service, and with IBCT, its Korean partner. The point of the signing was to get Korean parts makers onto the data side of the European supply chain.

The direction mattered to me more than the figures did.

A carmaker asks for carbon data once. The tier-one supplier receives the request, and from there it goes down to tier two and tier three. The asking happens once at the top. The answering spreads out along the chain.

Lee Jeong-ryun of IBCT has said that among the tens of thousands of second and third tier suppliers around Ulsan, Yangsan, Changwon and Busan, many do not know what a digital product passport is.

Europe decided the public purse should carry part of that cost. The support program running on 23 million euros from Germany's economics ministry is not a subsidy that refunds what a company spent. It pays a European automotive small or mid-sized firm up to 15,000 euros only after a real data exchange with a trading partner has been confirmed.

The money is attached to a handover that happened, not to a form that was filed.

"What tells us that the value we sent was read on the other side the way we wrote it?"

Pebblous unpacks AI training data with DataClinic. Requests for a diagnosis usually come from high up a chain, while the hands filling in the cells sit lower down. Supply chain data and training data are alike in that respect: the desk that sets the format and the desk that fills it are rarely the same desk.

The sender counts what was sent. Only the receiver knows what was read.

That may be why Europe hung its money on a completed exchange, not on the paperwork.
