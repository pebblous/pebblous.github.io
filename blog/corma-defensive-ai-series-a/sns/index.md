같은 범용 모델을 공격자 자리에 앉혔을 때와 방어자 자리에 앉혔을 때, 성적이 85%와 19%로 갈렸다. 숫자를 낸 쪽은 방어 전용 모델을 파는 회사다.

텔아비브와 샌프란시스코에 사무실을 둔 Corma가 11월 16일 시리즈 A를 마감했다고 밝혔다. NEA가 주도했고 세콰이아와 코슬라, 코아튜가 따라 들어왔다.

회사의 존재가 알려진 것은 올해 8월, 세콰이아가 이끈 시드 때다. 석 달 사이 기업 가치는 10억 달러가 됐고 누적 조달액은 1억 8,500만 달러가 됐다.

회사가 내세우는 논리는 둘이다. 뚫는 쪽은 여러 번 두드려 한 번만 열리면 되고, 막는 쪽은 그 모든 시도를 걸러야 겨우 본전이다. 그리고 양쪽이 읽는 자료가 다르다. 코드와 문서는 모델이 학습에서 가장 많이 본 종류의 글이지만, 방어가 종일 마주하는 감사 로그와 보안 이벤트는 산문도 코드도 아닌 표 모양의 기록이다.

85%와 19%는 같은 자에 찍힌 두 눈금이 아니다. 앞은 공격이 통한 비율, 뒤는 그 공격을 알아챈 비율이다. 실험을 설계하고 돌리고 공개한 주체는 모두 Corma이며, 회사가 올려 둔 원문 페이지는 기사를 쓰는 동안 열리지 않았다. 바깥에서 같은 설계를 다시 돌려 본 기록도 아직 보이지 않는다.

돈이 흐르는 방향은 아직 반대쪽이다. 모의 침투를 자동으로 돌리는 Horizon3.ai가 8월에 받은 2억 5,000만 달러는 Corma가 지금까지 모은 전부보다 많다.

페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 정량 진단한다. 방어 특화 모델이 읽을 자료는 결국 그 조직의 로그인데, 그 로그는 모델에게 읽히려고 남긴 기록이 아니다. 얼마나 오래 두는지는 저장 비용이 정하고, 한 사건을 적는 칸 이름은 장비를 바꿀 때마다 달라진다. 수집기가 멈춰 있던 동안은 아무 일도 없던 시간처럼 보인다.

모델은 올해 안에 고를 수 있다. 그 모델이 읽을 기록은 이미 몇 해치가 쌓인 뒤다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Three months after anyone outside had heard of it, a startup building AI models for defense and nothing else has been valued at a billion dollars.

On November 16 Corma said it had closed a $125 million Series A led by NEA, with Sequoia, Khosla and Coatue alongside. The company keeps offices in Tel Aviv and San Francisco, and a seed in August, its first public appearance, brings the two rounds to $185 million.

Corma's argument to investors begins with how the two sides are scored. An intruder can try many doors and needs one to give way, while the side guarding the building breaks even only by stopping every attempt. Code and documentation are what a model has read most of, while the defending side spends its day inside audit logs, security events and network traffic, which are neither prose nor code and take a thin slice of training material.

As evidence Corma published a study it ran itself. Four general-purpose models took turns as attacker and defender across 241 engagements on a fictional corporate network. Seated as the attacker, a model planted a backdoor 85% of the time. Seated as the defender, it noticed 19% of what reached it. The two percentages count different things rather than two ends of one scale: attacks that worked, and attacks that were caught.

Corma designed the study, ran it and released it. The page carrying the results would not open while this article was written, and no outside group appears to have rerun it.

The capital still moves the other way. Horizon3.ai, which automates penetration testing, took $250 million in August, more than Corma has raised in all.

Pebblous measures what condition AI training data is in, and DataClinic is where that measurement happens. A defense-tuned model reads whatever its buyer already wrote down, and nobody wrote those logs for a model. Retention follows the cost of storage, and the name of a field changes when the device changes. A stretch when the collector was down looks like a quiet night.

Picking the model is this year's decision. The years of logs it will read were settled a while ago.

## Facebook (EN)

Working out how someone got in usually means reading a log backwards.

Who came through and when, which machine went strange first, pieced together across fields that carry a different name on every device.

Those records were kept for exactly that. To retrace an incident afterward, and to have something ready for the auditors.

This week a second use was proposed for them.

Corma, a company split between Tel Aviv and San Francisco, has been valued at a billion dollars on the proposition that AI models should be built for defense and nothing else. Until three months ago nobody outside the company knew it existed.

What its founder keeps saying is that a general-purpose model is more useful to the side breaking in. Writing code and hunting bugs are what such a model has practised most, and those are the skills an intrusion runs on.

In an engagement study the company released, one model got a backdoor in 85% of the time while it sat as the attacker. Seated as the defender, it caught 19% of the attacks aimed at it. Corma designed the study and produced the figures, and no one outside has run it again.

The step after that one is where I slowed down.

If general-purpose models are weak on defense because they have read so few logs, then what makes a defense-tuned model worth anything is the record a company has already accumulated.

And that record had its retention window set by the price of storage, with the stretches where a collector was down left blank and unmarked.

"Can we tell a blank stretch apart from a quiet one?"

In DataClinic, Pebblous works out what condition a body of AI training data is in. Unless the story of how a value came to be sits beside the value, there is no getting it back later.

What the records being written today will end up proving, years from now, is not visible while they are being written.
