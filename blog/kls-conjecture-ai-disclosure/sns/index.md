## LinkedIn (KO)

31년 된 수학 난제를 푼 기록 가운데 AI의 기여를 가장 크게 적은 문서는, AI 회사의 발표가 아니었다.

마이크로소프트 리서치가 10월 21일 블로그로 KLS 추측의 해결을 알렸다. 근거가 된 자오 송·신즈 장의 논문은 마지막 절에 AI 공시를 따로 두고, 연구에 쓴 도구 넷을 이름으로 적었다.

그 논문 1판이 올라온 사흘 뒤, 피에르 비죌·보아즈 클라르타그·조제프 르엑이 따로 올린 32쪽 증명은 감사의 글에서 증명과 수학적 착상 대부분을 챗GPT가 찾았다고 밝혔다. 반면 마이크로소프트 블로그는 탐색 절차와 사람의 역할을 자세히 적으면서 모델 이름은 한 번도 쓰지 않았다.

세 기록이 채운 칸이 서로 다르다. 논문은 무엇을 썼는가를, 독립 증명은 얼마나 맡겼는가를, 블로그는 어떻게 썼는가를 적었다. 학술지가 AI 사용을 밝히라는 정책을 두기 시작했지만, 무엇을 어느 수준까지 적을지는 아직 저자가 알아서 정한다.

수학에는 기록이 모자라도 쓸 수 있는 최후 수단이 있다. 증명은 과정을 몰라도 결과를 한 줄씩 따라가면 참인지 거짓인지가 갈린다. 실제로 열흘 안에 독립 증명 둘과 Lean 4 형식 검증 하나가 그 일을 했다.

데이터 작업에는 그 최후 수단이 드물다. AI가 라벨을 붙이고 결측을 메운 표는 숫자만 들여다봐서는 어느 값이 원본이고 어느 값이 생성물인지 가려지지 않는다. 남는 길은 과정을 기록으로 함께 받는 것뿐이다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Of the three records behind the solution of a 31-year-old problem in mathematics, the one that credits AI most generously was not the announcement from the AI company.

Microsoft Research said on 21 October that the KLS conjecture, posed in 1995, had been settled. The paper behind that announcement, by Zhao Song and Xinzhi Zhang, closes with a dedicated AI disclosure section naming the four tools the work used.

Three days after that paper's first version, Pierre Bizeul, Bo'az Klartag and Joseph Lehec posted a separate 32-page proof. Its acknowledgements say most of the proofs and mathematical ideas in it were found by ChatGPT. The Microsoft blog, by contrast, sets out the search procedure and the human role in detail and never names a model.

Each record fills a different box. The paper records what was used, the independent proof records how much was handed over, the blog records how it was used. Journals have begun asking that AI use be declared, but what to declare, and to what depth, is still left to the authors.

Mathematics has a last resort for when the record runs thin. A proof can be followed line by line without knowing anything about how it was found, and within ten days two independent proofs and one Lean 4 formalisation did exactly that.

Data work rarely has that last resort. Once AI has attached the labels and filled the gaps, no amount of staring at the numbers separates what was observed from what was generated. The only thing left to ask for is the record of the process.

## Facebook (EN)

Someone carried a problem from 1995 around for the better part of a decade.

The first author of the 140-page paper that has now settled the KLS conjecture first heard of it on a visit to the University of Washington, took it up again as a postdoc at the Institute for Advanced Study around 2019 and 2020, and once more from roughly 2022 to 2024. Neither attempt worked.

None of this reached me secondhand. It is written into the last paragraph of the paper by the authors themselves.

The final date in that paragraph is 28 July. It is the day they read someone else's paper, posted the day before, and saw that the bound could come down further. It is also the day the two of them began testing more than 100 approaches alongside AI tools.

What survives in the paper is the one approach that worked. The rest is nowhere.

I have started calling the rest "the discarded routes."

"Is a thing you can still write down after the work is finished the same kind of record as a thing that vanishes unless you write it at the time?"

Tool names belong to the first kind. The paper duly listed four of them, and the 32-page proof posted three days after that paper's first version wrote that ChatGPT had found most of its proofs. But what the abandoned routes were, and why each was abandoned, cannot be recovered by anyone who was not writing it down as it happened.

Mathematics is in the better position here. A proof can be followed line by line with no knowledge of how it was found, and two independent proofs and a Lean 4 formalisation did that job within ten days.

The datasets that come to Pebblous have no such last resort. Once AI has attached the labels and filled the gaps, staring at the numbers will not tell you which value was observed. The only thing left is to receive the process as a record.

Which leaves the question. In the work we hand to the next person, how many lines of the discarded routes travel with it?
