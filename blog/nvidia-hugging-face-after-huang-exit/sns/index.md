## LinkedIn (KO)

129억 달러짜리 허깅페이스 인수는 젠슨 황이 떠난 뒤에도 그대로 간다. 11월 17일 실적 발표에서 임시 대표를 맡은 콜레트 크레스가 그렇게 밝혔다. 황이 즉시 사임한 지 닷새 뒤였다.

허깅페이스는 개발자 1,800만 명과 기업 20만 곳이 모델과 데이터셋을 올리고 내려받는 공용 창고다. 엔비디아는 이 플랫폼을 계속 열어 두겠다고 약속했고, 그 약속은 보도자료에만 있는 것이 아니다.

9월 2일자로 미국 증권거래위원회에 낸 8-K에 "다른 칩 공급사를 계속 지원한다"는 문장이 적혀 있다. 서명자는 당시 최고재무책임자 콜레트 크레스, 11월에 인수를 다시 확인한 바로 그 사람이다.

물어야 할 것은 약속이 적혔느냐가 아니라 무엇이 적히지 않았느냐다. 지원의 수준이 없다. 기한이 없다. 지키지 않았을 때 무엇을 물어 줘야 하는지가 없다. 잣대로 삼은 "허깅페이스의 기존 관행"은 어디에도 정의돼 있지 않다. 인수 계약서 자체는 이 서류에 붙지 않아, 그런 조항이 있는지조차 바깥에서 확인할 수 없다.

미국 법무부가 10월부터 이 거래를 반독점 조사하고 있다고 로이터가 전했다. 2018년 마이크로소프트의 깃허브 인수가 전례로 불려 나오지만 결정적인 칸 하나가 다르다. 당시 마이크로소프트의 본업은 깃허브에 올라온 코드와 직접 맞붙지 않았다. 엔비디아는 어떤 모델이 어떤 칩에서 잘 도는지가 본업 매출과 바로 이어진다.

페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 정량 진단한다. 이번 거래가 데이터를 쓰는 쪽에 남기는 물음은 품질표의 옆 칸에 있다. 쓰고 있는 모델과 데이터셋이 우리 저장소에 사본으로 있는가, 아니면 주소 한 줄로만 있는가.

## LinkedIn (EN)

The $12.9 billion purchase of Hugging Face outlasts Jensen Huang. Colette Kress, Nvidia's chief financial officer and now its interim chief executive, said on the November 17 earnings call that the deal stands. Huang had resigned with immediate effect five days earlier.

Hugging Face is the shared warehouse where 18 million developers and 200,000 companies upload and download models and datasets. Nvidia has promised to keep that platform open, and the promise is not confined to a press release.

The Form 8-K filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on September 2 says the company will continue to support other silicon vendors. It was signed by Kress, then chief financial officer, the same person who reaffirmed the deal in November.

The question is not whether the promise was written down but what was left out of it. No level of support. No deadline. No consequence for not keeping it. The yardstick the filing invokes, "Hugging Face's existing practices," has no public definition anywhere. The acquisition agreement itself was not attached, so nobody outside can confirm whether such a clause sits inside it.

Reuters reported in October that the Department of Justice had opened an antitrust review of the deal. Microsoft's 2018 purchase of GitHub gets reached for as the precedent, and one column differs. Microsoft's core business did not compete with the code hosted on GitHub. For Nvidia, which model runs well on which chip is the core business.

Pebblous quantifies the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. The question this deal leaves sits in the column next to quality. Do the models and datasets an organization uses exist as copies in its own storage, or only as a line of address?

## Facebook (EN)

One line of a model address, pasted in, and then the wait for a download bar to fill.

For several years now, using a model has meant, in practice, that one line. The pretrained weights came from there. So did the evaluation datasets, the tokenizers, the license text.

Eighteen million developers and two hundred thousand companies upload and download in the same place. "Shared warehouse" is the more accurate name for it.

The warehouse is changing hands.

Nvidia's agreement to buy Hugging Face for $12.9 billion survives the resignation of Jensen Huang. Colette Kress, the interim chief executive, said so on the November 17 earnings call, five days after he left.

The promise to keep the platform open is not confined to a press release. The Form 8-K filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission in September goes as far as a line about continuing to support other silicon vendors.

Reading it again, I found myself counting not the terms the filing sets but the ones it leaves blank.

No level of support. No deadline. No consequence for failing to keep it. And the yardstick the filing invokes, "Hugging Face's existing practices," is defined nowhere.

Leaving the door unlocked and sharing the shelves inside evenly are promises of different scope. A door can stay open while the shelving tilts.

"When my models and my data sit on someone else's distribution channel, and that channel is sold, what remains mine?"

Pebblous quantifies the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. The question this one leaves sits in the column beside quality.

Whether the models and datasets in use exist as copies in our own storage.

Whether the retrieval date and version were written down.

Whether a past experiment can be rerun with the weights it was run on.

There is no reason to doubt what Kress said. How long such a promise lasts, though, is decided by the ownership structure of a company rather than by the person who made it. These two weeks have shown that the person making the case most forcefully can be gone within five days.
