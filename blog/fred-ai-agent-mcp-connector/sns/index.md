10월 1일 세인트루이스 연방준비은행이 연 FRED Con에서 크리스토퍼 월러 연준 이사가 밝힌 수치다. FRED 트래픽은 지금 한 해 150%로 늘고 있고, 늘어난 몫의 대부분이 AI와 봇에서 나온다. 사람 사용자가 줄어서 절반이 된 것이 아니라 기계 쪽이 빠르게 붙어서 절반이 됐다. 연준은 같은 날 에이전트가 FRED를 직접 읽는 MCP 커넥터를 내놓았다.

월러는 FRED가 지금까지 사람이 보고 이해하도록 설계됐다고 적었다. 이제 새로운 종류의 독자에게 어떻게 내보일지를 바꿔야 한다는 것이 그의 결론이다. 사람이 읽을 때는 단위와 계절조정 여부가 그래프 아래 작은 글씨로 충분했다. 기계가 읽을 때는 그것이 값과 같은 자격으로 함께 실려 나가야 한다.

같은 연설에 조심스러운 문장이 하나 있다. 시험 과정에서 에이전트가 숫자를 원래 만든 기관이 아니라 FRED에 귀속시키는 장면이 더러 나왔다는 것이다. FRED는 통계를 생산하지 않는다. 85만 개가 넘는 시계열을 여러 기관에서 받아 한 형식으로 내보내는 통로다. 답변에 통로의 이름만 남으면 정의와 조사 방법과 개정 이력을 확인하러 갈 자리가 사라진다.

데이터를 기계가 읽게 만드는 일과, 읽고 난 뒤에도 숫자가 제 이름표를 달고 있게 하는 일은 같은 일이 아니다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Half the visits arriving at the Federal Reserve's public economic data site are no longer people.

Governor Christopher Waller gave the figure on October 1 at FRED Con, hosted by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. FRED traffic is growing at 150 percent a year, and most of that growth comes from AI and bots. The share did not reach half because human users fell away. It reached half because machines arrived quickly. The FRED team released an MCP connector the same day that lets an agent read the data directly.

FRED was built to be viewed and understood by humans, Waller said, and how it presents itself now has to adapt to a new type of viewer. Units and seasonal adjustment used to sit in small type under a chart, where a reader would go looking for them. A machine reader needs them handed over with the value itself.

The same speech carries a quieter sentence. In testing, agents have sometimes credited a number to FRED rather than to the agency that produced it. FRED produces no statistics. It takes more than 850,000 series from other agencies and republishes them in one consistent form. When only the conduit gets named, the place a reader would go for the definition, the method and the revision history is gone.

Making data machine-readable and making sure a number still carries its label after a machine has read it are two different jobs.

## Facebook (EN)

You have probably seen the line in small type under a chart in a news story. "Source: FRED."

For years I read that line as the source.

FRED is the public economic data site run by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. The unemployment rate is there, and so is GDP, but neither number was made there. The Bureau of Labor Statistics produces the unemployment rate. The Bureau of Economic Analysis produces GDP. FRED gathers them and sends them out in one consistent form.

On October 1, Governor Christopher Waller spoke at FRED Con about how that conduit is holding up.

About half the visits arriving at FRED are no longer people. They are AI agents.

Traffic is growing at 150 percent a year, and most of that growth comes from the machine side. The Fed opened a direct line for agents the same day.

One phrase from the speech kept catching. "A new type of viewer."

For thirty years the reader FRED designed around was a person opening a page to look at a chart. Half of that seat belongs to machines now, and machines do not look at charts. I have been writing them down as "machine readers."

The speech carries one quieter sentence.

In testing, agents have sometimes credited a number to FRED rather than to the agency that produced it. That came from the people who publish the data.

Waller separated production from reproduction. The ultimate credibility of a number rests with whoever produced it. What FRED guarantees is that the number was reproduced accurately. When an answer says only "according to FRED," those two collapse into one name.

When a person reads, the reader supplies much of the context. They infer the unit from the title, follow the footnote with their eyes, and open the agency's own site when something looks off.

A machine reader supplies none of that. Whatever is not handed over with the value simply vanishes, and the model fills the gap with something plausible.

Pebblous works on data quality. What separates data you can diagnose from data you cannot is rarely the values themselves. It is whether the line recording where those values came from travelled out with them.

"Of the visits hitting our servers right now, how many are people and how many are machines?"

FRED could put a figure on it because someone was keeping that record.

Whether a number can still say where it came from after it leaves your hands is settled before it leaves.
