## LinkedIn (KO)

메타와 오픈AI의 학습 데이터를 만들던 회사가 마지막으로 공개한 장부는 자산 약 100만 달러에 부채 1,700만 달러였다.

샌프란시스코에 본사를 둔 Sama 이야기다. 10월 23일 Samasource Impact Sourcing이 남은 자산을 'Sama ABC'라는 별도 법인으로 넘겼고, 2주 뒤 채권자들에게 그 사실을 알렸다. TIME이 11월 9일 이 사실을 단독으로 보도했다. 감사를 거치지 않은 수치인 데다 은행 한 곳이 자산 거의 전부에 우선변제권을 쥐고 있어, 순위가 뒤인 채권자에게 돌아갈 몫은 많지 않다.

이 회사는 2008년 비영리 Samasource로 출발했다. 데이터 작업을 잘게 쪼개 케냐·우간다·인도의 저소득층에게 맡기면 그 임금이 빈곤에서 벗어나는 사다리가 된다는 구상이었고, 업계는 이를 임팩트 소싱이라 불렀다. 메타가 맡긴 일은 콘텐츠 검수와 스마트 글래스 영상이었고, 오픈AI가 맡긴 일은 챗GPT 학습용 유해 콘텐츠 분류였다.

무너진 과정은 2026년 한 해에 몰려 있다. 3월에 스마트 글래스 영상을 검수하던 노동자들의 증언이 보도됐고, 4월에 메타가 7년 가까이 이어 온 계약을 끝내면서 나이로비 인력 1,108명 감원안이 나왔다.

9월 15일에는 케냐와 우간다 사업을 접었다. 그리고 한 달여 뒤 자산이 넘어갔다.

서한이 든 청산 사유는 매출 감소와 집단소송 방어 비용 두 가지인데, 어느 소송인지는 적지 않았다. 2022년 다니엘 모통이 제기한 강제노동·결사의 자유 침해 소송은 아직 케냐 법정에 있다. 그 사건에 피고로 올라 있는 이름은 서한을 보낸 미국 법인이 아니라 Samasource Kenya EPZ Limited다. 상호는 같아도 등록된 법인은 다르다.

공급사가 사라지면 대장에 적힌 이름부터 다시 읽게 된다. 그 이름이 상호인지 등록 법인인지가 그때 갈린다. 페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터를 항목마다 진단한다. 다만 라벨을 넘겨준 쪽이 어느 등기부에 올라 있었는지는 데이터가 아니라 계약서에 적혀 있다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Sama, the San Francisco company that supplied data labeling and content moderation to Meta and OpenAI, is being wound down. On its final ledger, roughly $1 million remains to be set against $17 million owed.

Samasource Impact Sourcing, Inc. moved what assets were left to a separate entity, Sama ABC, on October 23, then notified creditors on November 6. TIME broke the story on November 9. Those numbers were never audited, and a bank ranks ahead of everyone else on nearly all of what remains, so unsecured creditors are unlikely to see much.

Sama began in 2008 as a nonprofit. The premise: break data work into pieces that a short course can teach, route them to low-income workers in Kenya, Uganda and India, and the wage becomes a way out of poverty. The industry called it impact sourcing. Meta sent content moderation and smart-glasses footage; OpenAI sent harmful-content classification for ChatGPT training.

The collapse took one year. In March, workers described reviewing footage shot on Ray-Ban Meta glasses. In April, Meta ended a contract of nearly seven years and a layoff of 1,108 Nairobi staff was announced. On September 15 the Kenya and Uganda operations closed.

The letter blames falling revenue and the cost of defending a class action, and names no case. Daniel Motaung's 2022 suit over forced labor and freedom of association is still before a Kenyan court, and the defendant on that docket is Samasource Kenya EPZ Limited, not the US entity that wrote to creditors. Same trade name, separate registration.

When a supplier dissolves, the line in the vendor register gets read again. Whether that line holds a trade name or a filed company is settled then. ## Facebook (EN)

Daniel Motaung filed his case in May 2022.

He had been moderating Meta content in Nairobi. In 2019 he tried to organize a strike with colleagues and lost the job for it. For four and a half years the case moved through arguments about whether a Kenyan court could hear it at all. In February 2026, judgment was postponed.

The trial on the merits has not started. The company on the other side closed first.

That company is Sama. It handled content moderation for Meta and harmful-content classification for ChatGPT training, and on October 23 it moved what assets were left to another entity, then wrote to creditors on November 6.

About $1 million in assets against $17 million in debt. The reasons given were falling revenue and the cost of defending a class action, with no case named.

The same letter has another passage in it. Sama deliberately recruited from marginalized communities in East Africa, it says, and brought digital work to more than 70,000 lives, "at high living wages and benefits."

In January 2023, TIME put the take-home pay of that company's Kenyan workers at $1.32 to $2 an hour.

Neither sentence is a lie. The same labor has been written down twice, in two documents, in two different vocabularies. One is a farewell a company wrote about itself. The other is a figure counted from outside.

What held me on a second reading was the names. The letter came from Samasource Impact Sourcing, Inc., a US entity. The defendant on the Kenyan docket is Samasource Kenya EPZ Limited. The assets went to Sama ABC. All three read as Sama, and all three are separately registered.

"The name in our vendor register, which registry is it filed on?"
