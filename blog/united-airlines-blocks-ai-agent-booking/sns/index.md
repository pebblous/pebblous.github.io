## LinkedIn (KO)

유나이티드항공이 11월 11일 운송약관을 고쳐, 승인받지 않은 개인용 AI 비서가 자사 항공권을 예약하고 발권하고 바꾸는 일을 금지했다.

닫힌 것은 결제 단추 하나다. 일정과 운임을 조회하는 통로는 그대로 열려 있고, 여러 항공사의 값을 모아 주는 서비스에 유나이티드 운임이 실리는 것도 전과 같다. 약관이 말하는 AI 에이전트에는 소비자 앱의 비서, 웹을 대신 도는 AI 브라우저, 여행사 도구를 거쳐 닿는 비서가 들어가고, 승객이 옆에서 승인했는지는 따지지 않는다. 11월 11일 이전 예약은 그대로 인정한다.

근거는 둘인데 성격이 다르다. 하나는 계정이다. 자격증명을 남의 비서에게 넘기면 원치 않은 구매나 마일리지 사용이 뒤따를 수 있다는 것이다. 다른 하나는 법이다. 금지 물품이 없다는 수하물 확인은 승객 본인 몫이라고 연방 규정이 정해 두었다. 두 근거가 모두 결제와 체크인에만 걸려서 앞단은 열린 채 남았다.

집행 수단은 기술이 아니라 계약이다. 개정 약관은 AI 에이전트로 표를 사거나 바꾸는 일을 금지 관행에 올렸고, 그런 예약을 사전 통지 없이 취소할 수 있게 적었다. 들어오는 요청을 문 앞에서 가려내는 장치가 생긴 것은 아니다. 유나이티드는 자사 백엔드가 개인과 여행사와 AI 에이전트를 구분할 수 있다고 말했지만 무엇을 보고 구분하는지는 밝히지 않았고, 온라인 여행사를 거치면 그 구분이 흐려진다는 지적이 따른다.

델타는 먼저 지켜보겠다고 답했고, 사우스웨스트는 챗GPT 안에서 검색과 비교만 열었다. 보도에 이름이 오른 네 회사 모두 결제 단계는 자기 통제 아래 둔다. 갈리는 자리는 앞단을 누구에게 어떤 자격으로 열어 주느냐인데, 그 자격을 판정할 공통 규약이 없다. 비자와 마스터카드가 위임받은 에이전트를 증명하는 규격을 내놨지만, 쓴다고 밝힌 항공사는 아직 없다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

United Airlines rewrote its contract of carriage on November 11 to keep unauthorized personal AI assistants from booking, ticketing or changing its flights.

One button closed. Schedule and price searches still work, and United fares keep appearing on the services that pull many carriers into a single view. The clause reaches a wide set of tools: assistants inside consumer apps, AI browsers that roam the web for a user, agents running alone, and assistants arriving through an agency. Whether the traveler watched and approved makes no difference. Reservations created before November 11 stand.

Two grounds were offered, and they are not of one kind. The first is the account: hand credentials to someone else's assistant and purchases, account changes or mileage spending can follow that nobody intended. The second is federal law, which asks the passenger to be the one who confirms that the checked bag holds nothing prohibited. Both grounds attach at the back of the process, which is why the front was left alone.

Enforcement is written rather than built. The revised terms list AI-agent ticketing among prohibited practices and allow such reservations to be cancelled without notice. No gate inspects arriving requests. United says its backend can separate a booking from an individual, an agency and an AI agent, without saying what signal it reads, and airline distribution watchers note the separation blurs once an online travel agency sits in between.

Delta chose to watch before writing anything down. Southwest opened search and fare comparison inside ChatGPT while keeping payment on its own site. All four carriers named in the reporting hold the payment step themselves, and what divides them is whom they admit at the front, on what credential. Visa and Mastercard have published ways for an agent to prove a delegation. No airline says it runs one.

Pebblous reports on AI training data in figures, and DataClinic is the instrument. Delegation formats carry fields for limits and for scope. What none of them carry is a field for the acts only a person can complete.

## Facebook (EN)

Ask an AI assistant what a seat to San Francisco costs next month and the answer still comes back.

Ask it to buy that seat, and it has to take its hand off.

United Airlines wrote that line into its contract of carriage on November 11. Booking and ticketing by an unauthorized personal AI assistant are out. Looking and comparing were left exactly as they were.

Two reasons were given, and the second is the one that stays.

The first is familiar enough. Credentials handed to someone else's assistant can lead to purchases, account changes, miles spent that nobody intended. US banks have recently told their customers a version of this.

The second is a federal rule. At check-in the passenger is the one who confirms that the checked bag holds nothing prohibited. Let an assistant press through that screen and nobody has made the confirmation.

'An act no power of attorney can carry.'

The delegation formats now on offer all work by adjusting scope. How much may be spent. Which merchants are in bounds. How long the authority lasts. A step where the confirming itself is what the procedure consists of does not come loose when the scope narrows. Setting the limit to zero cannot say "this one is yours to do."

Medical consent is one of those. So is identity verification, and so is a safety inspection.

A training dataset comes into Pebblous and leaves DataClinic with figures attached. Those figures speak about the inside of the material. Which steps a person has to carry out is written somewhere else, in the rules rather than in the data, and that line is usually blank.

"In our own work, which steps fall apart if someone else presses the button?"

United had no such field, so it shut the channel instead. Whether a field would reopen it, or whether a shut door proves to be the easier answer, is not clear from here.
