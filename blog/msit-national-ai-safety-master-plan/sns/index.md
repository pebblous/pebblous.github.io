## LinkedIn (KO)

국가 AI 안전 종합계획을 쓸 사람 25명 안팎이 10월부터 모이기로 돼 있다. 명단도, 실제로 몇 번 모였는지도 공개되지 않았다.

과학기술정보통신부는 9월 30일 '(가칭) 국가 AI 안전 생태계 조성 마스터플랜'을 마련할 민·관 실무협의체를 구성했다고 밝혔다. 정부와 유관기관, 국내외 AI 기업과 협회, 기술·법·제도 전문가가 들어간다. 계획의 뼈대로 제시된 것은 국가 AI 안전 관리체계 구축, 첨단 AI 안전 대응역량 강화, 국민 일상의 AI 안전 환경 조성 세 가지다.

발표문이 기업에 적어 둔 역할은 하나다. 개발하고 쓰는 현장에서 겪은 것을 가져와 말하는 일이다. 과제를 점검하고 새로 발굴하는 몫은 전문가와 유관기관에 적혀 있다. 그래서 12월 문서의 눈금은 그 방에서 어느 현장의 말이 나왔느냐에 따라 달라질 수 있다. 제조·에너지처럼 공장에서 AI를 돌리는 회사 대부분은 그 방 밖에 있을 가능성이 크다.

12월은 법이 정한 기한이 아니다. 같은 계획을 두고 과기정통부는 2월 간담회에서 상반기 중 발표를 말했고, 9월 발표가 시한을 12월로 다시 적었다.

이미 시행 중인 AI 기본법 쪽도 비어 있다. 의무의 이행방법을 적을 고시 두 건은 10월 14일까지 제정되지 않았고, 그 뒤 제정됐다는 발표는 확인되지 않는다.

페블러스는 이 협의체에 참여하지 않는다. AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 정량 진단하는 일이 페블러스가 DataClinic으로 해 온 몫이다. 그 자리에서 보면 12월 문서에서 먼저 확인할 것은 적용 대상이다. 모델을 만드는 쪽과 가져다 쓰는 쪽에 같은 항목이 붙는지가 공장에서는 갈림길이 된다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

About 25 people are writing Korea's national AI safety plan, and neither their names nor an account of their meetings has been published.

The Ministry of Science and ICT announced on September 30 a public-private working group assembled to draft it. The plan goes by a provisional Korean title, '(가칭) 국가 AI 안전 생태계 조성 마스터플랜'; the ministry has issued no English name for it, and National AI Safety Master Plan is this piece's shorthand. Around the table sit the ministry, public agencies, AI firms Korean and foreign, the associations that speak for them, and specialists in technology and in law. The frame rests on three strategies: managing AI safety at national level, strengthening the response to advanced AI, and keeping AI safe in daily life.

The announcement hands companies a single job, which is to bring in what they have met while building and using AI. Reviewing the tasks and finding new ones belongs to the experts and the public agencies. The yardstick the December document ends up using can therefore shift with whose factory floor gets talked about in that room. Most firms running AI in manufacturing or energy are likely to be outside it.

No law fixes December. Of this same plan the ministry said in February that the substance would come during the first half of the year, and the September release then reset the target to December.

The statute already in force has gaps of its own. Two administrative notices that would spell out how the duties under the AI Framework Act are to be met had not been issued as of October 14, and nothing since confirms that either has gone out.

Pebblous is not in this working group. Putting a number on the quality of AI training data is what Pebblous does with DataClinic. From that vantage the first thing to look for in the December document is scope: whether the same items attach to the company that builds a model and to the company that buys one in.

## Facebook (EN)

There is a document that does not yet have a settled name.

In the government's own papers it goes by a working title: '(가칭) 국가 AI 안전 생태계 조성 마스터플랜'. The press release that announced it puts a different phrase in the headline, '국가 인공지능 안전 종합계획'. One document, two ways of naming it, and when it is settled in December even the name may change.

What Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT announced on September 30 was not the plan either. It was the people who will write it. About 25 of them, drawn from the ministry and from public agencies, from AI firms at home and abroad, from the bodies that represent them, and from technology and legal practice. The group was to begin full operation in October.

Read the announcement closely and the roles are already split. Companies are seated there to report what building and running AI has actually been like. Deciding which tasks hold up, and which new ones are needed, falls to the specialists and the agencies.

Which means the yardstick in the December document may shift a little, depending on which plants get described in that room.

No list of names has been published.

"Is there anyone in that room who would describe our process for us?"

A company without a seat is not without a voice. The Federation of Korean Industries collected what its member companies had to say and, on October 29, handed the ministry 19 suggested fixes to the AI Framework Act. An industry body or a business federation can still carry the message.

Numbering the quality of AI training data is the job DataClinic does at Pebblous. The same dataset tends to come around twice: once when it first arrives, and once after something about it has changed. The second pass is the one that gets skipped, and that is where the trouble usually turns out to have been waiting.

In December some companies will have written the document and the rest will be reading it. Being in the second group, though, may not be the same as having nothing to do.
