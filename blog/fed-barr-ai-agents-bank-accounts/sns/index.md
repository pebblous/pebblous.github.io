## LinkedIn (KO)

미국 중앙은행 이사가 11월 16일 밀라노 연설에서, 대형 은행들이 소매 고객 계좌에서 무언가를 하려는 AI 에이전트를 발견하고 있다고 말했다. 연방준비제도 마이클 바 이사다.

같은 문장에 단서가 붙어 있다. 은행이 그 에이전트의 진위도, 고객의 동의도, 고객 지시와의 일치도 확인하지 못한다는 것이다. 연설문의 동사는 "시도하는(attempting)"이다. 행동이 끝까지 갔다는 말은 들어 있지 않다. 발견 대상은 기업 계좌가 아니라 개인의 계좌다.

셋은 서로 다른 물음인데 공통점이 하나 있다. 어느 것도 모델의 성능을 묻지 않는다. 비서가 금리를 더 잘 비교하게 되어도 세 칸은 그대로 빈다. 은행 서버 앞에 도착하는 요청이 그 정보를 데려오지 않기 때문이다.

바 이사는 여기서 장면을 하나 더 그렸다. 고객이 직접 이체를 실행하던 단계까지 에이전트가 맡으면, 금리 차이를 감지한 즉시 자금이 움직이고 예금 인출 사태가 몇 분 만에 끝날 수 있다는 것이다. 다만 이쪽 조동사는 "~할 수 있다"이고, 그런 인출이 어디서 일어났다는 말은 연설문에 없다. 계좌 안의 에이전트는 보고된 사실이고, 몇 분은 아직 경고다.

연설에서 자리를 더 많이 차지한 쪽은 AI 투자 붐의 빚이었다. 가장 큰 AI 기업 일곱 곳의 장부상 부채가 연말 전에 8,000억 달러를 넘을 것으로 봤다. 연설문은 그 일곱 곳의 이름을 밝히지 않았다. 장부 밖 채무가 3조 달러를 넘는다는 금융안정보고서 추정도 함께 있지만, 명목 최대치라 앞의 수치와 더할 수 있는 값이 아니다.

페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 정량 진단한다. 자료를 깨끗하게 만드는 일과, 그 자료에 누가 어떤 자격으로 손을 댔는지를 남기는 일은 서로 다른 작업이다. 바 이사가 꼽은 안전장치 가운데 인증과 동의 확인은 뒤쪽에 걸린다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

A governor of the US Federal Reserve told a conference in Milan on November 16 that major banks are now reporting AI agents trying to take actions inside retail customer accounts.

Michael Barr attached a condition. The bank, he said, has no way to confirm the agent is genuine, that the customer agreed, or that the action matches the instruction given. His verb was "attempting," so nothing says the action ran to completion, and the accounts are retail, not corporate.

The three checks have one thing in common: not one of them is about model performance. An assistant that compares interest rates better leaves the three fields as empty as they were, because the request arriving at the bank server does not bring that information with it. Barr counted four guardrails that would have to exist: strict authentication, consent verified for specific actions, technical controls on what an agent may do, and constraints grounded in rules and values.

He then sketched a second scene. Where a customer once had to execute a transfer, an agent could read a yield differential and move the money itself, with a deposit run then compressed into minutes, outrunning both liquidity management and the moment a supervisor could act. That passage carries "may" and "could." The speech never says such a run has occurred. What banks have reported is the smaller thing: the agents inside the accounts.

By length, the body of the speech was the debt behind the AI investment boom. On-balance-sheet debt at the seven largest AI firms was expected to pass $800 billion before year end, and the speech does not name the seven. The Fed's Financial Stability Report puts obligations off the books above $3 trillion at their potential notional maximum, an undiscounted figure that cannot be added to the first.

Pebblous quantifies the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. Cleaning material up is one job. Keeping a record of who touched that material, and under what authority, is a different one. Two of the guardrails Barr named, authentication and consent verification, catch on the second.

## Facebook (EN)

Open the transaction history in a banking app and a line comes back. When, how much, where to.

One thing is not on that line.

Who set the transfer in motion.

It has not had to be there. Somebody always had to press the button.

In Milan on November 16, Federal Reserve Governor Michael Barr put a sentence on the record that unsettles the arrangement. Major banks, he said, now report finding AI agents attempting to take actions in the accounts of individual customers. And the bank cannot verify the agent's authenticity, the customer's consent, or alignment with what the customer asked for.

'What the request does not bring with it.'

All three of the checks Barr named ask about a record rather than a model. An assistant that compares interest rates better leaves the three fields exactly as empty as before, because the request arriving at the bank server does not bring that information with it.

He also listed what would have to be built: strict authentication, consent verified for specific actions, technical limits on what an agent may do, and constraints grounded in rules as well as values. Naming four things that must exist is another way of saying all four are missing now.

The work Pebblous does with DataClinic measures how good a set of AI training data is and says so in figures. Those figures answer what sits inside the material. Who reached into it, and under whose authority, belongs to a different record, and the different record is often not there at all. Where it is not, one line in a log is everything you have to open after something goes wrong.

"Of the requests that arrived today, can you separate the ones a person sent from the ones an assistant sent?"

Barr also warned that a deposit run could be over in minutes once an agent both reads the signal and moves the funds. That has not happened anywhere. The part already on the record is the smaller one: banks are finding something inside the accounts and cannot say whose instruction it is following. What can be written down in between is a question each set of records answers on its own.
