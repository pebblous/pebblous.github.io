## LinkedIn (KO)

정부 기관이 서로 주고받는 데이터는 848개 기관에 열려 있는데, 건강보험으로 치아 스케일링을 다시 받을 수 있는지에는 아직 답이 서지 않는다.

11월 6일 국가인공지능전략위원회 제3차 전체회의에서 배경훈 과기부총리는 부처별 공공데이터 활용 AI 전환 사업 약 30개를 12월 중순 나올 '모두의 AI'에 연결하는 중이라고 보고했다. 같은 자리에서 조준희 한국소프트웨어산업협회장이 다른 쪽을 짚었다. 데이터를 확보하는 일과 그 데이터로 국민이 쓸 서비스를 만드는 일은 별개 작업이라는 것이다.

그가 든 예가 스케일링이었다. 진료 기록에 적힌 것은 마지막으로 받은 날짜뿐이고, 날짜는 답이 아니라 답의 재료다. 그 항목이 얼마 만에 다시 급여 대상이 되는지는 건강보험 급여 기준에 있다. 둘이 한자리에 있어야 된다와 안 된다가 갈린다. 진료 기록을 백만 건 더 모아도 기준이 없으면 답은 똑같이 나오지 않는다.

모으는 쪽이 부실한 탓은 아니었다. 행정안전부의 범정부 데이터 파이프라인은 9월 시범 운영을 시작해 지금 848개 기관에 열려 있고, 등록된 공유데이터는 5만 4768건이다.

연결한 기관 수와 등록한 건수는 세기 쉽고, 어떤 물음에 답할 수 있게 됐는지는 세기 어렵다. 예산과 성과지표는 대개 세기 쉬운 쪽을 센다. 같은 부처가 회의 밖에서 따로 내놓은 2025년 공공데이터 평가에서 기관들의 관리체계 점수는 89.5점, 그해 새로 들어간 'AI 친화·고가치 데이터 개방' 지표는 37.8점이었다.

페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 파일 단위로 진단한다. 파일을 열어 확인할 수 있는 것은 거기 들어 있는 값이다. 그 값을 판정으로 바꿔 줄 기준은 대개 다른 문서에 적혀 있고, 데이터셋만 열어 봐서는 그 문서가 있는지조차 알 수 없다.

'모두의 AI'는 12월 중순에 나온다. 기록과 기준이 함께 있어야 답이 서는 질문을 하나 던져 보면, 연결된 것이 데이터인지 답인지 드러난다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

The data Korean public bodies pass to each other now reaches 848 institutions, and it still cannot say whether health insurance will cover another dental cleaning.

At the third plenary meeting of Korea's National AI Strategy Committee on November 6, Bae Kyung-hoon, the minister for science and ICT, reported that about 30 AI transition projects built on public data are being wired into Moduui AI, the government service due in mid-December. In the same room, Cho Jun-hee, chairman of the Korea Software Industry Association, took up the other side. Securing data and building the service citizens will use, he said, are separate jobs.

The dental cleaning was his example. All the treatment record holds is the date of the last visit, and a date is the material for an answer rather than the answer. How long before the procedure becomes eligible again is written in the health insurance benefit rule, and nothing separates a yes from a no until the two sit together. A million more treatment records will not change that.

The pooling side is not thin. The Ministry of the Interior and Safety opened a pilot in September, and its cross-government data pipeline now reaches 848 public bodies with 54,768 shared datasets registered.

Bodies linked up and datasets registered are easy to count. Which questions can now be answered is hard to count, and budgets and performance targets tend to count the easy side. In a 2025 evaluation the same ministry published outside this meeting, public bodies scored 89.5 on management systems and 37.8 on 'AI-friendly, high-value data opening', an indicator added that year.

Pebblous breaks down the quality of AI training data file by file with DataClinic. A file shows you the values inside it. The rule that turns those values into a judgment usually lives in a different document, and opening the dataset will not tell you whether that document exists.

Moduui AI arrives in mid-December. Put one question to it that needs both a record and a rule, and the reply shows which of the two got connected.

## Facebook (EN)

I once asked at the front desk, on my way out from a dental cleaning, when insurance would cover the next one.

"When does this come round again?"

Someone checked the date and told me. At the desk it was a simple thing.

The same question came up at the third plenary meeting of Korea's National AI Strategy Committee on November 6. Cho Jun-hee, chairman of the Korea Software Industry Association, raised it about Moduui AI, the government service due in mid-December.

What does an AI need in order to answer it?

The treatment record looks like enough. The date of the last cleaning is written there.

But that date is only 'the material for an answer'.

How long before the procedure becomes eligible again, and whether age or condition changes the terms, is written in the health insurance benefit rule rather than in the record. Both have to be in one place before yes and no can be told apart.

At the same meeting, Bae Kyung-hoon, the minister for science and ICT, reported that about 30 AI transition projects built on public data across ministries are being wired into Moduui AI.

The pooling side is not small. The Ministry of the Interior and Safety's cross-government data pipeline reaches 848 public bodies, with 54,768 shared datasets registered on it.

The number 848 tells you data has been gathered. It does not tell you which questions now have an answer.

Those are two kinds of maturity, and the first one growing does not make the second grow with it.

Pebblous puts a grade on the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. A score is worked out from what sits inside the file. The rule that turns those values into a judgment usually lives in another department's document or in one person's head, so a good score can leave that place empty.

"Could you write down, right now, ten questions you would actually put to your own data?"

Write them out and about half of them, usually, get no answer from the data you hold. If you can then tell whether what is missing is a record or a rule, you know whether gathering more would help.

Moduui AI arrives in mid-December. I intend to put a question like the dental cleaning one to it.

If an answer comes back, the work behind it included attaching rules and not only moving data.
