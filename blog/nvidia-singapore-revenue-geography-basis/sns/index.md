## LinkedIn (KO)

엔비디아 장부에서 한 나라가 사라지는 데 필요했던 것은 각주 한 문단이다.

직전 분기까지 엔비디아의 지역별 매출 표에서 싱가포르는 102억 달러, 전체 매출의 22%를 차지했다. 다음 분기 표에 그 줄은 없다.

바뀐 것은 매출이 아니라 '지역'이라는 칸을 채우는 기준이다. 청구서를 받는 주소로 세던 것을 고객 본사 주소로 바꿨고, 엔비디아는 그 편이 매출의 지리적 구성을 더 잘 나타낸다고 각주에 적었다. 과거 분기도 새 기준으로 다시 계산했다.

그 각주가 공시된 것은 약 1년 전이다. 로이터가 11월 20일 미국 증권거래위원회 조사의 초점이 이 회계 처리라고 전하면서 그 문단이 뒤늦게 읽혔다.

주가는 그날 하루에 13%가 넘게 밀렸고 시가총액 4,900억 달러가량이 사라졌다.

허위 기재를 가리키는 증거는 아직 나오지 않았다. 새 기준을 가장 날카롭게 비판한 마이클 버리조차 그렇게 적었다. 쟁점은 거짓이냐 아니냐가 아니라, 기준이 바뀐 사실은 각주에 남고 숫자는 표만 떼어 돌아다닌다는 점이다.

조직의 데이터 웨어하우스에도 같은 칸이 있다. '지역' 칸이 청구지로 채워졌는지 법인 본사로 채워졌는지, 그 기준이 언제 바뀌었는지가 어디에도 적혀 있지 않으면 지난 리포트와 지금 리포트는 같은 자로 잰 것이 아니다.

페블러스의 데이터 진단이 값의 정확도에서 끝나지 않는 것도 같은 문제 때문이다. 한 칸이 무엇을 세고 있는지는 값 안에 들어 있지 않다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Taking a country off Nvidia's books required one paragraph of a footnote.

In the quarter that ended in July, Singapore stood at $10.2bn in Nvidia's table of revenue by region, 22% of the total. In the next quarter's table that line is not there.

The revenue did not move. The rule for filling in the "region" column did. Nvidia had been counting by the address that receives the invoice and switched to where the customer is headquartered, a basis its footnote says better represents the geographic profile of its revenue. Prior quarters were recalculated on the same basis.

The footnote was filed about a year ago. When Reuters reported on 20 November that the SEC's examination of Nvidia centres on this accounting treatment, the stock closed down more than 13% and roughly $490bn of market value went with it.

Nobody has produced evidence of a misstatement, Michael Burry included, and he is the change's sharpest critic, calling the new basis "a much less geographically precise methodology". The question is not whether the filings were false. It is that the change lives in a footnote while the figures travel as a table.

Every corporate warehouse carries the same column. Unless the definition behind "region" is written down somewhere, together with the date it last changed, last year's report and this year's were not measured with the same ruler.

Pebblous scores datasets for quality, and the same gap shows up there. A score is only readable next to a record of what the column was counting.

## Facebook (EN)

Set the two quarterly filings side by side and one line is missing.

In the summer 2025 report, the table of revenue by region lists Singapore at $10.2bn. In the autumn report, the same table has no such line at all.

It is not that two figures disagree. A row that existed on one page is absent from the other.

What Nvidia touched was not the revenue. It was which address fills in the column called "region".

The company had been counting by where the invoice is sent and moved to where the customer is headquartered, with a sentence in the footnote explaining why. Earlier quarters were recalculated on the new basis. Nothing was concealed.

And still it took about a year for that footnote to become an event.

Through those quarters the new tables were filed and people read them and formed judgements. A table travels on its own. A footnote does not travel with it.

That the basis changed is nowhere in the table. It is in the footnote. "Quiet redefinition" is as good a name as any for it.

Corporate databases carry the same thing. One country field on a customer table. One region field on an orders table.

"What fills our 'region' column? Where is the date that rule last changed written down?"

A person who finds a table odd can walk over and ask. A model reading the same table has nobody to ask. It works from the column name and the values.

Working at Pebblous has taught me to ask of a dataset not only whether the values are clean but since when they have meant what they mean.

However the investigation ends, what stays with me is not the share price. It is that when the definition of one column changed, a footnote was the only place to put that fact.
