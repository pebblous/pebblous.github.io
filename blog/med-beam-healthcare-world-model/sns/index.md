응급실을 거쳐 입원한 환자 200명에게 다음 24시간 동안 어떤 처치가 이뤄질지 순서대로 짚게 한 과제에서, 의료 전용 모델이 평균 정밀도 0.565를 받았다. 같은 과제에 앉은 범용 모델들은 0.124에서 0.228 사이였다.

샌프란시스코의 Knit Health가 11월 16일 NEA 주도 3,500만 달러 시리즈 A와 함께 공개한 Med-BEAM이다. 회사는 이것을 의료를 위한 월드 모델이라고 부른다. 월드 모델은 로봇과 자율주행에서 쓰던 말이고, 다음 낱말이 아니라 세계의 다음 상태를 고르는 쪽이다. 의료에서 그 세계는 환자 기록이다. 보도자료가 학습 자료로 적은 것은 수십억 건의 진료 결정과 수억 명 분량의 장기 환자 기록, 두 줄이 전부다.

보도자료가 앞세운 '4배'는 함께 견준 모델 가운데 가장 낮은 점수와 맞춘 값이다. 가장 높은 쪽과 견주면 2.5배가 된다. 비교를 설계하고 수행하고 공개한 주체는 모두 회사이고, 과제는 하나였다.

같은 발표에 함께 실린 두 숫자는 성격이 또 다르다. 응답 시간 2초는 측정값이 아니라 추정한 처리 시간이고, 250배 저렴하다는 비용은 추론 시간에 컴퓨트 단가를 곱한 값이다. 독립 보도는 같은 항목을 37~257배로 적었다. 정확도와 속도와 비용이 한 자리에 같은 꼴로 적혀 있지만, 실제로 잰 것은 맨 앞 하나다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

On a task that asked models to rank the clinical actions a patient would receive over the next 24 hours, a healthcare-specific model scored 0.565 on average precision. The general-purpose models set against it landed between 0.124 and 0.228. The patient group was 200 emergency admissions.

The model is Med-BEAM, which Knit Health of San Francisco introduced on November 16 alongside a $35 million Series A led by NEA. The company calls it a world model for healthcare. World model is a phrase borrowed from robotics and self-driving, where the job is to pick the next state of the world rather than the next word. In medicine that world is the patient record. What the press release names as training material runs to two lines: billions of clinical decisions and hundreds of millions of longitudinal patient records.

The four-times figure the release leads with is measured against the lowest scorer in the lineup. Against the highest it comes to 2.5 times. Knit Health designed the comparison, ran it, and published it, and it covers a single task.

The pair of numbers sitting next to that one is worth a second look. The two-second response time is not a measurement but an estimated processing time, and the claim of 250 times cheaper comes from inference time multiplied by a compute rate. Independent coverage put the same item at 37 to 257 times. Accuracy, speed and cost are written out in the same form and in the same place, yet only the first was actually measured.

At Pebblous the question of whether AI training data is good enough gets answered by DataClinic, as a number. A metric usually arrives as one line, but the methods behind its columns rarely match. Which part is observation and which part is arithmetic is something the recipient has to go back and ask.

The first question to put to a medical AI is not about the model. It is how each of the numbers printed beside each other was produced.

## Facebook (EN)

A hospital visit ends, and a set of records stays behind.

They were written for billing, and for whoever sees the patient next.

On November 16, Knit Health of San Francisco introduced Med-BEAM and called it a world model for healthcare. The idea is to hold the path a patient takes through a hospital inside a model, so that the next step can be seen before it arrives. The training material the company names is billions of clinical decisions and hundreds of millions of longitudinal patient records.

World model is a phrase that came from self-driving.

On that side, knowing more of the world is a matter of adding sensors and driving more miles.

Medicine has no such road.

Records grow in only one way, which is that someone falls ill and goes in. A model that has read more has read the medical histories of more people.

And what those records hold is not only illness.

Every health system has its own coding, and the same treatment lands in a different field depending on where it was entered. So the material carries two things at once: how a condition unfolds, and how one hospital has been in the habit of writing it down.

Pebblous asks how good AI training data is before anyone trains on it, and DataClinic is how that question is answered. The same table can be usable or unusable depending on where it was gathered and under what arrangement. That part is rarely written inside the table. It sits outside it, in contracts and in the circumstances of the place the records came from.

"If our data grew this year, what was it that grew?"

Nobody sees a doctor in order to add a row to a table. Keeping that order the right way round still looks like the first thing to settle before a medical AI is chosen.
