## LinkedIn (KO)

구글 딥마인드의 영국 직원 계약서에는 회사를 그만둔 뒤에도 평생 비판하지 못하게 하는 문구가 들어 있다. 워싱턴포스트가 11월 3일 계약서를 직접 열람하고 현직자 두 명을 인터뷰해 보도했다. 두 사람 모두 보복이 두려워 이름을 밝히지 않았다.

조항이 바깥으로 나온 계기는 한 사람이 그것을 어기기로 한 일이었다. 군사용 AI 개발에 반대해 10월에 사직한 런던 연구 엔지니어 소피아 리구오리가 BBC에서 입을 열자, 남은 직원들이 자기 계약서를 꺼내 같은 문구를 찾아냈고 사내 메시지 스레드에 수십 명이 몰렸다.

구글은 이미 퇴사한 사람에게는 집행하지 않겠다고 답했고, 최근 신규 채용 계약에서는 퇴직 후 적용 부분을 뺐다고 밝혔다. 직원들이 요구한 것은 기존 계약서에서 조항을 지우는 일인데 그 부분은 그대로다. 회사가 내놓은 것은 집행하지 않겠다는 말이고, 계약서에 남은 것은 문구다. 전직 연구자가 공청회에서 자기가 본 것을 말하려 할 때 기댈 근거는 자기 계약서가 아니라 회사가 한 번 한 말이 된다.

영국 공익제보법은 보호받는 공개를 막으려는 조항을 그 범위에서 무효로 본다. 다만 보호 목록은 범죄와 법적 의무 위반, 건강과 안전에 대한 위험 같은 항목으로 한정된다. 평가에서 나온 수치를 어떻게 골라 실었는지, 내부에서 제기된 우려가 출시 결정에 어떻게 반영됐는지는 그 목록 바깥에 있다. 조항이 가장 세게 작동하는 자리가 거기다.

페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 재서 진단한다. 그 데이터는 괜찮다는 담당자의 답변과, 어떤 검사를 어떤 기준으로 돌렸는지 적힌 진단서는 같은 근거가 아니다. 앞의 것은 그 사람이 팀을 옮기는 날 함께 사라진다.

모델 카드도 시스템 카드도 안전 평가 보고서도 그 모델을 만든 회사가 쓴다. 공급사를 고를 때 물어볼 항목이 하나 늘어난 셈이다. 그 회사의 전·현직 직원은 위험에 대해 공개적으로 말할 수 있는가.

## LinkedIn (EN)

The employment contracts of Google DeepMind's UK staff bar them from criticizing the company for the rest of their lives, and the bar does not lift on the day the job ends. The Washington Post reviewed the contracts, interviewed two current employees and published the story on November 3. Both asked not to be named, for fear of retaliation.

The clause surfaced because one person decided to break it. Sofia Liguori, a research engineer in London who resigned in October over the company's military AI work, told the BBC that in principle she is not allowed to criticise Google for the duration of her life. The colleagues who stayed pulled up their own contracts, found the same wording, and dozens of them gathered in an internal thread.

Google answered that it will not enforce the clause against anyone who has already left, and said it had taken the post-termination part out of the terms it gives new hires. What the staff asked for was the removal of the clause from the contracts that already exist, and that has not happened. An assurance is what the company offered. Wording is what stays in the contract. When a former researcher wants to describe what they saw, at a conference or a hearing, the thing they can lean on is not their own contract but something the company said once.

UK whistleblower law treats a contractual term as void to the extent that it blocks a protected disclosure. The protected list, though, runs to things like criminal offences, breaches of legal obligation and dangers to health and safety. How the numbers in an evaluation were selected, or how a concern raised inside the company fed into a release decision, sits outside that list, and that is where the clause bites hardest.

Model cards, system cards and safety evaluation reports are written by the company that built the model. That adds one line to a vendor questionnaire. Can current and former employees of this company speak publicly about risk?

## Facebook (EN)

One person said something in an interview, and the people who stayed went and opened their own contracts.

The same wording sat in each of them. That you will not criticize the company, for the rest of your life, including after you have left it.

Sofia Liguori was a research engineer in London until October. Her work was AI for pushing electricity through the grid more efficiently, about as far from the military as a seat at that company gets.

The first thing she told the BBC was that her own contract forbids the very thing she was about to do.

Dozens gathered in an internal thread. Google answered that it will not enforce the clause against people who have already left, and that it had taken the part which applies after departure out of the terms new hires sign.

The contracts that already exist still carry the wording.

'A clause lifted by word of mouth only'.

When a former researcher wants to describe what they saw at a hearing, what they can lean on is not their own contract. It is something the company said once. Once the person who said it moves on, what remains is decided by the contract.

Pebblous monitors the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. Someone telling you the dataset is fine, and a report naming which checks ran against which thresholds, are not the same kind of ground to stand on. Only the second one still reads in a room the speaker has left.

"The safety assurance our vendor gave us: whose word is it right now, or which line of which document?"

Model cards, system cards, safety evaluation reports are written by the company that built the model. The people who could answer them back from the outside are the people who built it, and a contract has tied their hands.

Whether the clause would survive a tribunal is the question after that one. The person who has read it goes quiet well before then.
