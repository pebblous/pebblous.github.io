TV 모델명 한 줄을 AI 에이전트에 건네면 선탑재 앱이 꺼집니다. 10월 19일 테크크런치가 그런 사람들을 모아 보도했습니다.

한 이용자는 하루 오후에 선탑재 패키지 25개를 끄고 192MB 기본 런처를 83MB짜리로 바꿨습니다. 따라 한 기종으로는 TCL과 소니, 파이어 TV, LG가 꼽혔습니다.

예전에도 못 할 일은 아니었습니다. 패키지 이름을 뒤지고, 안드로이드 디버그 브리지로 셸을 붙이고, 런처를 따로 구해 설치할 줄 알아야 했을 뿐입니다. 달라진 것은 그 지식을 쥔 쪽이 사람에서 에이전트로 옮겨 갔다는 점입니다.

다만 걷어낸 것은 광고 앱이고, 무엇을 보는지 알아내는 기능은 한 층 아래에 있습니다. ACR, 우리말로 자동 콘텐츠 인식은 화면이나 소리에서 지문을 떠 서버의 대조 목록과 맞춥니다. 2024년 ACM 인터넷 측정 학회에 실린 측정 연구에서는 TV를 HDMI 외부 모니터로만 쓸 때도 ACR 통신이 이어졌습니다. 수집을 멈추는 스위치는 설정의 동의 항목에 따로 있습니다.

수집은 자동으로 돌고 거부는 수고에 맡겨져 있습니다. 약관을 끝까지 읽고, 설정을 두 곳 이상 들러 철회하고, 펌웨어가 올라간 뒤 같은 자리를 다시 열어 보는 일은 시간과 지식을 가진 사람만 합니다. 에이전트가 그 수고를 대신하기 시작하면, 동의는 아는 사람의 몫이던 자리에서 물어볼 줄 아는 사람의 일로 내려옵니다.

페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 정량 진단합니다. 수집을 걷어내는 쪽이 기술에 밝은 집단에 몰리면 광고 측정이 기대는 시청 패널에서 그 집단이 먼저 빠집니다. 집계된 숫자는 멀쩡해 보이는데 모집단이 기울어 있게 되고, 결측이 어디서 생겼는지를 적어 두지 않으면 그 기울기는 나중에도 복원되지 않습니다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Hand an AI agent a TV model number and the preloaded apps come off. TechCrunch gathered the people doing it on October 19.

One user spent an afternoon switching off 25 preloaded packages and swapped the 192MB stock launcher for an 83MB one. Others followed on TCL, Sony, Fire TV and LG.

None of this was impossible before. It took digging up package names, attaching a shell over the Android Debug Bridge, and finding and installing a launcher yourself. What changed is which side holds that knowledge. It has moved from people to agents.

What came off, though, were the ad apps. The feature that works out what is playing sits a layer below them. ACR, or automatic content recognition, lifts a fingerprint from the picture or the sound and matches it against a reference list on a server. In a measurement study presented at the ACM Internet Measurement Conference in 2024, ACR traffic continued even when the set was used only as an external HDMI monitor. The switch that stops the collection sits separately, among the consent items in the settings.

Collection runs automatically and refusal is left to errands. Reading the terms to the end, going into more than one settings menu to withdraw, and checking after a firmware update that the withdrawal held are things only people with time and knowledge do. When agents take on those errands, consent moves from the portion of those who know the technology to those who know how to ask.

Pebblous verifies the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. If the people stripping out the collection cluster among the technically fluent, that group drops out first from the viewing panels ad measurement leans on. The aggregate still reads fine while the population underneath tilts, and data that never recorded where its missingness came from cannot recover that tilt later.

## Facebook (EN)

Switch on the television at your parents' house and an ad arrives before Netflix does.

That bothered a man who goes by Gathin T. So he handed the TV's model number to Claude and asked it to strip out what had come preinstalled.

Twenty-five Xiaomi packages went off, and the screen that greeted him at power-on became a launcher resembling Apple TV. He put the prompt on GitHub, and over the next two days, he says, a few thousand people did the same to their own sets.

"The quieted TV."

The ads are gone. Quieted and not watching, though, are two different things.

The feature that works out what is playing belongs to the TV rather than to any app. It lifts a fingerprint from the picture or the sound and matches it against a list on a server. In a 2024 measurement study, that traffic continued even when the set was used only as an HDMI monitor. Stopping it means withdrawing a consent item inside the settings.

"What is my television sending right now?"

Answering that means reading the terms to the end, opening settings in more than one place, and going back to the same screen after a firmware update. Consent is written as everyone's right. In practice it has been the portion of those who could afford the errand.

When agents start running the errand, the tilt moves a little. Work that belonged to people who know the technology comes down to people who know how to ask.

Pebblous verifies the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. What that work keeps catching is not the wrong value but the absent one. If the people stripping out the collection cluster in one group, that group drops out of the viewing panels first. The aggregate reads fine while only the population underneath tilts.

Which leaves the consent rate, and the question of what that number has been measuring all along.

Whether so many people consented because they agreed, or because they never found the switch, is something that starts coming apart from here.
