## LinkedIn (KO)

정부가 준비 중인 AI 에이전트 채점표는 최종 답이 맞았는지만 보지 않는다. 에이전트가 짠 계획과 도구를 부른 순서, 중간에 바꾼 판단의 타당성까지 점수에 들어간다.

과학기술정보통신부는 7월 23일 제11회 과학기술관계장관회의에서 관계 부처와 합동으로 '에이전틱 AI 이니셔티브'를 발표했다. 안전·신뢰 선도와 개방형 실행 기반 조성, 수요 중심 도입 촉진이 세 축인데 기업 실무와 먼저 맞닿는 쪽은 첫째다. 개인정보 보호와 권한 관리, 목표 이탈 방지를 담은 안전·신뢰 가이드라인을 연내 마련하고, 성능과 안전성을 잴 데이터셋과 벤치마크 도구를 함께 갖추겠다고 했다.

시험 도구 쪽은 이미 움직이고 있다. 과기정통부와 한국지능정보사회진흥원이 공고한 검증 체계 사업에 정부출연금 18억 원이 붙었고, 9월에 확정된 수행 컨소시엄은 검증 데이터셋을 공고 기준 5,000건 이상에서 7,000건 이상으로 늘렸다. 예약 가능 시간이나 가격처럼 실시간으로 바뀌는 데이터까지 시나리오에 들어간다.

규범 문서 쪽은 속도가 다르다. 11월 6일 과기정통부와 한국인터넷진흥원이 에이전틱 AI 제로트러스트 안내서를 먼저 내놓았고, 가이드라인 본체는 11월 17일까지 공개가 확인되지 않는다. 약속한 시한이 연말이라 기한은 남아 있다. 다만 올해 안에 에이전트를 업무에 넣으려는 기업이 참고할 공식 문서는 지금 그 안내서 한 건이다.

안내서가 확인 절차를 두라고 지목한 자리는 입력을 받는 곳과 도구를 부르는 곳, 일을 넘기는 곳, 바깥과 통신하는 곳이다. 지점마다 확인한다는 말을 기록 쪽에서 뒤집으면, 그 지점마다 무엇을 근거로 그렇게 했는지가 남아 있어야 한다는 뜻이 된다. 점수를 매기려면 채점할 대상이 먼저 있어야 한다.

페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 같은 잣대로 되풀이해 잰다. 모델을 올리거나 붙여 둔 도구를 바꾸면 앞서 받은 점수는 그대로 쓸 수 없고, 다시 재야 두 점수가 비교된다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

The scorecard Korea's government is assembling for AI agents does not stop at whether the final answer was right. The plan the agent drew up, the order it called its tools in, and the reasoning behind each change of mind along the way all go into the score.

The Ministry of Science and ICT announced the Agentic AI Initiative on July 23 at the 11th Science and Technology Ministers' Meeting, jointly with other ministries. Three strategies hold it up: leading on safety and trust, opening a base for execution, and driving adoption from the demand side. The first reaches a company's daily work soonest. A safety and trust guideline covering personal data, permission management and goal drift was promised within the year, with the datasets and benchmark tools to measure performance and safety alongside it.

The testing side is already under way. A verification project with the National Information Society Agency carries 1.8 billion won, and the consortium confirmed in September raised the dataset from the 5,000 cases asked for to more than 7,000. Scenarios take in data that moves in real time, like prices and booking slots.

The drafting side keeps another pace. A zero trust guide for agentic AI arrived on November 6 from the ministry and the Korea Internet and Security Agency, while the guideline itself is unpublished as of November 17. The deadline the government set is the end of the year, so the time has not run out. For a company putting an agent into service before then, it is the one official document on the desk.

The guide names four places to check: where input is taken in, where a tool is called, where a task is passed on, where something is sent outside. Turn "check at each place" toward the record and it asks for something to be left behind at each. Nothing can be scored before it exists.

## Facebook (EN)

Ask how much work an agent handled last month and what usually comes back is a single rate.

Seven out of ten.

What the other three were is not inside that number. A case that stopped because the permission was not there, a case the agent passed to a person, and a case it simply got wrong all sit in the same column.

I thought about that column while reading the Agentic AI Initiative that Korea's government announced on July 23, at the 11th Science and Technology Ministers' Meeting. The idea is to build up, at a national level, AI that takes a goal, plans its own route, calls outside tools and finishes the job.

Of the three strategies, safety and trust was placed first.

What held my attention was the scorecard being assembled underneath it. It does not stop at whether the final answer was right. The plan it drew up, the order it reached for its tools, and why it changed course partway all go into the score.

That a job came out right and that the route to it was sound are two different sentences.

A zero trust guide followed on November 6. Where input is taken in. Where a tool is called. Where a task is passed on. Where something is sent outside. At every place the agent moves on its own, the guide asks for a step that checks.

Turn "check at each place" around and face it toward the record, and one question is left standing.

"Of the work your agent did last month, how many cases went to a person? Could you pull that list today?"

Measuring the same training data twice, against one yardstick that did not move in between, is routine work for DataClinic at Pebblous. Raise the model or swap a connected tool and the score you already hold no longer carries. The two numbers speak to each other only once that yardstick has been run again.

The guideline itself is still unpublished, and the deadline set for it is the end of the year.

The list, though, is one a company can pull while the document is being written.
