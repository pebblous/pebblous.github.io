## LinkedIn (KO)

AI가 내린 인사조치를 두고 부당하다는 말이 나왔을 때 근거를 대야 하는 쪽을 정해 둔 조항은, 한국에서 법보다 단체협약에 먼저 들어갔다.

금속노조와 금속산업사용자협의회가 10월 27일 2026년 금속산업 중앙교섭 조인식에서 'AI 도입 시 노동인권 및 고용 보호' 조항을 새로 넣은 산별협약에 서명했다. 사용자는 작업공정이나 인사관리에 AI를 들이기 전에 조합에 알려야 한다. 조합이 요청하면 고용과 노동에 미칠 영향을 노사가 함께 점검하고, 대책에 합의해야 비로소 도입할 수 있다. 노조는 AI를 노동 친화적으로 쓰도록 강제한 국내 첫 초기업 산별협약이라고 이 조항을 규정했다.

협약은 회원사 69곳에 적용된다. 조합원 투표에서는 86.4%가 찬성했는데, 그 표가 추인한 것은 AI 조항 하나가 아니라 통상시급 인상까지 묶인 중앙교섭 결과 전체다.

설명을 요구하는 제도 자체가 여기서 처음 나온 것은 아니다. 올해 1월 전면 시행된 AI기본법도 고영향 인공지능 사업자에게 결과와 주요 기준, 학습용 데이터 개요를 설명할 방안을 세우라고 한다. 갈리는 자리는 세 군데다. 법이 조문에 이름으로 적은 예시는 채용과 대출심사여서 뽑은 뒤의 배치와 평가, 승진과 징계는 포함 여부가 불명확하다. 법이 준 설명 요구권은 결과가 나온 다음 불복하면서 쓰는 권리인데, 협약은 그 시점을 도입을 결정하기 전으로 당겼다. 그리고 다툼이 났을 때 근거를 댈 쪽을 정한 조항은 법에 없다.

세 가지 차이가 회사에 요구하는 것은 결국 하나다. 그 판단이 무엇을 보고 나왔는지가 나중에도 남아 있어야 한다. 학습에 쓴 자료가 어느 해 어떤 평가 지침 아래 기록된 것인지, 개별 판단의 입력값과 결과가 짝으로 보관되는지, 모델을 교체한 이력이 남는지. 모델 성능과 무관한 항목들이고, 도입하고 나서 뒤늦게 만들어 넣기 가장 어려운 항목들이기도 하다.

노사는 이 협약의 효력을 다른 산업과 업종, 지역으로 넓혀 달라고 정부에 함께 요구했다. 같은 문구를 다음 교섭에서 꺼낼 노조가 금속산업 바깥에도 있다면, 준비가 끝나 있어야 하는 시점은 협약을 맺은 다음이 아니라 그 전이다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

In Korea, the rule naming which side has to justify an AI-driven HR decision arrived in a collective agreement before it arrived in a statute.

On October 27, at the signing ceremony for the 2026 central bargaining round in Korea's metal industry, the Korean Metal Workers' Union and the Korea Metal Industry Employers' Association signed an industry-wide agreement carrying a new clause on protecting labor rights and employment when AI is adopted. Before AI goes into a work process or into HR management, the employer has to notify the union; if the union asks, the two sides examine jointly what the adoption will do to jobs and working conditions; and the system goes in only once they have agreed on the measures. The union described it as the first supra-enterprise industrial agreement in Korea to compel labor-friendly use of AI.

The clause binds 69 member companies. In the members' ballot 86.4% voted in favor, and what that vote ratified was the whole bargaining round, wage settlement included, not the AI clause on its own.

A duty to explain is not new here. The AI Framework Act, in full force since January, already requires operators of high-impact AI to set out a plan for explaining the outcome, the main standards behind it and the shape of the training data. Three things part. The uses the Act names in its own text are hiring and loan screening, so assignment, appraisal, promotion and discipline after the hire sit in an unclear place. The right to demand an explanation is a right exercised after a result lands and someone objects, while the agreement moves that moment to before the decision to adopt. And no provision of the Act says who carries the burden once a dispute arises.

What the three differences ask of a company comes down to one thing: what the judgment looked at has to still be on hand afterwards. Which year and which appraisal guideline the training records were written under. Whether the inputs and the output of a single decision are kept as a pair. Whether there is a log of the model being swapped or retrained. None of that moves with model performance, and none of it is easy to manufacture after the system is already running.

Both sides asked the government to extend the force of the agreement to other industries, sectors and regions. If unions outside the metal industry bring the same wording to their next round, the preparation has to be finished before an agreement is signed, not after.

## Facebook (EN)

Someone is told their assignment has changed and asks why.

"That is what the system scored."

After that answer, there is not much left to say.

On October 27 the Korean Metal Workers' Union and the Korea Metal Industry Employers' Association signed an industry-wide agreement. The clause written into it decides in advance who has to answer at that moment.

Where AI is used in an HR action, the standards and the procedure behind it have to be made public, and where someone calls the action unfair, the side that has to present reasonable grounds is the employer.

Nor can the adoption be settled alone. Whether the AI runs a process or manages people, the employer notifies the union first; if the union asks, both sides work out together what it will do to jobs and working conditions; and it goes in only once they have agreed on the measures.

What stayed with me was not how strong the clause is but what it assumes.

To answer a demand for grounds, the material that would make the answer has to still be there.

'The material of an explanation.' It does not live inside the model.

What the system was taught on. Which year and which appraisal guideline those records were written under. Whether the inputs and the output of one person's case are kept as a pair. None of it moves when you swap in a better model, and all of it is hardest to manufacture once the system is already running.

Pebblous puts a number on the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. Giving grounds for a single decision means bringing out the lineage of the data that decision stands on.

Had this demand come as regulation, it would have come with a start date and a grace period. Coming through bargaining, it can be put to you at any time.

"One decision from last month: can you bring it back today and give the grounds for it?"

That the question has no date on it is what stays with me longest.
