## LinkedIn (KO)

보안회사가 같은 날 AI 스타트업 두 곳을 사들였는데, 큰 돈이 간 쪽은 공격을 막는 회사가 아니었다.

크라우드스트라이크가 11월 3일 바르셀로나 팰컨 유럽 행사에서 피들러AI와 드레드노드 인수를 나란히 발표했다. 회사는 금액을 밝히지 않았고, 월스트리트저널이 두 건을 합쳐 약 4억 9,500만 달러로 보도했다. 그 가운데 다섯 중 넷가량이 에이전트가 무엇을 읽고 무엇을 실행했는지 적어 두는 피들러AI 쪽이다.

왜 적어 두는 쪽이 더 비쌌나. 조지 커츠 최고경영자가 발표문에 쓴 문장이 답에 가깝다. 규제기관이나 이사회가 에이전트가 무엇을 했느냐고 물을 때 '모델이 그랬다'는 답이 되지 않는다는 것이다. 그는 피들러를 모든 에이전트의 비행기록장치라고 불렀다. 비행기록장치는 사고를 막는 장비가 아니라 사고 뒤에 경위를 말해 주는 장비다.

발표문은 그 앞에 전제를 하나 더 깔아 둔다. 에이전트는 침해당해야만 위험한 것이 아니다. 정상 자격증명으로 접속하고 승인된 도구만 쓰면서도 의도하지 않은 조치를 실행할 수 있다. 그러면 사고 뒤의 물음은 누가 뚫었나가 아니라 어느 단계에서 어긋났나가 되고, 뒤쪽 물음에 답하는 자료는 기록뿐이다.

기록 쪽 사정은 아직 덜 정리돼 있다. 배포 전 시험에서 찾은 결함은 OWASP의 대규모언어모델 10대 위험이나 마이터 아틀라스처럼 이름이 정해진 공개 규격에 걸어 주고받을 수 있다. 운영 중 에이전트가 남기는 기록에는 그만한 합의가 아직 없고, 피들러AI가 쓰는 오픈텔레메트리의 생성형 AI 항목도 개발 단계로 표시돼 있어 이름과 범위가 바뀔 수 있다.

페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 항목별로 진단한다. 무엇을 어떤 이름의 칸에 적을지가 정해져야 진단 결과를 주고받을 수 있는데, 에이전트 운영 기록은 그 칸 이름이 아직 움직이는 영역이다. 팰컨 옵서브가 나오는 분기는 이미 시작됐지만, 제품을 들이는 날과 기록이 시작되는 날은 같지 않다. 그사이 남기지 않은 로그는 소급해 만들어지지 않는다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

A security company bought two AI startups on the same day, and the larger check did not go to the one that blocks attacks.

CrowdStrike announced both agreements, for Fiddler AI and Dreadnode, at its Falcon Europe event in Barcelona on November 3. It did not disclose the price; the Wall Street Journal put the two deals together at about $495 million. Roughly four of every five of those dollars went to Fiddler AI, which writes down what an agent read and what it ran.

Why did the writing-down side cost more. The sentence George Kurtz used in the announcement comes closest. When a regulator or a board asks what an agent did, he said, saying the model did it is not an answer, and he called Fiddler the flight recorder for every agent. A flight recorder does not prevent an accident. It tells you what happened after one.

The announcement lays one more premise underneath that. An agent does not have to be compromised to be dangerous. It signs in to internal applications with legitimate credentials, uses approved tools, and can still carry out an action nobody intended. The question left after an incident is then not who got in but which step went off course, and only the record answers the second.

The record side is the less settled of the two. A flaw found in pre-deployment testing can be hung on a named public framework, the OWASP Top 10 for LLMs or MITRE ATLAS, and passed between teams under that name. The traces an agent leaves while it is running have no comparable agreement, and even the generative AI entries in OpenTelemetry, which Fiddler AI instruments with, are still marked Development.

Pebblous itemizes the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. Handing that work to someone else depends on prior agreement about what belongs under which name, and for agent operating records those names are still moving. Falcon Observe arrives in a quarter that has already begun, but the day a product is installed and the day a record starts are not the same day. Logs never written in between do not get generated retroactively.

## Facebook (EN)

Think back to a day an internal agent approved one refund it should not have.

Finding the cause means pulling up which documents it read that day, and which tools it called in what order.

Where that record lives is a question fewer teams can answer on the spot than you might expect.

On November 3, in Barcelona, CrowdStrike announced two acquisitions of AI security startups side by side.

One of them attacks models and agents directly, before they go into service, to find where they break. The other writes down what an agent read and what it ran.

By the reported figures, the second cost more than four times as much.

George Kurtz, the chief executive, called that second company the flight recorder for every agent.

A flight recorder prevents nothing. It tells you what happened after the fact.

That a security company put its largest check on the explaining side rather than the blocking side is the part of this announcement that stayed in view.

There is one more premise underneath it, written into the same announcement. An agent does not have to be compromised to be dangerous.

It signs in with legitimate credentials and uses approved tools, and an action nobody intended gets carried out anyway. The question left afterwards is not who got in but which step went off course.

Pebblous tabulates the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. Work like that rests on knowing which name each thing gets written under, and for agent operating records nobody has settled the names yet.

"What did our agents read last month, and can we pull it up today?"

The day a product arrives and the day a record begins are not the same day. The stretch nobody logged in between is not filled in later by any tool you bring in.
