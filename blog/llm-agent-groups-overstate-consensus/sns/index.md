## LinkedIn (KO)

사람이 실제로 나눈 토론을 LLM 에이전트 조로 다시 돌리자, 전원이 발언한 45개 조에서 완전합의율이 사람 51.1%에 에이전트 85.2~95.6%로 갈렸습니다.

와세다대 글로벌교육센터의 사오 텅페이가 9월 17일 arXiv에 올린 사전등록 연구입니다. 공개 코퍼스 델리데이터에 기록된 웨이슨 카드 과제 토론 100개 조를 봉인해 두고, 참가자 한 명마다 에이전트 하나를 세워 그 참가자가 토론 전에 혼자 쓴 답을 심었습니다. 에이전트는 정답도, 다른 참가자의 말도, 사람들이 실제로 나눈 대화도 받지 못했습니다.

격차의 한 축은 침묵입니다. 사람 참가자의 20.1%는 토론 내내 한 글자도 올리지 않았고, 똑같이 넘어갈 권한을 받은 에이전트가 침묵한 비율은 0.2%였습니다. 사람 조에서 끝까지 대화에 오르지 않는 의견이 에이전트 조에서는 거의 매 턴 반박에 노출됩니다.

합의가 잘 됐다는 것이 잘 풀었다는 뜻은 아닙니다. 카드 넉 장을 논리 구조만 남기고 무의미한 낱말로 갈아 끼우자 추론 모드 에이전트 조의 합의율은 98.7%로 거의 그대로였는데, 오답에 합의한 조가 12.0%에서 74.0%로 올라갔습니다. 저자의 표현으로는 시뮬레이션된 합의가 집단 정확도를 따라가지 않았습니다.

논문이 그어 놓은 한계도 같이 읽어야 합니다. 사전등록된 확증 검정은 100개 조 전체 비교이고, 위의 44.4%포인트는 봉인을 연 뒤 채택한 참여 맞춤 값으로 두 명에서 다섯 명 규모의 조에 한정됩니다. 정답이 없는 채용 윤리 토론을 같은 장치로 돌렸을 때는 반대 방향으로 실패했습니다. 에이전트가 주입된 의견을 사람보다 더 고집스럽게 붙들어 의견이 사람 조만큼 좁혀지지 않았습니다.

설문과 포커스그룹을 에이전트로 대체하려는 팀이 확인할 것은 합의율이 아닙니다. 소수 의견이 몇 개 살아남았는지, 사람의 침묵이 침묵으로 기록됐는지입니다. 페블러스가 데이터 품질을 정확도보다 분포의 문제로 먼저 보는 까닭도 그쪽에 있습니다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Replay a discussion people actually had with a group of LLM agents, and in the 45 groups where every member spoke, full consensus lands at 51.1% for the humans and between 85.2% and 95.6% for the agents, depending on the mode.

The study is a preregistered replication posted to arXiv on September 17 by Tengfei Shao of the Global Education Center at Waseda University. One hundred groups from DeliData, a public corpus of Wason card-task discussions, were sealed before analysis. Each participant was given one agent, seeded with the answer that participant had written down alone before the discussion, and no agent saw the answer key, the other participants' answers, or the conversation the humans had.

Silence is one axis of the gap. Of the human participants, 20.1% never posted a word, while the agents, who held the same right to pass, stayed silent 0.2% of the time. An opinion that never reaches the conversation in a human group is exposed to challenge almost every turn in an agent group.

Agreeing is not the same as getting it right. When the four cards were swapped for neutral words that leave the logic intact, consensus in reasoning-mode agent groups held at 98.7%, but the share of groups agreeing on a wrong answer rose from 12.0% to 74.0%. As the author puts it, simulated consensus did not track collective accuracy.

The limits the paper draws deserve equal weight. The preregistered confirmatory test is the full 100-group comparison, and the 44.4-point figure above is a participation-matched value adopted after unblinding, bounded to groups of two to five members. A follow-up run on an open-ended discussion about the ethics of AI in hiring failed in the opposite direction: agents held their seeded opinions more stubbornly than people and never narrowed the spread as far.

For teams moving surveys and focus groups onto agents, the number to audit is not the consensus rate. It is how many minority positions survived, and whether human silence was recorded as silence. That is why Pebblous treats data quality as a question about distributions before accuracy.

## Facebook (EN)

I have sat through a meeting without saying a single word.

Not because I disagreed. It was simply hard to start.

The minutes listed me as present, and under the conclusion someone typed: no objections.

A study that went past my feed a few days ago brought that meeting back. Tengfei Shao at Waseda University took 100 groups of people working through a logic puzzle and replayed every discussion, with one agent standing in for each participant.

One human participant in five posted nothing at all. Among the agents, who were given the same right to pass, silence came up 0.2% of the time.

There is one phrase I keep reaching for: the filled blank. A synthetic respondent takes the seat a person left empty and fills it with a diligent contribution, and once filled, the seat cannot be told apart later.

The blank was never a defect. It is what remains of someone who was tired, or uninterested, or found it hard to speak. This is why Pebblous looks at data quality as a question about distributions before it is a question about accuracy. The moment emptiness is treated as a gap to be filled, whatever that emptiness was recording disappears.

"Where, exactly, does silence get recorded in our data?"

Sometimes the one opinion that never narrowed tells you more than the line in the minutes saying no one objected.
