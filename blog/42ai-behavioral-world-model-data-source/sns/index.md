## LinkedIn (KO)

사람의 다음 행동을 확률로 맞힌다는 모델이 이스라엘에서 스텔스를 벗었다. 그 모델이 올라선 데이터 한 층은 원래 의학 연구를 위해 모인 자료다.

42AI가 11월 2일 시드 6250만 달러와 함께 내건 '행동 세계모형' 이야기다. 대형 증권사 열 곳이 이미 이 모델로 고객의 거래 방향과 물량을 예측해 유동성과 마진 요구량, 헤징 노출을 관리한다. 회사가 내놓은 성적표는 자체 집계이고, 같은 조건의 외부 검증은 함께 나오지 않았다.

성능보다 먼저 읽을 값은 분모 쪽에 있다. 학습 데이터는 한 덩어리가 아니라 성격이 다른 세 층으로 쌓였다. 맨 아래는 심리 평가와 행동 시나리오로 42AI가 직접 관찰한 수천 명이다.

가운데는 정부 데이터셋과 바이오뱅크에서 받아 온 200만 명 넘는 자료이고, 맨 위는 증권사가 영업하며 쌓아 둔 고객 기록이다. 셋을 합치면 거의 3000만 명이 된다.

가운데 층만 출신이 다르다. 바이오뱅크는 생체 시료와 건강 기록을 의학 연구에 쓰라고 세운 기관이고, 정부 통계도 공중보건과 정책 설계를 명분으로 모인다. 거래 예측은 그 동의서에 적혀 있던 쓰임새가 아니다. 어느 나라의 어떤 자료를 어떤 절차로 받았는지, 2차 이용을 허용한 근거가 무엇인지는 공개되지 않았다.

프라이버시 질문에 회사는 모델이 그 사람이 누구인지 결코 모른다고 답했다. 같은 기사는 한 문장 뒤에, 그럼에도 고객 활동에 연결된 개인 단위 행동 프로필이 만들어지는 것으로 보인다고 적었다. 식별자를 지우는 일과 사람을 구별하지 않는 일은 다른 작업이다. 성향 점수는 계좌에 붙고, 계좌와 사람을 잇는 표는 증권사 쪽에 이미 있다.

페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 따져 진단한다. 진단서가 참인 범위는 파일이 아니라 자리다. 내보낼 때 안전하다고 매긴 표도 식별 키를 쥔 조직에 닿으면 그 판정부터 다시 계산해야 한다.

이 사례에서 새로 발명된 기술은 찾기 어렵다. 달라진 것은 데이터에 손이 닿는 범위다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

An Israeli startup that says it can put a probability on what a person will do next came out of stealth. One layer of the data this model stands on was gathered for medical research.

42AI announced $62.5 million in seed funding on November 2 and calls the technology a behavioral world model. Ten large brokerages already run it to predict which way and how much their clients will trade, and to manage liquidity, margin requirements and hedging exposure. The scorecard it offers is its own, with no external verification alongside it.

The number to read before the performance claims sits in the denominator. The training data is not one block but three layers. At the bottom are a few thousand people 42AI studied itself through psychological assessments.

In the middle are government datasets and biobank records covering more than two million people. At the top are records the brokerages had already accumulated in business. Together they come to almost thirty million.

Only the middle layer is out of place. A biobank exists to gather biological samples and health records for medical research; government statistics are collected in the name of public health. Trade prediction was not among the uses written into those consent forms. Which country, which datasets, on what basis secondary use was permitted: none of it was disclosed.

Asked about privacy, the company said the model never knows who the person is. One sentence later the same report noted that individual behavioral profiles still appear to be built from linked customer activity. Erasing an identifier is one job. Declining to tell people apart is another. Trait scores attach to an account, and the table joining accounts to names is already the brokerage's.

Pebblous assays the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. Where the data sits is part of the verdict rather than a detail outside it. A table cleared on its way out has to be scored again once it reaches an organization holding the linking key.

There is no new invention in this case. What widened is the reach into data.

## Facebook (EN)

"Used for research purposes only."

I have read that line and signed under it. The uses I pictured at the time were papers and clinics.

On November 2 an Israeli startup called 42AI came out of stealth and described what its model was trained on.

At the bottom sit a few thousand people studied directly through psychological assessments. Above them sit government datasets and biobank records covering more than two million people. At the top sit the customer records of brokerages. Almost thirty million in total.

Ten large brokerages run the model. They use it to see which way and how much a client is likely to trade, and to set liquidity, margin and hedging accordingly.

Only the middle layer comes from somewhere else.

A biobank exists to hold biological samples and health records for medical research. Trade prediction was not one of the uses written on that consent form.

The company said the model never knows who the person is. One sentence later the same report observed that individual profiles do still appear to be assembled from linked customer activity.

"A borrowed layer."

That is what I ended up calling the middle one while reading. The data did not change. Its location did, and what widened was not the modeling but the reach into the data.

The work I do at Pebblous runs through DataClinic, which puts a measured number beside the quality of AI training data. One thing that keeps showing up in it is that a verdict of safe cannot be reached by looking at the data alone. A table with its identifiers stripped becomes people again once it arrives somewhere that holds the account numbers.

"The data we took in from outside: what was it originally gathered for?"

Once everything is merged, tracing which row arrived under which consent gets hard. That column can only be written before the merge.
