## LinkedIn (KO)

소프트웨어를 만드는 회사가 첫 투자금으로 가장 먼저 한 일이 센서 장치 설계였습니다.

샌프란시스코 스타트업 노에티브가 9월 16일 스텔스를 벗고 4,100만 달러 규모의 첫 투자 유치를 알렸습니다. 이클립스가 라운드를 이끌었습니다. 제품은 둘로 나뉩니다. 하나는 고객사가 이미 깔아 둔 소프트웨어 위에 얹혀 돌아가며 운영을 배우는 AI입니다. 다른 하나는 회사가 직접 설계했다는 다중모달 센싱 포드입니다. 그 장치에 무엇이 들어 있는지는 공개되지 않았습니다.

하드웨어를 짊어지면 비용이 늘고 배포가 느려집니다. 그런데 공장과 물류 창고는 설비도, 설비가 내보내는 신호의 형식도 회사마다 다릅니다. 소프트웨어만 들고 들어가면 고객사마다 연동을 처음부터 다시 만들어야 하고, 그렇게 모은 데이터는 제각각인 채로 남습니다. 같은 규격의 장치를 함께 넣으면 현장이 달라도 관측값은 같은 규격으로 나옵니다. 이 해석은 회사 발표문이 아니라 한 업계 매체가 붙인 것입니다.

공개된 성과는 한 곳뿐입니다. 초기 협업 기업으로 이름을 올린 식음료 제조사 스튜벤 푸즈입니다. 대표는 한 달에 한 번 일주일씩 걸리던 생산 계획을 이제 매일 수 분이면 끝낸다고 말했습니다. 불량률이나 비용이 어떻게 달라졌는지는 이 발언에도, 다른 자료에도 없습니다.

회사가 겨냥한다고 밝힌 30조 달러 물리 경제 역시 산정 근거가 공개돼 있지 않습니다. 지금 확인되는 것은 이 회사가 모델을 키우는 쪽이 아니라 현장을 계측하는 쪽에 먼저 돈을 썼다는 순서입니다. 데이터가 있다는 말과 데이터가 배울 수 있는 형태로 남아 있다는 말은 다릅니다. 페블러스가 AI-Ready Data를 이야기할 때 이 구별을 앞에 두는 것도 같은 자리에서 시작합니다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

A software company spent the opening of its first funding round on designing a sensor.

Noetive, a startup in San Francisco, came out of stealth on 16 September and announced $41 million in first-round funding led by Eclipse. The product has two halves. One is an AI that rides on top of the software a customer already runs and learns how the operation works. The other is a multi-modal sensing pod the company says it designed itself. What sits inside that pod has not been disclosed.

Carrying hardware raises costs and slows deployment. Yet no two factories or warehouses hold their equipment, or the signal formats that equipment emits, in the same shape. Software alone means rebuilding an integration at every customer, and the data that comes back stays in a different shape at each site. Put the same device on every floor and the readings come out to one specification. That reading belongs to a trade publication rather than to the company's announcement.

One result has been published. Steuben Foods, a food and drink manufacturer working with Noetive as an early partner, says planning that used to take a week once a month now takes minutes each day. Neither that account nor any other released material puts a figure on defect rates or cost.

The $30 trillion the company assigns to the physical economy arrives without a published basis as well. Only the order of the spending can be confirmed today: measuring the floor came before any work on a larger model. Data that exists and data left in a form something can learn from are two different things, and Pebblous puts that distinction first when it talks about AI-Ready data.

## Facebook (EN)

The machine ran a little slower than usual this afternoon.

Someone who has worked that line for years hears it. They ease the speed down and pass a word to the next station.

That judgment lands on no screen anywhere.

Noetive, a startup in San Francisco, came out of stealth on 16 September and announced $41 million in first-round funding, led by Eclipse. The announcement names two products. An AI that runs alongside the software a customer already has, and a sensing pod the company says it designed itself. Noetive calls the pod its 'eyes and ears'.

For a software company, building a device and sending it into someone else's factory is the worse choice on nearly every count. Design and manufacturing cost money, and every new customer means a person travelling out to install. A trade publication read the reasoning this way: equipment and record formats differ from plant to plant, so putting the same device on every floor is the only route to readings in one common format.

Factories are not short of records. Output is tallied, equipment histories are kept, inspection results accumulate. Those records take the shape a person needs in order to read and judge. The context that decides the matter usually goes unwritten and stays in the head of whoever was on the floor. So does the anomaly heard in the opening scene.

"Of everything that happened at our site today, how much has been left in a form an AI can read next year?"

New sensors are not the only route to an answer. Recording when a value was made, on which machine and under what conditions, is already part of one. It is why Pebblous separates data that exists from data left in a form something can learn from when it talks about AI-Ready data.

Whether Noetive judged it right will take a few years to show. We noticed the order more than the answer. Before asking which model to use, this company reached for what gets left behind as data.
