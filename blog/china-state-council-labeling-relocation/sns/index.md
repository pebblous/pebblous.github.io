이어받는다고 옮긴 원문의 동사는 승접(承接)이다. 중국의 산업 정책 문서에서 공장 공정이 다른 지역으로 넘어갈 때 쓰던 말이고, 이번에 그 자리에 학습 데이터를 만드는 일이 들어갔다. 라벨링 기지를 맡을 도시 일곱 곳은 2024년 5월에 이미 지정됐고 그 가운데 넷이 중부와 서부에 있다. 이번 문건이 더한 것은 두 권역에 같은 산업의 다른 층을 맡긴 분업이고, 그 문장은 산업 육성 문서가 아니라 고용 문서에 적혔다.

같은 문건의 일곱 번째 항은 AI 사업에 고용 위험 평가를 붙이라고 하면서 대상을 두 문장으로 가른다. 인허가가 걸린 영역은 사업 심사와 동시에 평가하고, 그 밖의 영역은 실정에 맞게 한다. 라벨링은 면허 없이 여는 업종이라 뒤 문장에 걸린다. 평가 대상에서 빠진다는 뜻은 아니다. 갈리는 것은 대상이 아니라 평가가 붙는 시점과 절차다.

디지털화 관리사와 AI 훈련사를 새 직업군으로 전한 국내 보도가 많았는데, 두 이름은 2019년과 2020년에 이미 국가 직업 분류에 올라 있다. 조문이 쓴 동사는 확대이고, 확대의 목적어는 수요다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

China's State Council General Office published an employment opinion on October 23 whose very first clause names regions for data labeling. The eastern provinces are to hold the core AI industries. The central and western provinces are to take on labeling and smart-terminal manufacturing.

The verb in the original is chengjie (承接), the word Chinese industrial policy reaches for when a production process moves in from somewhere else. Seven cities had already been designated labeling bases in May 2024, four of them inland. What this document adds is the division itself: two halves of the country assigned different layers of one industry. And the sentence sits in an employment document rather than an industrial one.

Point seven of the same document asks for an employment risk assessment on AI projects and splits the target across two sentences. Where market entry is licensed, the assessment runs alongside the project review. For everything else, it is to be carried out according to actual conditions. Labeling needs no license to open, which puts it in the second sentence. That is not an exemption. What differs is not the target but the moment the assessment attaches.

Much of the Korean coverage framed digitalization managers and AI trainers as newly created occupations. Both names entered China's national occupational classification years ago, in 2019 and 2020. The verb in the clause is expand, and what it expands is demand.

## Facebook (EN)

"Take on."

In Chinese industrial policy writing, that verb usually belongs to factories. One region sets a production process down, another picks it up.

This time what gets picked up is data labeling.

It is the first clause of the employment opinion the State Council General Office published on October 23. The eastern provinces are to hold the core AI industries. The central and western provinces are to take on labeling and smart-terminal manufacturing.

Assigning regions to the work of building training data is not new in itself. Seven cities were named labeling bases in May 2024, four of them inland. What this document adds is a sentence that stands the two halves of the country on different floors of the same industry.

The part I stayed with was not that sentence. It was point seven.

It asks for an employment risk assessment on AI projects, and it runs to two sentences. The first: where market entry is licensed, the assessment runs alongside the project review. The second: everywhere else, it is to be carried out according to actual conditions.

Opening a labeling business requires no license. That low threshold is part of why a new region can start there.

In the assessment, the same property works the other way. With no review to ride on, no moment is named for the assessment to attach to.

This is not an exemption. The second sentence does ask for the assessment. What differs is the when.

Of the two columns that clause draws, labeling sits in the second one. The column without a threshold.

And a phrase we use every week came back to me. Data preparation cost.

We use it as a procurement line. Quotes, unit prices, delivery dates. In that document the same work is a line in regional employment policy.

"Which grade of skill produced the labels we received?"

China's national occupational skill standard for AI trainers separates skill into five grades. Data labeling is written as work for the bottom two. The grades are there. Whether the order ever asks about them is another matter.
