# Each CSAT AI Benchmark Crowns a Different Perfect Scorer

_On a Soonchunhyang University student_

## Executive Summary

> [!callout]
> Thirty-five AI models have been handed this year's Korean university entrance exam and graded on it. The [CSAT-benchmark](https://hehee9.github.io/CSAT-benchmark/) dashboard, which scores Korean and foreign models on the 2027 academic-year College Scholastic Ability Test, went up on November 20, and the person who set that exam paper is Koo Yu-gyeom, a student in the computer software engineering department at Soonchunhyang University. Not the state institute that writes the exam, and not a government agency.

> Two models took the full 1,276 points there, the sum of raw scores across every subject on offer: OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra and Google's Gemini 4 Neon. A second individual benchmark on GitHub graded the same exam, and on that one the only model with full marks is Claude Fable 5.2. The totals differ before the models do, 1,276 against 1,226, and the difference comes from which subjects each person put inside the exam.

> Sections 1 to 4 set out what the published scorecards and the reporting confirm. Section 5, which reads the situation as a problem of evaluation data, is this blog's own judgment.

## Two Exam Papers, Two Sets of Rules

Koo Yu-gyeom, who publishes under the handle hehee9, has graded the major AI models on the CSAT every year since 2024 and posted the results. This is the third run. This time 35 models were given the whole 2027 exam: every elective in Korean language and mathematics, English, Korean history and all 17 inquiry subjects. Out of a full-subject raw-score total of 1,276, GPT-6.2 Astra and Gemini 4 Neon took every point.

Koo is not the only person who set an exam paper this year. The GitHub user GonGe1018 published 27suneung-bench, which puts the full-subject total at 1,226 points, and there exactly one model answered all 1,226: Claude Fable 5.2. GPT-6.2 Astra, perfect on the first paper, sits second here with 1,223, followed by Claude Opus 5.5 on 1,221 and GPT-6.2 Sol on 1,220.
