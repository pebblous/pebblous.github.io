신규 채용이 아니라 이미 IBM에 있는 컨설턴트 수만 명을 몇 달에 걸쳐 다시 가르치고 인증하는 계획입니다. GPT-5.6과 Codex는 IBM 컨설턴트 13만 명이 쓰는 딜리버리 플랫폼에 들어가고, 재교육된 인력이 향하는 곳은 금융·정부·통신 고객사의 재무와 조달, 인사 업무입니다.

모델 회사가 컨설팅 채널을 확보하는 이유는 매출 구조에 있습니다. 포레스터는 IBM 컨설팅 매출의 최소 40%가 파트너와 연관돼 있다고 봅니다. 고객의 업무 시스템을 이미 만지고 있는 조직에 기술을 얹는 편이, 금융과 정부에 직접 영업 조직을 세우는 것보다 빠릅니다.

정작 데이터 접근 범위와 로그, 보존에 관한 조항은 공개된 문서 어디에도 없습니다. 두 달 전 같은 두 회사가 낸 사이버보안 서비스에는 코드 저장소에 읽기 전용으로 접근하고 실행 범위를 제한한다는 문장이 있었지만, 8월에 넓어진 업무에는 아직 그에 해당하는 문장이 없습니다.

접근 범위를 정해 두어도 데이터가 어떤 손을 거쳤는지가 기록에 남지 않으면 지켰는지 확인할 방법이 없습니다. 페블러스가 데이터 품질을 진단하면서 이력을 함께 보는 이유도 같은 자리에 있습니다. 도입하는 쪽이 먼저 정할 것은 어느 모델을 쓸지가 아니라, 재교육된 인력이 우리 데이터의 어디까지 볼 수 있고 그 기록이 누구에게 남는지입니다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

The number IBM attached to its OpenAI partnership on August 13 was not a model spec. It was a headcount.

IBM will train and certify tens of thousands of consultants it already employs over the coming months, retraining rather than hiring. GPT-5.6, Codex, and ChatGPT Work go into IBM Consulting Advantage, the delivery platform 130,000 IBM consultants work in, and the retrained staff head into finance, procurement, and HR work at clients in financial services, government, telecom, and retail.

The commercial logic sits in the revenue mix. Forrester puts at least 40% of IBM's consulting revenue in partner-linked work. Layering a model onto an organization already inside the client's systems is faster than a model vendor building its own route into banks and government agencies.

What no published document covers is data access scope, logging, or retention. The cybersecurity service the two companies launched in June was explicit on that point: read-only access to code repositories, limited execution, everything running inside the client environment. August's scope is wider and has no equivalent sentence yet.

Access rules are only as good as the record showing they were kept, which is the ground Pebblous works on: data quality diagnosed alongside the lineage that makes it auditable. The first thing a buyer should settle is not which model to run, but how far a retrained consultant can see into their data and where that trace is kept.

## Facebook (EN)

The line most buyers linger over in a review memo is model performance and unit price.

But what walks into the room next quarter is not a model. It is people.

IBM announced its OpenAI partnership on August 13. Neither company disclosed the terms, and the one item carrying a number was headcount: tens of thousands of consultants to be retrained and certified over the coming months.

They are headed into finance, procurement, and HR work at clients in financial services, government, telecom, and retail. The teams placed inside those clients include data science roles, not consultants alone.

Two months earlier, the same two companies shipped a cybersecurity service with a sentence in it. Read-only access to code repositories, limited execution, running inside the client's environment.

August's wider scope has no equivalent sentence yet. Redesigning a procurement process or automating HR work does not end at reading. It writes data, produces intermediate artifacts, and rewrites the rules of the work itself.

Writing the piece, I started calling what stays behind the "residue." Summaries, the operational data that went into prompts, whatever was used for fine-tuning. Things that outlive the engagement.

Which left me circling two questions.

"How far into our data can a retrained consultant see? And where does that trace live?"

Without a record, neither question has an answer. That is the ground Pebblous works on, diagnosing data quality alongside the lineage that makes it auditable.

Where the name on the contract and the hands on the data come apart, what remains later is not which model was chosen. It is what was written down in between.
