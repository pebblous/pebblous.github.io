## LinkedIn (KO)

SAP가 10월 6일 인수에 합의한 벨기에 테크울프는 직원에게 보유 기술을 묻지 않는다. 인사 시스템과 지라, 이수한 교육처럼 이미 쌓여 있는 기록을 읽고 거기서 기술을 역산한다.

이렇게 만들어지는 '일의 맥락 그래프'는 세 층으로 되어 있다. 직무를 그 안의 과업 단위까지 쪼갠 층, 사람이 보유하고 실제로 쓰는 기술 층, 외부 노동시장 층이다. SAP 오토노머스 스위트 사장 겸 최고제품책임자 마노지 스와미나탄은 이 그래프가 인재 관리에 관한 에이전트 질의에 "훌륭한 근거 층"을 제공한다고 했다. 인사 AI가 답을 지어내지 않도록 붙잡아 두는 사실의 바닥을 사들인 셈이다. 거래 조건은 공개되지 않았고, 규제 승인을 거쳐 4분기에 종결될 예정이다.

받침대가 단단할수록 거기 적힌 한 줄의 무게도 커진다. 테크울프는 직원이 자동 생성된 자기 기술 프로필을 보고 고치는 도구를 두고 있고, 검증의 기본값도 직원이라고 FAQ에 적어 두었다. 다만 같은 답의 뒷문장이 조건을 하나 붙인다. 관리자가 먼저 검증하는 더 엄격한 경로를 고객사가 도입할 때 고를 수 있다는 것이다. 자기 기술 한 줄을 자기가 먼저 손볼 수 있는지가 제품의 성질이 아니라 고용주가 계약할 때 고른 설정이라는 뜻이다. 수정 요청이 거절됐을 때 어디에 다투는지, 다투는 동안 그 값이 인사 판단에 계속 쓰이는지는 공개 자료에서 확인되지 않는다.

회사가 자기 위치를 먼저 밝힌 대목도 있다. 테크울프는 자사 솔루션이 유럽 인공지능법 부속서 III 4절에 따라 고위험으로 분류된다고 직접 적어 두었다. 규제 당국이 지목하기 전에 벤더가 스스로 적은 문장인데, 그 분류에 붙는 의무의 시행일은 2027년 12월 2일로 미뤄져 있다.

추론된 값이 근거가 되려면 어떤 기록에서 나왔고 언제 갱신됐는지가 그 옆에 함께 있어야 한다. 출처가 지워진 추론은 맞을 때도 근거가 아니라 주장이다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

SAP agreed on 6 October to buy TechWolf, a Ghent company that never asks employees what they can do. It reads what has already accumulated in the HR system, in Jira, in completed training records, and works backward from there to a set of skills.

What that produces is called a context graph for work, and it runs in three layers: jobs broken down into the tasks inside them, the skills people hold and actually use, and the external labour market. Manoj Swaminathan, president and chief product officer of SAP Autonomous Suite, said the graph provides "an excellent grounding layer for agent queries" about work, skills planning and talent management. What SAP bought is the floor its HR agents will stand on when asked a question about a person. Terms were not disclosed, and the deal is expected to close in the fourth quarter subject to regulatory approval.

The firmer that floor, the more weight each line written on it carries. TechWolf does give employees a tool to review and update the skill profile generated for them, and its FAQ says the default validator is the employee. The next sentence of that same answer attaches a condition: a stricter manager-first route is available as a governance choice the customer makes at rollout. Whether you get to touch your own skill line first is therefore a setting your employer bought rather than a property of the product. Where you appeal a rejected correction, and whether that line keeps feeding hiring and redeployment decisions while the dispute runs, is not in the public record.

The company did state its own position first. TechWolf has written that its solution is classified high-risk under Annex III, Section 4 of the EU AI Act, which is rare among vendors in this field. The obligations attached to that classification now start on 2 December 2027.

An inference becomes evidence only when what it was drawn from, and when it was last refreshed, sit beside it. Strip the provenance and even a correct inference is a claim.

## Facebook (EN)

Suppose every Jira ticket I carried for the past six months was maintenance work.

Does that mean I cannot do design, or that no design work ever came my way?

In the data, those two look exactly the same.

That is where I stayed longest, reading that SAP had agreed on 6 October to buy TechWolf, a company in Ghent.

TechWolf never asks employees what they can do. It reads what has already accumulated in the HR system, in Jira, in the training records, and works backward from there to a set of skills. Given how surveys behave, that is a reasonable choice. A list filled in once is never updated, and two people doing the same job will write their skills down in entirely different words.

SAP's chief product officer called the resulting graph "an excellent grounding layer for agent queries."

A floor of facts, so that the HR agent does not invent its answers. I think the name is exact. What comes with it is that the floor is made of traces one person left behind while working.

The firmer the floor, the more solid a wrong line written on it also looks.

TechWolf has not left this question blank. Employees get a tool to review and update the profile generated for them, and the FAQ says the default validator is the employee. The next sentence of that same answer notes that a stricter manager-first route is available as a governance choice the customer makes at rollout.

"Why should whether I get to touch my own skill line first depend on a setting my employer picked at purchase?"

This is where our habit at Pebblous comes from, of asking teams to keep the context a value was created in right next to the value. An inferred skill works as evidence only if what it was drawn from and when it was last refreshed sit beside it, because otherwise there is no way to point at what needs fixing. The fact that someone disputed the line is data as well. A system that keeps no record of objections has no input from which to learn where it is wrong.

What SAP bought, put plainly, is a company's memory of its people. Before asking whether that memory is accurate, I would rather ask whether it was built so that it can be corrected.
