## LinkedIn (KO)

프런티어 연구소의 모델을 채점하던 회사가, 이번에는 개별 기업의 업무를 채점하겠다고 한다.

AI 학습·평가 데이터를 만드는 Mercor가 11월 10일 시리즈 D로 7억 5천만 달러를 모았다. 매겨진 기업 가치는 200억 달러로, 지난해 10월 시리즈 C의 두 배다. 새로 이름을 올린 곳은 엔비디아와 세일즈포스, 줌이다. 라운드를 이끈 투자자는 밝히지 않았다. 세일즈포스와 줌은 모델을 만드는 회사가 아니라, 에이전트가 실제로 일하게 될 화면을 가진 쪽이다.

도구는 넉 달 전에 들어왔다. 7월 9일 인수한 Deeptune은 지식 노동자가 매일 쓰는 소프트웨어를 흉내 낸 강화학습 환경을 2년 동안 수백 개 지어 둔 회사다. 등록 전문가 500만 명 네트워크에 그 환경을 붙이면, 한 회사의 업무를 모사한 자리에서 에이전트를 돌리고 그 직종의 사람이 결과를 채점하는 일이 한 묶음이 된다.

근거는 공동창업자 아다르시 히레마스가 적은 한 문장이다. 공개 벤치마크 점수가 높은 모델도 그 사업을 떠받치는 시스템과 정책, 예외 처리 앞에서는 넘어질 수 있다는 것이다. 공개 시험을 부정하는 말은 아니다. Mercor 자신이 2025년 10월부터 APEX라는 공개 벤치마크를 운영해 왔고 성적도 순위표에 올려 둔다.

그림자는 두 군데다. 평가를 만드는 쪽과 그 평가를 통과하도록 모델을 후학습시키는 쪽이 같은 회사다. 그리고 회사별 채점표의 재료는 되돌아온 결과물과 그때 붙은 사유, 예외를 누가 어떻게 넘겼는지 같은 안쪽 기록이다. 시스코가 12개 시장 5,200여 명에게 물은 조사에서, 데이터의 소유권과 책임, 배상을 계약 조항으로 적어 두는 곳은 55%였다.

평가 데이터가 비용 항목에서 자산 항목으로 넘어가는 중이라면, 넘기기 전에 적어야 할 것은 소유권이다.

페블러스가 DataClinic으로 하는 일은 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 재는 일이다. 다만 그 자료로 만든 정답이 몇 해 뒤 누구 손에 놓여 있을지는 계약 조항이 정하고, 그 조항은 발주 전에만 쓸 수 있다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Mercor, whose business has been grading the models of frontier labs, now says it will grade the work inside individual companies.

The company announced a $750 million Series D on November 10 at a $20 billion valuation, twice the price it carried in its Series C last October. Nvidia, Salesforce and Zoom are new on the investor list, and no lead was named. Salesforce and Zoom do not build models. They own the screens where an agent would be expected to do the job.

The tooling arrived four months earlier. Deeptune, acquired on July 9, had put two years into turning several hundred enterprise applications into reinforcement learning environments: mock spreadsheets, Salesforce queues, places to practice. Set that beside a registered expert network of more than five million people and one package falls out. Run an agent inside a copy of one firm's operations, then have someone from that trade grade the output.

The reasoning comes from co-founder Adarsh Hiremath, who writes that a model with strong public benchmark scores can still fail on "the systems, policies and edge cases that define how a specific business runs." It is not a rejection of public tests. Mercor has run one of its own since October 2025, APEX, and still posts the scores.

Two things sit in the shadow of that. The firm writing the evaluation is also the firm keeping the infrastructure that post-trains models until they pass it. And the material for a per-company answer key is that firm's own record of what came back, the reason attached to it, and who decided an exception. In a Cisco study across 12 markets, 55% pin ownership, liability and indemnity for data into contract terms.

If evaluation data is moving out of the cost column and into the asset column, the line to write before handing it across is ownership.

## Facebook (EN)

A piece of work that comes back usually comes back with one sentence attached.

Not ready to send. Which rule it broke is not written down. That rule lives in the head of someone who has done the same job for years, and on the days that person is out, the same piece of work sometimes goes out untouched.

On November 10, a price was attached to the job of moving what is in that head onto paper.

The company is Mercor. It builds the data that AI models get trained and graded on, and the round it closed came to $750 million against a valuation of $20 billion. Nvidia, Salesforce and Zoom are new names on the investor list.

The same announcement changes who the customer is. What the firm has graded until now is the models of frontier labs. What it says it will grade next is the work inside individual companies. Deeptune, bought in July, spent two years copying the software knowledge workers open every day into hundreds of practice environments, and the expert network runs to more than five million people.

The part that held me is what comes after. Writing an answer key takes material. Jobs that actually ran to completion. Work that came back, with whatever reason was attached to it. Some trace of who decided an exception and on what grounds.

That is where the structure of this business sits. A loose answer key is easy to hand over and does not measure our work. An answer key that does measure our work has our work inside it. The more accurate the evaluation, the further inside the company the material has to come from.

In a study Cisco ran across 12 markets with 5,200-odd respondents, 55% pin ownership, liability and indemnity for data into contract terms.

"In the contract that commissions the evaluation, is there a line saying when the answer key gets deleted?"
