## LinkedIn (KO)

앤트로픽이 11월 12일부터 클로드를 거듭 괴롭히는 대화를 금지한다. 그 금지를 1차로 집행할 주체로 발표문이 적어 둔 것은 사람 심사팀이 아니라 클로드 자신이다.

10월 8일 공개된 개정에서 이 줄은 새 절로 서지 않았다. 자해 조장이나 괴롭힘처럼 사람을 향한 행위를 금지하던 기존 절의 맨 끝에 한 줄로 붙었다. 앤트로픽은 적용 범위도 같은 자리에서 좁혔다. 뚜렷한 목적 없이 거듭 잔인하게 구는 극단적인 경우에만 적용하겠다는 것이다. 해당하지 않는 것도 넷을 꼽아 두었다. 흔한 불만 표출, 반박, 어두운 창작 소재, 모델 시험과 연구다.

예외를 넉넉히 적어 둔 것은 조항을 좁히려는 선의다. 다만 그 목록이 금지와 허용을 서로 다른 행동으로 가르지는 못한다. 같은 말을 한 번 던지면 불만이고 쉰 번 던지면 학대 쪽으로 기운다. 경계는 '거듭'과 '뚜렷한 목적'이라는 두 낱말이 통째로 쥐고 있는데, 몇 번부터 거듭인지를 적은 문서는 밖에 나와 있지 않다.

집행 수단으로 지목된 기능도 이번에 만든 것이 아니다. 2025년 8월 클로드 Opus 4 계열에 들어간 대화 종료 기능으로, 이번 조항보다 1년 넘게 앞선다. 당시 공지에 따르면 대화 하나가 닫혀도 계정의 다른 대화는 영향을 받지 않고 이용자는 곧바로 새 대화를 열 수 있다. 계정 정지가 아니라 대화 한 건을 닫는 차단기에 가깝고, 그만큼 판정이 틀렸을 때 드는 값도 작다.

같은 개정의 다른 조항은 반대쪽으로 갔다. 자율 하드웨어에 클로드를 붙일 때 운전 한계는 모델 출력과 무관한 장비나 제어기가 걸어야 한다. 걸 항목으로는 속도·힘·온도를 비롯한 아홉 가지를 이름으로 적어 두었다. 선거 쪽에서도 유권자 맞춤 접촉을 통째로 막던 넓은 금지가 밖에서 확인 가능한 행위 다섯으로 바뀌었다. 세 조항 가운데 학대 조항만 아직 선언에 머물러 있다.

라벨링 기준서를 써 본 쪽에는 익숙한 모양이다. 선언 다음에는 해당과 비해당과 보류의 경계 사례가 붙고, 두 사람에게 같은 기록을 주어 일치율을 재고, 일치율이 낮으면 데이터가 아니라 기준서를 고친다. 페블러스가 AI 학습 데이터를 진단할 때 점수보다 기준서와 판정자 일치율을 먼저 보는 자리도 여기다. 금지 행위를 한 문장으로 적는 일과, 그 문장으로 실제 기록을 분류할 수 있게 적는 일은 다른 작업이다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

From November 12, Anthropic's usage policy bans sustained abuse of its own model. The announcement names the first enforcer of that ban, and it is not a human review team. It is Claude.

The clause, published on October 8, did not get a section of its own. It was appended as the last line of an existing section that bans conduct aimed at people, from encouraging self-harm to harassment. Anthropic narrowed the reach in the same breath: the rule is meant for extreme cases in which users act cruelly toward the model with no discernible purpose, and the company listed four things it does not cover, among them ordinary complaints, pushback, dark creative material, and testing or research on the model.

Writing the exemptions out that generously is an attempt to keep the clause small. What the list cannot do is separate the banned conduct from the permitted conduct by kind. Say something once and it is a complaint; say it fifty times and it leans toward abuse. The boundary rests entirely on two words, "repeatedly" and "discernible purpose," and no published document says where the count begins.

The enforcement mechanism is not new either. Claude's ability to end a conversation shipped in August 2025 on the Opus 4 line, more than a year before the clause that now relies on it. Per that announcement, closing one conversation leaves the rest of the account untouched and the user can open a new one immediately. This is a breaker on a single thread rather than a suspension, which also means a wrong call is cheap.

Another clause in the same update went the other way. Hook Claude to autonomous hardware and the operating limits have to be enforced by equipment or controllers independent of model output, with nine of them named outright, including speed, force and temperature. On elections, a blanket ban on voter-targeted outreach was replaced by five deceptive acts that can be checked from outside. Of the three changes, only the abuse clause is still a declaration.

Anyone who has written annotation guidelines knows the shape. A declaration is followed by edge cases marked in, out, or undecided; two annotators label the same records; agreement is measured, and when it comes back low the guidelines get rewritten rather than the data. That is the layer Pebblous reads first when it diagnoses training data, ahead of any score. Writing down what is forbidden and writing it so that records can be sorted by it are two different jobs.

## Facebook (EN)

I have asked Claude the same thing more than ten times in a row.

It was late and the code would not come apart. By around the eighth attempt my own phrasing had turned sharp enough that I noticed it.

From November 12, Anthropic's new usage policy forbids some of those conversations.

The wording covers extreme cases where a user is repeatedly cruel to the model with no discernible purpose. Ordinary complaints, pushback, a cruel scene in a novel, poking at the model to see how far it bends: the company wrote all four down as things the clause does not reach.

So which side was my eighth message on?

"How many times before it counts as repeatedly?"

No published document answers that. And the mechanism the announcement names as its primary enforcement is not a review team but Claude itself, ending the conversation. The feature has been there for more than a year.

Another clause in the same update runs the other way.

Put Claude on equipment that moves without a person approving each step, and nine operating limits, among them speed, force and temperature, have to be held by a controller outside the model. Whatever the model happens to output.

One side has limits you can measure. The other has a sentence.

The sentence has no bones yet. What falls inside it, what falls outside, whether two people reading the same transcript land in the same place. Anyone who has written annotation guidelines knows how long the work after the declaration runs. It is also why Pebblous opens the guidelines before the score when it looks at training data.

Writing that something is forbidden and writing it so two people would draw the line in the same spot are not the same task.

Most of the time we stop after the first one.

I still do not know where I was standing that night.
