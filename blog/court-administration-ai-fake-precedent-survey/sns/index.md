## LinkedIn (KO)

대법원 판결서 형식을 그대로 본뜬 가짜 판결서가 법정에 제출됐다. 처음 보는 법리와 함께 사건번호까지 적혀 있었는데, 막상 검색해 보면 그 판례가 없었다.

대법원 법원행정처가 지난달 민사·가사·행정 사건을 맡은 법관 360명에게 묻고 결과를 11월 2일 공개했다. 법원이 이 문제로 법관에게 직접 물은 것은 처음이다. 응답자 가운데 243명이 허위·부존재 판례가 인용된 서면을 한 번이라도 받아 봤다고 답했다. 셋 중 둘이다.

기사 제목에 오른 80.7%는 전체 법관의 비율이 아니다. 유형을 묻는 문항부터 분모가 바뀌어, 겪었다고 답한 243명을 다시 100%로 놓았을 때 아예 존재하지 않는 판례를 본 사람의 비율이다. 두 겹을 하나로 눌러 읽으면 조사가 하지 않은 말이 만들어진다.

그런 서면을 낸 쪽으로는 변호사 없이 직접 소송하는 당사자 본인이 69.5%로 가장 많이 지목됐다. 법원행정처는 바로 그 당사자들이 상대가 낸 오류를 찾아내고 대응하기도 가장 어렵다고 덧붙였다. 가장 많이 들고 오는 쪽과 가장 알아보기 어려운 쪽이 같다.

응답 법관의 91.7%가 제도적 대응을 요구했다. 고른 방안은 과태료 부과 근거, 인용 판례 검증의무, AI 활용 사실 기재의무 순이었고 셋 다 60%대에 붙어 있었다. 어느 것도 AI 사용 자체를 금지하지 않는다. 확인했는지 묻고, 무엇을 거쳤는지 적게 하고, 틀린 것을 넣은 쪽에 값을 치르게 한다.

페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 정량 진단한다. 진단을 나가 보면 비어 있는 칸은 품질 점수가 아니라, 그 숫자가 어느 파일 몇 번째 줄에서 나왔는지를 적어 두는 자리인 경우가 많다.

법정에서 가짜 판례가 걸린 것은 상대방 대리인이 서면을 뜯어보고 재판부가 사건번호를 검색했기 때문이다. 회사에서 AI가 만든 보고서와 제안서에는 그 둘이 다 없는 경우가 많다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

A filing arrived in a Korean courtroom in the shape of a Supreme Court judgment, docket number and all. The reasoning was unfamiliar, and when the judge searched for the case, it did not exist.

Korea's National Court Administration surveyed 360 judges handling civil, family and administrative cases last month and published the results on November 2. It is the first time the court has put the question to judges directly. Of those who answered, 243 said a filing citing false or nonexistent case law had reached them at least once. That is two in three.

The 80.7% that travels in the headlines is not the share of all judges. The denominator changes at the question about types: it is the share, among the 243 who said yes, who saw case law that does not exist at all. Press the two together and you get a sentence the survey never made.

Litigants running their own cases without a lawyer were named most often, by 69.5% of the judges who had met one. The same office noted that those litigants also have the hardest time finding and answering the errors the other side files. The group bringing in the most fake case law is the group least able to recognize it.

91.7% of the judges asked for rules. What they chose clustered in the low sixties: grounds for an administrative fine, a duty to verify the case law you cite, a duty to state that AI was used. None of the three bans the tool. They ask whether you checked, what you ran it through, and who pays when the citation turns out to be wrong.

Pebblous quantifies the quality of AI training data with DataClinic. On that work the empty field is rarely the quality score. It is the one naming the file and the row a number came from.

The fake citations were caught in court because opposing counsel reads the filing and the bench searches the docket number. A report drafted with AI inside a company usually has neither.

"It gave a Supreme Court docket number alongside reasoning I had never seen before, and when I actually searched for it, the case law did not exist."

A judge in Korea wrote that on a survey form last month.

I keep rereading four words of it. "When I actually searched."

Someone opened the thing.

Korea's National Court Administration put the question to 360 judges between October 6 and 16 and published what came back on November 2. Of those who answered, 243 said a filing citing case law that AI had invented reached them at least once. Two in three. Many of the filings copied the layout of a Supreme Court judgment. In some, the conversation with the AI was still sitting inside the document, left there by whoever pasted it.

The place to slow down is the question that comes next.

From the moment the survey asks which types the judges saw, the base is no longer the 360 but the 243 who said yes. The 80.7% that traveled into the headlines is a share of the people who met one, not of the bench. Press the two together and you get a sentence nobody measured.

91.7% of the judges asked for rules. What they chose was grounds for a fine, a duty to verify the case law you cite, and a duty to state that AI was used.

None of the three tells anyone to stop using the tool.

They ask whether you checked, what you ran it through, and who pays when the citation is wrong.

I have come to read the three as a demand for 'the address of a claim'.
