87년 동안 열려 있던 수학 난제가 소셜미디어 게시물 하나로 끝났다. 2026년 7월의 어느 일요일이었다.

세 변수 다항식 하나가 야코비안 추측의 반례였고, 수학계는 그날 오후에 그것을 받아들였다. 런던의 한 수학자가 다음 날 아침 깨어났을 때는 기계 검증까지 끝나 있었다. 이 속도는 AI의 성능에서 나온 것이 아니다. 반례가 유한하고 정확해서 의심스러우면 누구든 다시 계산해 볼 수 있는 물건이었기 때문이다.

남은 질문은 종류가 다르다. 그 반례가 어떤 프롬프트에서, 몇 번째 시도에서, 사람이 어디까지 손을 대서 나왔는지에 대한 기록은 공개되지 않았다. 규칙을 어긴 사람이 있어서가 아니라 그런 것을 남기라는 규칙이 아직 없기 때문이다. 2주 뒤 다른 회사는 미해결 문제 열 개를 풀었다며 형식 증명과 사고 과정 요약까지 함께 내놓았는데, 그 문제를 냈던 수학자는 그것이 자신에게 이해를 주지는 않는다고 말했다.

문제는 공개의 양이 아니라 종류였다. 형식검증은 이게 참인가라는 질문을 닫는다. 이게 어떻게 여기까지 왔는가라는 질문은 닫지 못한다. 형식화 논문들의 비교표에 "로그를 공개했는가"라는 열이 새로 생겼고, 아홉 칸 중 채워진 것은 하나다.

수학은 세상에서 검증 문화가 가장 엄격한 분야다. 그런 곳에서도 산출 속도가 기록 인프라를 앞질렀다면, 그보다 느슨한 곳에서는 이미 오래전에 그렇게 됐다는 뜻이다. 페블러스가 AI-Ready Data라는 말로 이야기해 온 것도 깨끗한 데이터가 아니라 출처와 이력이 붙어 있는 데이터였다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

An 87-year-old conjecture fell to a single social media post, and mathematicians accepted it the same afternoon. By the time one of them woke up in London the next morning, a machine-checked verification was already done.

The speed had little to do with AI. A counterexample to the Jacobian conjecture is a finite, exact object, and anyone who doubts it can recompute it. What collapsed here was the cost of verification, not the difficulty of the mathematics.

What never arrived was the record. No prompt, no count of attempts, no account of where the human stepped in. Nobody broke a rule; there is no rule requiring any of it to be kept. Two weeks later a different lab announced ten solved open problems and released far more: formal Lean proofs, a narrative of the model's reasoning, written manuscripts. The mathematician who had posed one of those problems said it "doesn't give me any understanding."

So the amount disclosed was never the issue. Formal verification closes the question of whether a result is true. It leaves open the question of how the result came to be, and why that path. Comparison tables in formalization papers have grown a column asking whether the logs were published. One box of nine is filled.

Mathematics runs the strictest verification culture on earth, and even there output has outrun the record. In looser fields that happened long ago. AI-Ready Data, the phrase Pebblous keeps returning to, has never meant clean data. It means data that arrives with its lineage attached.

## Facebook (EN)

A mathematician in London went to sleep on a Sunday night and woke up to find that an 87-year-old problem was over.

Machine-checked, too.

Speed like that is rare in mathematics. But it did not come from the AI. It came from the kind of object a counterexample is. Finite and exact, so anyone who doubts it can just sit down and recompute it.

The strange part came after.

Nobody knows which prompt produced it, how many attempts came before it, or how far the human hand reached in. Not because anyone broke a rule. Because no rule asks for any of it to be kept.

Two weeks later, a different lab announced that it had solved ten open problems. This time it published the formal proofs and a narrative of the path the model had taken. Far more disclosure, by any measure. And the mathematician who had posed one of those problems said: "That doesn't give me any understanding."

That was where the two gaps came apart for me.

The "truth gap" is closed. Is this correct? Confirmed.

The "provenance gap" is not. How did this come to be, and why this path? A formal proof only answers the first question.

Mathematics is the strictest place on earth for checking whether a result holds, and even there, making has outrun recording. It is the same question we run into while diagnosing the lineage of data. Records cannot be built after the fact. Once an audit begins, the prompts are gone, and what is left to fill the gap is somebody's memory.

A result we can confirm but cannot retrace: how far can we really call that an asset?
