프런티어 모델을 하나도 만들지 않는 스무 개 관할권이 범용 AI 규정을 쓰기 시작했다. 조문 587건을 한 줄씩 세어 보니, 실제로 무언가를 요구하는 조항 가운데 시행 중인 구속력 있는 법에 들어간 것은 다섯에 하나였다.

아르카디아 임팩트와 더 퓨처 소사이어티 연구자들이 이달 arXiv에 올린 매핑 연구다. 다루는 주제는 놀랄 만큼 닮았다. 위험을 재고, 잰 것을 검증하고, 넘지 말아야 할 선을 긋고, 사고가 나면 알린다. 끊기는 자리도 일정하다. 관찰할 권한은 세웠고, 조치할 권한은 대체로 비워 두었다. AI 안전연구소나 그에 준하는 평가 기구를 세운 관할권이 열한 곳인데, 이름 붙은 안전·평가 기구 가운데 집행권을 가진 곳은 하나도 없다. 영국과 캐나다와 인도의 연구소가 조치하지 못하는 것은 역량이 모자라서가 아니라 재고 조언하도록 설립됐기 때문이다.

한국은 예외적인 자리에 있다. 정의를 쓰고 연산량 문턱을 붙이고 평가 의무까지 잇는 구속 사슬을, 표본에서 유일하게 자력으로 입법했다. 다만 기본법에도 시행령에도 중대사고 보고 채널은 없고, 논문이 코딩한 한국의 유일한 보고 조항은 금융회사를 대상으로 한 자율 가이드라인이다. 과태료 계도기간은 최소 1년이고, 정부 설명으로 지금 그 문턱을 넘는 국내 모델은 없다.

원자료는 동료심사를 거치지 않은 working paper이고, 데이터는 7월 30일 스냅숏이다. 저자들 스스로 몇 달이면 낡는다고 적었는데 실제로 곧바로 낡았다. 스냅숏 직후 EU 디지털 옴니버스가 발효되면서 유럽 AI 사무국은 현장검사와 과징금 부과 권한을 더 받았다. 격차는 좁혀진 것이 아니라 벌어졌다.

그래서 이건 규제 뉴스가 아니라 증빙에 관한 질문이다. 우리가 낼 문서를 읽고 되물을 권한을 가진 기관이 그 시장에 없다면, 그 문서의 실제 수신자는 규제기관이 아니라 고객 실사와 조달 심사다. 수신자가 바뀌면 문서의 설계 기준도 바뀐다. 데이터와 모델의 품질을 문서로 남기는 일을 해 온 회사가 이 지도를 끝까지 따라가 본 이유가 여기에 있다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Twenty jurisdictions that host no frontier developer have started writing rules for general-purpose AI. Read clause by clause, only one provision in five sits in binding law that is actually in force.

The map comes from researchers at Arcadia Impact and The Future Society, who coded 587 provisions across the twenty. The subject matter converges to a striking degree: assess the risk, verify what was assessed, draw the line that must not be crossed, report it when it is. So does the break. The power to observe was built and the power to act was mostly left blank. Eleven jurisdictions have stood up an AI safety institute or an equivalent evaluation body, and not one named safety or evaluation institute in the corpus holds enforcement powers. The institutes in Britain, Canada and India cannot act on what they find because they were chartered to measure and advise.

Korea is the outlier. It is the only jurisdiction in the sample to have legislated its own binding upstream chain, running from a definition through a compute threshold to an evaluation duty. Neither the framework act nor its decree creates a serious-incident reporting channel, and the single reporting provision coded for Korea is a voluntary guideline written for financial firms. Penalties sit under a grace period of at least a year, and by the government's own account no domestic model currently crosses the threshold.

The source is a working paper, not peer reviewed, built on a 30 July snapshot the authors expected to age within months. It aged immediately. Days after the snapshot the EU's Digital Omnibus entered into force and handed the AI Office on-site inspection and fining powers. The distance widened rather than closed.

Which makes this a question about evidence rather than a regulatory news item. Where no institution has the standing to read your evaluation report and ask a follow-up question, the real recipient is not a regulator but customer due diligence and procurement review. Change the recipient and you change what the document has to be built for.

## Facebook (EN)

When a new rule arrives, the first thing that multiplies inside a company is paperwork.

A risk review. An evaluation summary. A safety statement. You file them, and it feels like the matter is closed.

Has anyone on the receiving end ever come back to you with a question about one of them?

Twenty jurisdictions that host no frontier developer have started writing rules for general-purpose AI, and a research team went through those rules one clause at a time.

The subjects converge almost eerily. Assess the risk, verify what was assessed, draw the line that must not be crossed, report it when it is.

So does the place where the chain breaks. The power to observe was built. The power to act was mostly left blank.

Eleven jurisdictions have stood up an evaluation body. Exactly one of those bodies, the EU AI Office, can act on what it finds. The rest were chartered to measure and advise.

"Is there an institution in this market with the standing to read what we file and ask us a follow-up question?"

"Evidence without a recipient." Once I had the phrase, my worry changed direction.

The demand for the document does not disappear when no regulator can act on it. The recipient moves. To customer due diligence. To procurement review. To the auditor.

Korea has the most fully drafted chain in the sample. A definition, a compute threshold, an evaluation duty, all legislated at home.

And still no place to report a serious incident inside the framework act. The one reporting provision coded for Korea is a voluntary guideline written for financial firms.

For a company whose work is recording the quality of data and models in writing, the question this study leaves is not whether regulation is strict or lax.

Whose desk is our evidence built to be read on? And if the reader is no longer a regulator, can the document stay in the shape it is in?
