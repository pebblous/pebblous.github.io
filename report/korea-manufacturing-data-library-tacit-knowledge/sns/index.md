## LinkedIn (KO)

정부는 2026년부터 용접·조립 현장 장인의 감각을 데이터로 뽑아내고, 2027년부터 그것을 보관하는 제조 데이터 라이브러리를 짓는다. 보안 설계는 이미 구체적이다. 외부와 차단된 클린룸 안에서만 쓰고, 외부 반출은 금지하며, 열람에도 별도 심사가 붙는다.

정해지지 않은 것은 그 안에 무엇을 어떤 규격으로 넣는가다. 용접 문헌에서는 같은 용융풀 영상을 놓고도 정답이 네 갈래로 갈린다. 어떤 연구는 모재 뒷면 비드의 폭을 세 범주로 적고, 어떤 연구는 시편을 절단해 용입 깊이를 밀리미터로 적는다. 그래서 92.70%와 R² 0.998을 나란히 놓고 어느 모델이 더 낫다고 말할 수 없다.

기업이 망설이는 이유는 법이 아니다. 대법원은 제3자에게 자료를 제공한 뒤에도 비밀유지의무를 볼 사정이 있으면 영업비밀의 비밀관리성을 인정해 왔다. 클린룸에 데이터를 넣는 일이 영업비밀 지위를 깨뜨리지 않는다는 뜻이다. 망설임은 무엇이 얼마나 나가는지 모른다는 데서 온다.

한국은 2020년에도 세계 최초라는 제조데이터공유규범을 만들면서 라벨과 품질과 조건 메타데이터를 규율 대상에서 비워 뒀다. 6년이 지나 같은 칸이 다시 비어 있다.

반입 규칙은 개방의 반대가 아니라 개방의 조건이다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Korea's industry ministry will begin extracting the tacit skill of welders and assemblers into data in 2026, and from 2027 will house it in a manufacturing data library. The security design is already specific: the data may be used only inside a clean room sealed off from the outside, removal is prohibited, and even viewing requires a separate review.

What has not been decided is what goes in, and in what format. In the welding literature, one and the same molten pool footage yields at least four different ground truths. One study records the width of the bead on the back of the plate in three categories; another cuts the specimen and records penetration depth in millimetres. Which is why 92.70% and an R² of 0.998 cannot be placed side by side to say which model is better.

Companies are not hesitating because the law blocks them. Korean courts have upheld the secrecy management requirement for trade secrets even after material was handed to a third party, where a duty of confidentiality can reasonably be inferred, so depositing data in a clean room does not by itself forfeit trade secret status. The hesitation comes from not knowing what leaves, and how much of it.

Korea already stood here in 2020. A manufacturing data sharing norm, announced as the first of its kind in the world, left labels, quality and condition metadata outside its scope. Six years on, the same cells are empty again.

Intake rules are not the opposite of openness. They are its precondition.

## Facebook (EN)

I read an observation about what welding students fell back on once the augmented reality guidance was switched off.

Sound. Vibration. The steadiness of the hand holding the torch. No one had told them to.

The study, WeldAR, was presented at ACM CHI this year. The overlay had been computing contact tip to work distance, work angle and travel speed in real time, and over the sessions the learners came to experience that feedback as interference.

Korea's industry ministry plans to start extracting exactly this kind of sense into data in 2026, and to keep it in a manufacturing data library from 2027.

The security design is meticulous. The data may be used only inside a clean room sealed off from the outside, removal is prohibited, and even viewing goes through a separate review.

The vault has been designed.

What is missing sits on the other side of the door. What goes in, and in what format.

"What do we write down as a good weld?"

Welding researchers have not settled on one answer either. Given the same molten pool footage, one study records the width of the bead on the back of the plate in three categories, and another cuts the specimen and records penetration depth in millimetres. The units of the scorecard differ, so the two results cannot be compared.

I have come to think of this as a question of intake rules. What makes a library a library is not the building or the vault but the specification that decides what may be brought in, and in what shape.

Korea stood in this same place in 2020. A manufacturing data sharing norm, announced as the first of its kind in the world, settled definitions, transaction requirements and revenue sharing, and left labels, quality and condition metadata outside its scope. Six years on, the same cells are empty again.

That is why we have spent so long with this policy at Pebblous. Data whose label definitions disagree, and whose measurement conditions were never written down, is the defect we meet most often in diagnosis, and it is being reproduced line by line inside a policy document.

Attach the sensors and something will certainly remain. Whether what remains can be called a craftsman's sense, with no one having decided what counts as correct, is something we may only learn after the library opens.
