과학자들이 클로드에 가장 적게 맡긴 일이, 같은 조사에서 가장 중요하다고 답한 일이었다.

앤트로픽이 11월 5일 공개한 조사는 연구 목적 대화 20만 7,049건을 여덟 칸으로 갈랐다. 가장 큰 칸은 데이터 분석으로 33.7%, 가장 작은 칸은 아이디어·가설 생성으로 4.2%다.

같은 조사가 과학자 528명에게 중요한 연구 성과를 내는 데 네 활동이 각각 얼마나 중요한지 100점을 나눠 매기게 했다. 1위는 35.6점을 받은 아이디어·가설이었다.

두 칸을 가른 것은 난이도가 아니다. 보고서가 따로 재 둔 자료에서 사용량과 가장 세게 붙어 움직이는 변수는 강화학습 적합도이고 상관이 0.78이다. 그 지수를 열어 보면 검증 가능성과 시뮬레이션 가능성과 피드백 밀도 셋으로 되어 있다. 셋 다 같은 것을 묻는다. 맞았는지를 언제 아는가. 데이터를 정리하고 분석 코드를 돌리는 일은 그 자리에서 판별되고, 가설이 좋았는지는 몇 해 뒤에 안다.

이 수치를 양으로 읽으면 안 된다. 분류기가 대화 하나에 라벨을 하나만 붙이도록 설계돼 있어, 분석이나 집필에 섞여 일어난 발상은 그 칸에 남지 않는다. 선별기의 기본값도 연구 작업 아님이고, 애매하면 그쪽으로 보내라는 지시가 프롬프트에 그대로 적혀 있다. 이 칸의 수는 일어난 양이 아니라 하한값이다.

그래도 방향은 흔들리지 않는다. 연구 전용 도구인 클로드 사이언스에서는 같은 칸이 8%로 올라가는데, 거기서 늘어난 것은 구조 데이터베이스로 곧장 맞대 볼 수 있는 가설들이다. 확인 장치가 붙은 자리에서 그 칸이 움직였다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

The task scientists hand to Claude least often is the one they rate as mattering most.

Anthropic's November 5 study sorted 207,049 research conversations into eight task categories. Data analysis is the largest at 33.7% of Claude conversations and 48.1% on Claude Code. Ideation and hypothesis generation is the smallest at 4.2% and 2.1%. A survey run alongside the usage data asked 528 scientists to split 100 points across four activities by how much each matters for producing important research. Ideation came first with 35.6 points.

Difficulty is not what separated the two. The variable that moves most tightly with usage is RL suitability, at a correlation of 0.78, and that index is built from three scores: verifiability, simulatability and feedback density. All three ask one question. When do you learn that the answer was right? Cleaning a dataset and running analysis code settle on the spot. Whether a hypothesis was any good settles years later. Across quintiles of that index the use ratio climbs from roughly 0.2 to 2.2 on Claude and 4.2 on Claude Code.

Read the 4.2% as a floor rather than a count. The classifier assigns one label per conversation, so ideas that surfaced while someone was analyzing data or drafting a paper land in those categories instead. The screening prompt also defaults to not research work and tells the model to choose that default whenever the case is unclear.

The direction survives the caveat. On Claude Science, the research-specific tool, the same category sits at 8%, and the tasks driving that rise are hypotheses that a structure database or a computational tool can check immediately.

## Facebook (EN)

A researcher does two things in the same afternoon.

They paste in a table of measurements and ask for a figure.

They do not ask whether the hypothesis behind those measurements is any good.

Anthropic sorted more than two hundred thousand research conversations into eight task categories. Data analysis came to 33.7%. Ideation and hypothesis generation came to 4.2%. In the survey that ran alongside the usage data, 528 scientists named that second category the one that matters most for producing important research.

My first reading was that the model is simply weaker there.

The report has a separate measurement that says otherwise. What tracks usage most closely is not difficulty but an index built from verifiability, simulatability and feedback density.

The work that moved across has one thing in common. You find out on the spot whether it was right.

The work that stayed has the opposite property. You find out years later.

The 15.8x ceiling the report puts on acceleration has the same shape. It is an hour of a researcher divided by what an hour of AI costs to stand in for it, and the quality of an idea is priced at zero inside the model.

"When do the results of the work we handed to an AI come back to us? On the spot, at quarter end, or never?"

Pebblous rates AI training data on quality with DataClinic. The classifier in this report truncates long conversations at 100,000 characters, and it keeps turns alternately from each end. The longer the conversation, the more of its middle disappears. Whoever wrote that one rule about what to keep also decided, without meaning to, which column of the final tally would come out thin.

The part left to us is the other half. It is attaching a way to check to the work we call important. On Claude Science the same category sits at 8%, which suggests that once something can be checked, it starts moving across too.
