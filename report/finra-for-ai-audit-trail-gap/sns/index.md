## LinkedIn (KO)

오픈AI와 앤트로픽과 구글이 7월부터 서로의 모델을 출시 전에 검사할 기구를 논의해 왔다. 공개된 설계 문서 다섯 건을 전문으로 받아 대조하면 같은 자리가 비어 있다. 검사관이 개발사의 관행을 감사한다고는 적혀 있는데, 그 감사의 증거를 개발사가 무엇으로 어떤 형식으로 얼마나 오래 남겨야 하는지 적은 문장은 어디에도 없다.

원본 쪽은 순서가 반대였다. 금융은 심판보다 심판이 볼 장부를 먼저 지었다. 통합감사추적을 전 산업에 깔기까지 약 10년이 걸렸고, 그 사이 연간 예산은 최초 추정의 네 배 반 가까이로 불었다. 가장 비싸고 가장 논쟁적인 층이 바로 장부였다.

판정은 좁혀야 정확하다. AI에 기록 의무라는 발상이 없는 것이 아니다. EU AI법 부속서 XI은 학습 데이터의 출처와 큐레이션 방법, 평가 프로토콜과 결과, 레드팀 기록을 이미 요구하고 있고 같은 회사들이 유럽에서 그 목록을 지킨다. 빠진 것은 미국에서 그들이 직접 그리는 설계도 쪽이다. 기록 대신 설명만 남았을 때 무슨 일이 생기는지도 이미 계측돼 있다. 옥스퍼드 인터넷연구소 연구에서 개발사 안전 프레임워크의 중대 변경 383건 가운데 257건은 개발사 자신의 공지만 읽어서는 무엇이 어떻게 바뀌었는지 복원되지 않았다. 문서가 전부 공개돼 있는데도 그랬다.

페블러스도 밖에서 남의 데이터를 진단하는 일을 한다. 열어 보면 현재 상태는 있는데 그 상태에 이르는 경로가 없는 경우가 잦고, 그럴 때 내놓을 수 있는 것은 판정이 아니라 관찰이다. 심판을 세우는 일과 심판이 볼 장부를 갖추는 일은 별개다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

OpenAI, Anthropic and Google have been meeting since July to design a body that would test each other's models before release. Read all five published design documents in full and the same slot is empty in every one. They say inspectors would audit a developer's practices, from data selection through post-deployment monitoring. None says what the developer must keep as evidence of those practices, in what form, or for how long.

The original worked the other way around. Finance built the ledger before it built the referee. Wiring the Consolidated Audit Trail across the industry took roughly a decade, and its annual budget grew to nearly four and a half times the first estimate. The ledger was the expensive, contested layer.

The claim needs bounding to stay true. The idea of a record-keeping duty is not missing from AI. Annex XI of the EU AI Act already requires the provenance and curation of training data, evaluation protocols and results, and red-teaming records, and these same firms comply with it in Europe. What omits it is the blueprint they are drawing at home. What happens when explanation stands in for records has also been measured: in an Oxford Internet Institute study, 257 of 383 material changes to developers' own safety frameworks could not be reconstructed from the developers' own announcements. Every document was public.

Pebblous audits other people's data from the outside. Often the current state is there and the path that produced it is not, and at that point what you can issue is an observation rather than a verdict. Appointing a referee and assembling the ledger the referee reads are two different projects.

## Facebook (EN)

When we open someone else's dataset for the first time, the thing that stops us is never the yardstick. It is the material.

The current state of the data is there. The path that produced it is not.

Which records arrived when, what was dropped, which labels were reapplied. Without that, what we can hand back is an observation, not a verdict.

Reading that OpenAI, Anthropic and Google have been meeting since July to design a body that would test each other's models before release, that scene is what came to mind first.

"Say the referee is settled. Where is the thing the referee reads?"

The proposal is modeled on FINRA. But in finance the ledger came before the referee. Wiring a record layer that can replay a trade across the whole industry took about a decade, and it was the most expensive and most contested part of the build. This spring, four years after it finally went live, US securities regulators approved deleting anything older than three years. That layer is still unsettled.

We read all five published design documents in full and asked them the same question. Not one says what a developer must keep, in what form, or for how long. In Europe, these same firms already comply with a list the law spells out. That is why we followed this story to the end. Before deciding who the referee is, someone has to decide what the referee gets to look at.

The body has no name yet and no charter. Something may be getting decided ahead of the name: how much anyone will be able to see later.
