## LinkedIn (KO)

로봇 성공률이 40.8%에서 69.0%로 올랐다는 실험에서, 정작 연습한 것과 같은 조건에서 잰 성적은 7.5%포인트밖에 오르지 않았다.

2026년 8월 6일 arXiv에 올라온 논문이다. 월드모델이 만든 합성 궤적을 로봇 정책 학습에 두 단계로 넣고, 네 가지 작업을 다섯 조건에서 구성마다 수백 회씩 실물 로봇으로 시험했다. 본문에 실린 것은 평균 세 개이고, 조건별 스무 칸은 부록에 있다.

그 스무 칸을 펴면 오른 자리가 갈린다. 물체 배치를 무작위화한 궤적을 넣자 배치가 달라진 조건이 25.0%포인트 뛰는 동안 방해 물체와 조명은 각각 5.0%포인트에 머물렀다. 그다음에 환경을 다양화한 궤적을 넣자 이번에는 방해 물체 쪽이 33.7%포인트 움직였다. 넣은 데이터가 오르는 칸을 정한다는 뜻이고, 합성데이터의 효과는 데이터셋 크기의 함수가 아니라 구성의 함수라는 뜻이다.

그러므로 성공률이 올랐다는 문장과 실력이 올랐다는 문장은 같은 뜻이 아니다. 오른 것은 작업 자체를 더 잘하게 된 쪽이 아니라 조건이 달라져도 버티는 쪽이다. 조명 조건은 두 번 보강한 뒤에도 52.5%로 세 구성 모두에서 가장 낮은 칸에 남았다.

한계도 같은 표에 있다. 같은 수의 실제 시연을 추가한 대조군이 이 실험에는 없고, 데이터의 양과 다양성이 함께 늘었으므로 어느 쪽이 성적을 올렸는지도 가릴 수 없다. 합성 궤적을 넣으면 겨눈 조건이 오른다는 것까지가 이 표에서 끌어낼 수 있는 결론이며, 같은 비용의 실제 수집보다 낫다는 뜻은 아니다.

국내에서는 2026년 6월에 착수한 2년 340억원 규모의 피지컬 AI 사업이 월드모델 미적용 대비 20%포인트 향상을 핵심 목표로 걸었다. 조건을 고정하지 않은 향상폭은 채점할 수 없다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

A robot study reports success climbing from 40.8% to 69.0%. On the conditions the robot had actually trained on, the score moved 7.5 points.

The paper went up on arXiv on 6 August 2026. World-model-generated trajectories were added to a robot policy's training set in two rounds. Four tasks were then run on real hardware across five evaluation conditions, hundreds of trials per data regime. Three averages made it into the body. The twenty per-condition cells sit in the appendix.

Open those twenty cells and the gains separate. Trajectories built by randomising object placement lifted the repositioned-object condition by 25.0 points. The added-distractor and changed-lighting conditions each moved 5.0. The second round diversified the environment, and this time the distractor condition moved 33.7. What goes in decides which cell rises. The effect of synthetic data is a function of composition, not size.

So a rise in success rate and a rise in competence are not the same sentence. What the data bought was tolerance to shifted conditions rather than skill at the task itself. Lighting stayed the weakest cell in all three regimes, ending at 52.5%.

The limits sit in the same table. No arm added the same number of real demonstrations. Volume and diversity also rose together, so the table cannot separate which of the two did the work. What it supports is that targeted synthetic trajectories lift the condition they target. It does not show that they beat real collection at equal cost.

South Korea launched a two-year, 34 billion won Physical AI programme in June 2026. Its headline target is a 20-point gain in real robot success over a no-world-model baseline. A gain reported without its condition cannot be scored.

## Facebook (EN)

In the same kitchen, the shadow under a cup falls in one place under morning light and somewhere else under a ceiling lamp.

A person reaches for the cup without registering the difference.

In a paper posted in August 2026, the condition a robot never quite climbed out of was that one. Changed lighting.

By most readings the experiment went well. Synthetic trajectories from a world model went into the policy's training data, and overall success rose from 40.8% to 69.0%.

Underneath that average the authors had measured a great deal more. Four tasks, five evaluation conditions, hundreds of trials per data regime. The twenty-cell table went into the appendix. Three averages went into the body.

Open the twenty cells and the picture changes.

When the added trajectories came from scattering objects into new positions, one condition rose sharply. The one with repositioned objects. Nearby distractors and changed lighting stayed roughly where they were. The next round diversified the environment, and this time the distractor condition climbed.

What goes in decides which cell rises.

And the score measured under the very conditions the robot had practised on barely moved across both rounds.

What the synthetic data bought was not "skill" but "tolerance."

Lighting was still sitting near half after two rounds of augmentation. Visual diversity went in and lighting did not follow. That, on its own, is a diagnosis. The variation that went in may not have contained the lighting the real site has.

"When a success rate goes up, which cell went up?"

That question carries weight in Korea right now. A two-year national programme began in June 2026, and its headline target is a 20-point gain in robot success over a no-world-model baseline. A gain with no condition written next to it leaves nobody able to mark it.

At Pebblous, for the same reason, the first thing we go looking for in a dataset is not the average but the table underneath it. Which cell is empty has to be known before anything can be built to fill it.

The twenty cells were already measured. They simply did not make the headline. If enough readers get used to opening the appendix, the next paper may print that table in the body.
