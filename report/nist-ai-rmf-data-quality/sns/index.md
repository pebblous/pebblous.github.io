## LinkedIn (KO)

실패한 AI 프로젝트의 85%가 원인으로 데이터 품질을 지목합니다. 그런데 AI를 지탱할 만큼 품질이 충분한 데이터를 가진 조직은 12%뿐입니다.

NIST AI RMF 1.0은 흔히 규제 준수 체크리스트로 읽힙니다. 하지만 네 함수(GOVERN·MAP·MEASURE·MANAGE) 가운데 실제 엔지니어링 작업이 몰리는 MAP과 MEASURE를 실행 단계로 내려보면, 거의 전부가 데이터 계보·표현성·드리프트 문제로 바뀝니다. "AI 리스크를 관리하라"는 규제 언어는 파이프라인 위에서 "학습 데이터를 감사하고 추적하고 모니터링하라"가 됩니다.

문제는 시차입니다. EU AI Act의 고위험 시스템 시한은 2027년 12월로 연기됐지만, 투명성·워터마킹 의무는 2026년 안에 그대로 발효됩니다. 규제를 데이터 품질 문제로 번역해 둔 팀만이 이 유예를 준비 시간으로 씁니다.

페블러스가 이 주제를 다루는 이유도 여기 있습니다. MAP의 데이터 갭 평가는 계보·커버리지 진단과, MEASURE의 편향·드리프트 추적은 데이터 품질 메트릭 모니터링과 거의 겹칩니다. 페블러스는 DataClinic으로 AI 학습 데이터의 품질을 정량 진단합니다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

85% of failed AI projects point to data quality as the cause. Yet only 12% of organizations hold data good enough to sustain the AI they want to build.

NIST AI RMF 1.0 reads like a compliance checklist. But of its four functions (GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE), the two where the engineering actually concentrates, MAP and MEASURE, resolve almost entirely into questions of data lineage, representativeness, and drift once they hit the implementation layer. The regulatory instruction to "manage AI risk" becomes, on the pipeline, "audit, trace, and monitor your training data."

The timing matters. The EU AI Act's high-risk deadline slipped to December 2027, but its transparency and watermarking obligations still take effect within 2026. Teams that have already translated the framework into data-quality work are the ones treating the delay as preparation time.

That translation is why Pebblous covers this. MAP's data-gap assessment overlaps with lineage and coverage diagnostics; MEASURE's bias and drift tracking overlaps with data-quality metric monitoring. Pebblous runs DataClinic to quantify the quality of AI training data.

## Facebook (EN)

Picture, for a moment, the desk of a data engineer who has just been handed a compliance document.

"Manage your AI risk." The sentence is clear enough. What it doesn't say is which line of the pipeline to change tomorrow morning.

That gap is where we lingered while re-reading NIST AI RMF 1.0. Of its four functions, GOVERN and MANAGE speak the language of organizations and procedure. But follow the two where the real work piles up, MAP and MEASURE, down to the implementation layer, and they turn out to be almost entirely about looking closely at data: tracing where the training set came from, noticing which groups were left out, watching for the drift that creeps in quietly after deployment.

Eight in ten failed AI projects name data quality as the cause, and yet only about one in ten organizations hold data good enough to carry the AI they hope to build. That distance between the regulation and the reality is what makes "manage it" stall on the desk.

Which leaves a question worth sitting with: "How do we translate an abstract line of regulation into an actual piece of data work?"

Pebblous took up this subject for the same reason. MAP's data-gap assessment comes down to lineage and coverage diagnostics; MEASURE's bias and drift tracking comes down to monitoring data-quality metrics. It is where a company that has spent its time diagnosing data arrives when it opens a regulatory document.
