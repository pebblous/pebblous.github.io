## LinkedIn (KO)

유럽연합 인공지능법을 지원하는 조화표준 가운데 EU 관보에 인용된 것은 2026년 8월 현재 한 건도 없다.

올해 처음 발행에 도달한 유럽표준 EN 18286:2026이 다루는 것은 데이터가 아니라 품질경영시스템이다. 데이터 조항에 대응하는 표준은 아직 초안 단계에 머물러 있다. 발행과 관보 인용은 서로 다른 칸이고, 적합성 추정은 마지막 칸에서만 생긴다.

국내 성적서가 유럽 증서로 바뀌지 않는 이유는 시험 능력이 아니다. 인정은 시험기관이 그 시험을 할 수 있는지 확인하는 제도다. 피통보기관 지정은 회원국 정부가 자국 영토 안의 기관을 상대로 하는 별개의 행위다. 인공지능법 제39조는 협정을 앞에 두고 역량을 그다음에 본다. 유럽연합이 적합성평가 상호인정협정을 맺은 제3국 일곱 나라에 한국은 없다.

그래서 국내 공인 성적서는 통행증이 아니라 기술문서에 넣어 제출하는 증거다. 로봇 쪽은 문이 더 좁다. 기계학습이 안전기능을 맡는 부품은 기계류 규정 부속서 I 파트 A에 걸린다. 조화표준을 온전히 따르더라도 자기선언이 불가능하다.

그리고 로봇 기업이 먼저 만나는 날짜는 2028년 8월이 아니라 기계류 규정이 적용되는 2027년 1월 20일이다. 제10조가 부르는 대표성 기록과 데이터 공백 로그는 데이터를 모으는 동안 함께 쌓여야 존재한다. 남은 기간은 면제 기간이 아니라 적립 기간이다.

페블러스가 서 있는 칸은 인정도 지정도 아닌 그 앞, 데이터 품질을 재고 그 판정을 문서로 남기는 계층이다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Not one harmonised standard supporting the EU AI Act has been cited in the Official Journal.

The first European standard to reach publication under the Act arrived this year, and it covers quality management systems rather than data. The standard that answers the Act's data clause is still a draft. Publication and citation are separate steps, and the presumption of conformity attaches only at the last one.

The reason a Korean test report does not convert into a European certificate is not laboratory capacity. Accreditation confirms that a lab can run the test. Designating a notified body is something a Member State government does, over bodies on its own territory. Article 39 of the AI Act puts the agreement first and the competence second, and Korea is not among the seven third countries holding a conformity assessment agreement with the EU.

So a Korean report crosses the border as evidence inside the technical file, not as a passport. For robots the opening is narrower. Once machine learning performs a safety function, the Machinery Regulation places the product in Annex I Part A. There, full compliance with harmonised standards still does not permit self declaration.

The first date on a robot maker's calendar is not August 2028 either. It is 20 January 2027, when the Machinery Regulation starts to apply. The records Article 10 asks for accumulate while the data is being collected, which is what makes the remaining months a ledger rather than an exemption.

Pebblous sits one square earlier in that chain, where dataset quality is measured and the verdict is written down.

## Facebook (EN)

"We will issue the evidence."

I have read that line in more than one proposal. I have written something close to it myself.

The robot training centers Korea plans to build stand in the same place. What the science ministry actually wrote in July was a system for validating data and setting interoperability standards so that quality can be managed. Which means verification records will pile up beside the datasets.

So what happens when a Korean accredited test report is attached to those records and carried into Europe?

"Is it a passport, or is it evidence?"

Reading the clauses, the answer turned out to be the second one.

Accreditation confirms that a laboratory can run a test. Designating a notified body is a different act, performed by a Member State government over bodies on its own soil. Article 39 of the AI Act puts the agreement before the competence, and Korea is not among the seven third countries that hold such an agreement with the EU.

And no harmonised standard capable of granting a presumption of conformity has been cited in the Official Journal yet.

I would rather this not read as a closed road.

What Article 10 asks for is a record of how representativeness was checked, how label error rates moved, what conditions were missing from the data and when they were filled. Those things only exist if they accumulate while the data is being collected. Nobody can sit down in the summer of 2028 and reconstruct two years of collection history.

For a company building robots, even the first date on the calendar is different. Not August 2028, but 20 January 2027, when the Machinery Regulation begins to apply.

The square Pebblous occupies is neither accreditation nor designation. It is the one before them, where dataset quality is actually measured and the verdict is written down.

Whether the remaining time is called a delay or a ledger seems to decide what gets recorded now.
