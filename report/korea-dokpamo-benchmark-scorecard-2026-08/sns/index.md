## LinkedIn (KO)

독파모 2차 평가에서 탈락한 팀은, 100점 중 75점을 매긴 시점까지 2위였다.

8월 27일 팀별 부문 점수가 전부 공개되면서 그 채점표를 되돌려 볼 수 있게 됐다. 벤치마크와 전문가 평가를 합친 75점 구간에서 네 팀은 1.7점 안에 붙어 있었다. 순서가 바뀐 것은 마지막 사용자 평가 구간이다. 한 부문씩 덜어 내고 다시 세워 보면, 어느 하나를 빼도 최하위는 그대로인데 사용자 평가를 통째로 걷어내야 순서가 달라진다.

채점 오류가 아니라 가중합 점수표의 성질이다. 순위를 만드는 힘은 배점이 아니라 응시자들이 그 항목에서 얼마나 흩어졌는가에서 나온다. 부문별 표준편차로 재면, 배점이 가장 큰 전문가 평가 35점이 순위 차이의 20%만 만들었다. 합성지표 연구가 대학 순위와 인간개발지수에서 오래전에 측정해 둔 어긋남이다.

같은 표 안의 두 벤치마크도 서로 다른 자를 쓰고 있었다. AAII 25점은 환산식 때문에 세계 1위 모델을 넣어도 상한이 15.8점이다. 반대쪽 NIA는 네 팀 전부를 만점의 85% 언저리에 묶어 놓았다. 어느 부문에도 신뢰구간은 붙어 있지 않다.

세계 3위 주장 쪽은 리더보드 610건을 발표 시점으로 되돌려 확인했더니 그대로 재현됐다. 다만 여드레 뒤 싱가포르 모델 하나가 같은 구간에 들어왔다. 순위를 성과로 인용하려면 조회 시점을 함께 적어야 한다는 뜻이다.

채점표도 다섯 지표를 가중합해 만든 파생 데이터셋이다. 학습 데이터에서 중복과 포화와 결측을 재는 절차가 여기에도 그대로 적용된다. 이번에 그 확인이 가능해진 것은 발표 아흐레 뒤였다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

The team eliminated from Korea's sovereign AI model programme was in second place through 75 of the evaluation's 100 points.

The full per-team block scores were released on 27 August, nine days after the verdict, and only then could the scorecard be rebuilt. Across the benchmark and expert blocks the four teams sat within 1.7 points of one another. The order rearranged only in the final user-evaluation stretch. Drop any single block and the last-placed team stays last; lift out the whole user evaluation and the ordering changes.

This is not a scoring error but a property of weighted sums. What makes a ranking is not the weight attached to an item, but how far apart the candidates fall on it. Measured by the spread between teams, the expert block, the largest allocation on the sheet at 35 points, produced a fifth of the separation. The composite-indicator literature measured the same mismatch in university league tables and the Human Development Index years ago.

The two benchmarks inside the same sheet were using different rulers. The AAII block converts a raw index score at a fixed rate, so the world's top-scoring model would still cap out at 15.8 of its 25 points. The NIA block did the opposite and packed all four teams around 85% of full marks. Neither carries a confidence interval, and nor does any other block.

The government's world-ranking claims hold up. We rebuilt the leaderboard, 610 models with vendor, country and release date, and rewound it to the announcement; both verifiable claims reproduce. Eight days later a Singaporean model had entered the same band. Citing a ranking as an achievement means citing when it was read.

A scorecard is a derived dataset built by weighting five indicators. Duplication, saturation, scale mismatch and missing values are what we already measure in training data. Here that check only became possible nine days after the result.

## Facebook (EN)

Two sentences sat side by side in the news on 18 August.

Korea now ranks third in the world, behind the United States and China.

And the team behind the model that scored highest in the evidence for that ranking would not advance to the next stage.

Both came out of the same government briefing. Neither could be checked that day. What was released was a set of block averages and the gap between first and fourth. The per-team scores arrived nine days later, on 27 August.

Once they did, I rebuilt the scorecard from the bottom up.

Through the benchmark and expert blocks, 75 of the 100 points, the four teams sat within 1.7 points of each other. At that stage the team that was eliminated was in second place. The order rearranged in the final 25 points, the user evaluation.

What stayed with me was not the question of who should have won.

It was that a block marked 35 points on the sheet produced a fifth of the separation between the teams, and a block marked 25 produced more than 40% of it.

Call it the effective weight. Not the number written on the scorecard, but the one made by how far apart the candidates actually fell. People who study composite indicators measured this same mismatch years ago, in university league tables and in the Human Development Index.

"Is this ranking made by the weights, or by the spread?"

The work we do in front of training data is the same work. Stripping out duplicates, checking which indicator has saturated against its ceiling and stopped carrying information, keeping a ledger of why each call was made. A 100-point scorecard is a derived dataset built from five indicators, and it answers to the same questions.

None of this says the government overstated anything. We rewound the leaderboard to the announcement date, and both of its verifiable ranking claims reproduce exactly. Eight days later a Singaporean model had entered the same band. The ranking had an expiry date.

What is left, I think, is a question of timing.

This time nine days sat between the announcement and the check. Next time I hope there is nothing in between.
