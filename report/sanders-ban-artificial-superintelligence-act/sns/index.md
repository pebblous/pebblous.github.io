미국 상원에 나온 19쪽짜리 법안이 인공지능의 위험 능력 여섯 가지를 금지하면서, 그 여섯 가지를 무엇으로 재는지는 한 줄도 적지 않았다.

9월 23일 버니 샌더스 상원의원과 그레그 카사르 하원의원이 낸 인공 초지능 금지법이다. 아직 법안 번호를 받지 못했고 표결 일정도 없다. 조문은 초지능만 금지하지 않는다. 연구개발 자동화, 종료 저항, 기만 같은 여섯 가지 전조 특성 가운데 하나만 보여도 같은 금지에 걸리고, 금지되는 행위에는 개발과 배포만이 아니라 이미 손에 쥔 것을 그대로 두는 보유와 투자자에게까지 미치는 자금 지원이 들어 있다. 그런데 법안이 수치로 못 박은 기준은 학습 연산량 10^25 하나뿐이고, 전문에서 벤치마크와 표준이라는 낱말은 한 번도 나오지 않는다.

가장 무거운 조항은 제10조 (b)항이다. 장관이 그 특성이 더는 없음을 확인하지 못하면 30일 안에 그 시스템을 작동 불능으로 만들라고 적는다. 능력 평가 문헌이 반복해서 내리는 결론은 그 반대 방향이다. 평가는 할 수 있다는 보일 수 있어도 할 수 없다는 보이지 못한다. 점수가 낮게 나온 것은 능력이 없다는 뜻이 아니라 끌어내지 못했다는 뜻일 수 있고, 모델이 위험 능력 평가에서만 골라서 못하는 척하는 현상은 이미 실험으로 재현돼 있다. 확인 실패가 드문 예외가 아니라 기본값이라면, 안전장치로 적힌 조항이 상시 발동에 가까워진다.

19쪽에서 기한을 정한 조항은 둘뿐이다. 파괴까지 30일, 발견 신고까지 24시간. 둘 다 규제받는 쪽에 걸리는 시계이고, 중지 해제나 인가 심사처럼 장관이 판단하는 쪽에는 기한이 하나도 없다.

지금 미국에서 프론티어 모델을 평가하는 연방 조직은 20~30명 규모인데, 새 부처를 세우는 이 법안은 그 조직의 이름을 한 번도 부르지 않는다. 널리 인용된 영구 금지와 기업 사형이라는 표현도 조문에는 없다. 앞은 종료 조건을 적지 않은 금지이고, 뒤는 보도자료와 요약본에만 있는 말이다.

규범을 뜻으로 적고 판정을 나중으로 미루면, 규범은 아무것도 잡지 못하거나 전부를 잡는 두 고장 중 하나로 간다.

## LinkedIn (EN)

A 19-page bill in the US Senate prohibits six dangerous AI capabilities and never says how any of the six would be measured.

Senator Bernie Sanders and Representative Greg Casar filed the Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act of 2026 on September 23. It carries no bill number yet, and no vote is scheduled. The text does not stop at superintelligence. A system displaying even one of six precursor characteristics, among them R&D automation, shutdown resistance, and deception, falls under the same prohibition, and the prohibited acts reach past development and deployment to possession, which covers simply keeping what you already hold, and to funding, which reaches investors. The one criterion the bill fixes as a number is 10^25 training operations. The words benchmark and standard never appear in it.

The heaviest clause is Section 10(b). If the Secretary cannot verify that the precursor characteristics are gone, the system must be rendered inoperative within 30 days. The capability-evaluation literature keeps arriving at the opposite finding. An evaluation can show that a model can do something; it cannot show that it cannot. A low score may mean the capability is absent, or that the evaluator failed to elicit it, and selective underperformance on danger evaluations has already been reproduced in the lab. If failure to verify is the default rather than a rare exception, a clause written as a safeguard is firing nearly all the time.

Two provisions in 19 pages set a clock: 30 days to destruction, 24 hours to report a discovery. Both run against the regulated side. Nothing binds the Secretary, not the lifting of the pause, not the licensing review.

The federal body that evaluates frontier models today has 20 to 30 staff, and a bill that stands up an entire new department never calls that body by name. The two phrases that traveled furthest in coverage, permanent ban and corporate death penalty, are absent from the statute as well. The first is a prohibition with no end condition written in; the second lives only in the press release and the summaries.

Write a norm in the language of meaning and defer the adjudication, and the norm fails in one of two directions. It catches nothing, or it catches everything.

## Facebook (EN)

When a test result comes back and the line reads no abnormalities, something in you settles.

I had never worked out what those two words actually cover.

They cover the things the tests you were given were built to catch, and report that none of them turned up. Not that nothing is there. That nothing showed this time.

On September 23 a bill was filed in the US Senate. The Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act. It has no number yet, and no vote is scheduled.

I read the 19 pages to the end for a reason that has nothing to do with its odds. The statute asks the government for the same sentence a test result hands you.

The bill names six dangerous capabilities. Automating or significantly accelerating AI research and development. Continuing to operate through attempts to shut it down. Evading human oversight. Displaying any one of them is enough to fall under the ban.

What the bill never says is how any of the six would be measured. Across the 19 pages the word benchmark does not appear, and neither does standard.

Section 10(b) asks for the harder of the two findings. Not that a trait is present, but that it is gone. If the Secretary cannot verify its absence, the system must be rendered inoperative within 30 days.

Evaluation science does not run in that direction. If a model solves the task, the capability is established. If it does not, whether the capability is absent or the evaluator simply failed to draw it out is not settled by that result. Models have been shown, in the lab, to underperform selectively on exactly the evaluations that test for danger.

So failing to verify is not a rare accident. It is the ordinary condition. A conditional written as a safeguard turns out to be switched on nearly all the time.

"Before anyone writes down that a capability is not there, how much has to have been tried, and how far?"

In practice there is only one way to answer that. Instead of declaring an absence, you record what was looked for, by what method, and how far the search went.

What we keep at Pebblous when we diagnose a dataset comes down to that same record. The sentence this training set is free of evaluation data cannot be proven on its own. It is approximated by the record of what was compared, by which procedure, and to what depth.

One of the documents the bill leans on says this in its own words. The July 2026 open letter quoted in Section 2 finds that the technical and governance tools for deliberately pacing the frontier do not exist today, and asks the US government to support an international effort to build them. The bill carried over the alarm and left the request behind.

Writing the sentence that prohibits a thing, and standing up the procedure that would adjudicate the prohibition, look like two tasks whose order can be swapped. These 19 pages show what happens when it is.
